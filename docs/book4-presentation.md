# Book 4, Thousand-Li Ride (ch. 20–28): presentation plan

This plan comes from Plot. The beats and facts are History's (`docs/book4-beat-sheet.md`, with line numbers). This file decides how a player who has never read the novel meets each beat:
- what is set up first
- where the player is
- what plays as a transition still
- what the objective and hint say
- which mechanic carries it

It has no dialogue. The writer takes the novel's lines from the beat sheet.

Rules, the same as the clarity pass:
1. Name a person, and say why they matter, before they act.
2. Explain each event before or as it happens.
3. Each scene's first line follows from where the last one ended.
4. A still comes after the line that explains it, never before.
5. The objective always says what to do next and where. A "not yet" line names what comes first.

## Shape

- **Spine:** faith kept at a cost.
- **Party:**
  - Part 1, Xuchang and Xuzhou, scenes 1–7: Liu Bei, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei.
  - Part 2, scenes 8–15: the player walks as **Guan Yu alone**, with Liu Bei's two wives in his care. This is the spine made playable. Liu Bei and Zhang Fei are absent, as in the novel.
  - The boss and the last scene: Zhang Fei and then Liu Bei come back.
- **Titles:**
  - Cao Cao is 丞相 throughout.
  - Liu Bei is 左将军, called 刘皇叔 from scene 1.
  - Guan Yu is 偏将军 until Baima, then 汉寿亭侯.
- **Opening scroll** (Chapter 20): it picks up from Book 3's closing, which now ends on the road back to Xu with Che Zhou left in Xuzhou. It covers the Emperor held at Xu, and Liu Bei lodged next to Cao Cao's residence.
