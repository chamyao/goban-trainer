# Book 4 "Thousand-Li Ride": beat sheet from the novel (chapters 20-28)

Source: Chinese text of Romance of the Three Kingdoms, Gutenberg #23950; bracketed numbers are line numbers in that file. Drafted by research helpers reading the chapters (Part A: ch.20-24, Part B: ch.25-28). The historian spot-checked these against the text: Liu Bei's title 左將軍宜城亭侯 and the name 劉皇叔 at the audience (3601-3602); Cao Cao is addressed 丞相, and 司空 only in Chen Lin's proclamation (3953); the edict dated 建安四年春三月 (3690); "five passes, six generals" (4891); Guan Yu made 漢壽亭侯 only after Yan Liang (4598); Cao Cao's 吾為漢相 (4446); Cai Yang's death at Gucheng (5028).

## Read this first

- **Zhang Liao's rescue is the first beat of ch.20 (3579-3582)** and Book 3 already ends with it. Book 4 can start at Xuzhou's people asking for Liu Bei (3585).
- **What Book 3 must set up for Book 4:** Zhang Liao's rescue (Liu Bei seizes Cao Cao's arm and Guan Yu kneels to vouch for him), because in ch.25 Zhang Liao reminds Guan Yu of this favour (昔日蒙兄救弟, 4420), and Guan Yu's loyalty is a debt that cuts both ways. Also the Emperor's weakness and Cao Cao's hold on him (ch.13-14 rescue of the Emperor, Dong Cheng's merit), and Liu Bei's lineage (the genealogy is the payoff of ch.1).
- **Offstage:** Liu Bei is absent from ch.27 and does not meet Guan Yu until Guan Ding's farm (5083). Zhang Fei is absent until Gucheng (5006). Ch.23 is a side chapter (the brothers are offstage). Ch.24 follows Liu Bei's flight to Yuan Shao.
- **No calendar dates** in ch.25-28 (the year 建安四年 first appears at 5126 in ch.29); ch.20-22 give a few (see Part A's standing facts).
- **Ambiguities flagged by the readers:** Chen Deng serves both sides in the Che Zhou plot; Yuan Shao's camp is at Liyang in ch.22 but Guandu in ch.24; how Cao Cao learns Liu Bei was in the plot isn't stated; the hunt is undated; Liu Bei is at Runan (4903) and later back at Yuan Shao's (5047), as the novel has it.

# Part A: chapters 20-24

# Book 4 "Thousand-Li Ride" - Beat sheet, chapters 20-24

Source: sanguo.txt (Gutenberg #23950), line numbers in brackets. Ch.20 = 3577-3733, ch.21 = 3734-3903, ch.22 = 3904-4097, ch.23 = 4098-4293, ch.24 = 4294-4398 (ch.25 title at 4399, not covered here).
Quotes are copied exactly from the source (traditional). "LB / GY / ZF" = Liu Bei (玄德), Guan Yu (雲長/關公), Zhang Fei (翼德).

## Standing facts (check before writing any scene)

- Cao Cao is addressed as **丞相** throughout these chapters by everyone (LB at 3630, 3744, 4079; Dong Cheng at 3678). Only Chen Lin's proclamation calls him **司空** (3953): that is the enemy's slur/legal title, not how he is addressed. Do not use 司空 in dialogue. Cao Cao's own men also call him 明公 (3612, 4296, 4326).
- Liu Bei's titles in this span:
  - Before the audience: 劉使君 (Xuzhou people, Cao Cao, 3587). Earlier 徐州牧 (3917) and 豫州牧 (Ma Teng, 3736, still uses the old title). 使君 is the standard polite address from Cao Cao (3766, 3789) and others.
  - After the audience (3601): **左將軍、宜城亭侯**; everybody then calls him **劉皇叔** (3602). He signs the oath as 「左將軍劉備」 (3756). Does not become 豫州牧/徐州牧 again by court appointment in the text: Cao Cao puts 車冑 in as 權領徐州 (3587) and 刺史 (3850); LB just takes the city by force of the army (see ch.21 ambiguity below).
  - Cao Cao tells LB "請使君" etc. Never "將軍" from Cao Cao in these chapters except Dong Cheng (3744, 將軍) and Yuan Shao (4386 LB says 將軍 to Shao).
- Offices: Zhang Liao made 中郎將 and 關內侯 (3582). Zang Ba made 瑯琊相 (3585). Che Zhou = 車騎將軍/刺史 (3587, 3850). Dong Cheng = 車騎將軍, 國舅 (3648). Jia Xu made 執金吾使, Zhang Xiu 揚武將軍 (4118). Han Song 侍中, 零陵太守 (4189).
- Dates in text: 建安四年春三月 (secret edict, 3690); 建安四年六月 (Yuan Shu dies, 3865); autumn: Cao Cao-Yuan Shao standoff 八月至十月 (4026); 初冬 snow when GY takes Wang Zhong (4044); 隆冬 (4101); 建安五年元旦, 元宵 (4207-4211); Ji Ping dies 建安五年正月 (4280). Ch.24 itself gives no month; it follows directly (Ch.24 events = early 200 AD, i.e. after 正月 in text). The novel puts the edict in 建安四年 but the plot starts in 建安四年 spring through 五年 early spring; do not date the hunt (see ch.20 note).
- Geography (as the novel has it): Xuzhou (city, LB's base) / Xiapi 下邳 (GY holds it with the two ladies, 4091) / Xiaopei 小沛 (LB and ZF, 4092). Xuchang 許都 / 許昌 (Han capital, Cao Cao's seat) with 許田 (hunting grounds outside, 3619). Liyang 黎陽 (Yuan/Cao stand-off, 4023). Guandu 官渡 (Cao Ren's camp at 4028; in ch.24 4325 Cheng Yu says Yuan Shao himself is at Guandu: the novel is loose here). Qingzhou 青州 (Yuan Tan's seat, 4381), Pingyuan 平原, Ji/Ye 冀州/鄴 (Yuan Shao, 4384-4388). Mangdang Mountain 芒碭山 (where ZF flees, 4372).
- Who is where at the start of the span: Xuzhou was taken by Cao Cao's army after Lü Bu's fall (end ch.19); LB, GY, ZF go to Xuchang with Cao Cao (3588) and LB is lodged in a house next to the Chancellor's residence. Mi Zhu, Sun Qian, Jian Yong and Mi Fang are in Xuzhou later (3851, 4092).

## Chapter 20: 曹阿瞞許田打圍, 董國舅內閣受詔 (3577-3733)

**Emotional turn: relief and honour (a kinsman of the Emperor) turn to humiliation at the deer hunt, then to a secret vow.**

### 1. BEATS

1. **Zhang Liao spared** [3579-3582]. Place: Cao Cao's camp after Lü Bu's death (Xiapi). Present: Cao Cao, LB, GY, Zhang Liao (captured). Cao Cao raises a sword to kill Zhang Liao; LB seizes his arm, GY kneels to plead and offers his own life as guarantor. Cao Cao laughs it was a test and ennobles him.
   - Quote: LB: 「此等赤心之人，正當留用。」(3579). GY: 「關某素知文遠忠義之士，願以性命保之。」(3580).
   - This is the debt that will matter when Zhang Liao pleads with GY (ch.24 end, ch.25).
2. **Xuzhou people ask for LB; Cao Cao puts Che Zhou in** [3585-3588]. On the road back through Xuzhou, people block the road with incense and ask for 劉使君 as governor; Cao Cao says wait until he has seen the Emperor, and appoints Che Zhou to hold it temporarily. LB is lodged beside Cao Cao's residence in Xuchang (3588).
   - Quote: 「劉使君功大，且待面君封爵，回來未遲。」Cao Cao (3587).
3. **Audience with Emperor Xian: the genealogy** [3590-3602]. Palace, Xuchang. Present: Emperor Xian, LB, Cao Cao (brought him in), the 宗正卿 who reads the register. LB recites: descendant of Prince Jing of Zhongshan, grandson of Liu Xiong, son of Liu Hong. Register checked; the Emperor finds LB is his uncle, treats him in the side hall as uncle-nephew. Makes him 左將軍宜城亭侯. From now 劉皇叔.
   - Quote: LB: 「臣乃中山靖王之後，孝景皇帝閣下玄孫，劉雄之孫，劉弘之子也。」(3591-3592). Narration: 自此人皆稱為劉皇叔。(3602). The Emperor's private thought: 「今得此英雄之叔，朕有助矣！」(3600-3601).
4. **Cao Cao's reading of the "uncle"** [3604-3607] (Cao Cao's scene; LB absent, GY/ZF absent). Cao Cao: he is near the court but really in my grasp. Quote: 「名雖近君，實在吾掌握之內，吾何懼哉？」(3606).
5. **The hunt at Xutian: the arrow** [3615-3632]. Place: 許田, 10万 troops, a 200-li enclosure (3619). Present: Emperor, Cao Cao, LB with GY and ZF and some dozens of riders, wearing armour under their clothes (3618). LB shoots a hare and the Emperor cheers; the Emperor misses a stag three times; Cao Cao takes the Emperor's bow and golden arrow and kills it. Officers shout 萬歲 thinking the Emperor shot; Cao Cao rides forward to receive it. GY bristles, draws his blade; LB waves and signals him with his eyes, GY stops. LB praises the shot. Cao Cao keeps the bow.
   - Quote: GY narration: 剔起臥蠶眉，睜開丹鳳眼，提刀拍馬便出，要斬曹操。玄德見了，慌忙搖手送目。(3629-3630). LB: 「丞相神射，世所罕及！」(3630). Cao Cao: 「此天子洪福耳。」(3631).
6. **GY and LB argue afterwards** [3634-3638]. Place: back at the lodging, "眾人各自歸歇". Present: LB and GY (ZF not named in the exchange).
   - Quote: GY: 「操賊欺君罔上，我欲殺之，為國除害，兄何止我？」(3634-3635). LB: 「『投鼠忌器』。」(3635). GY: 「今日不殺此賊，後必為禍。」(3637). LB: 「且宜秘之，不可輕言。」(3637-3638).
7. **Emperor and Empress Fu; Fu Wan's plan** [3640-3651] (court thread, LB absent). Emperor weeps: 「背若芒刺」(3642). Fu Wan proposes Dong Cheng and the hidden edict in the jade belt.
8. **Belt edict handed to Dong Cheng; Cao Cao inspects the belt** [3653-3679] (court thread). Emperor writes with blood from a bitten finger; hall of meritorious ministers; Cao Cao makes Dong Cheng take off robe and belt, tries them on, hands them back.
9. **Dong Cheng finds the edict; the first conspirators sign** [3681-3702]: edict text 3687-3690 (dated 建安四年春三月). Wang Zifu, Zhong Ji, Wu Shuo, Wu Zilan sign the oath.
10. **Ma Teng joins; Ma Teng names LB** [3712-3732]. Ma Teng, governor of Xiliang, comes to take leave (入覲將還) and is angry at being turned away; shown the edict; blood oath. Ends on the cliffhanger: Ma Teng says to look at the clan register - 「何不共此人商議？」(3730). Ends with the poem (3731-3732). LB is the 7th, not yet named.

### 2. SET-UP each beat needs earlier
- The Han court is a captive of Cao Cao: explain 丞相 vs 天子; Emperor Xian has already survived Dong Zhuo and Li Jue/Guo Si (Emperor's own words 3640-3641). Emperor Xian's era name 建安 (3690).
- Zhang Liao: needs to be introduced as Lü Bu's general who just lost at Xiapi; LB/GY must show they respect him (GY's vouching).
- LB's lineage: the novel's opening (Yellow Turban poster, Han descent) must have been seeded. The genealogy is the payoff; show LB's weaving background earlier or Yuan Shu's insult in ch.21 (織席編屨小輩, 3859).
- GY's quickness to anger and sword: the "ZF" is present at the hunt (3618) but does not speak.
- Dong Cheng's "西都救駕之功" (3664, 3671): the rescue of the Emperor from Li Jue/Guo Si (ch.13-14) must be known. Fu Wan and Empress Fu are the Emperor's father-in-law/wife.
- Ma Teng is a Xiliang warlord who "入覲" (came to court) and is leaving; introduce him as a minor Han loyalist.

### 3. SIDE EPISODES / CUTS
- Side thread: the court conspiracy (Emperor, Fu Wan, Dong Cheng, Wang Zifu, Zhong Ji, Wu Shuo, Wu Zilan, Ma Teng). Seen from Dong Cheng's POV, can be a short playable thread with LB's party outside it until ch.21.
- Side thread: Cao Cao vs Yang Biao (3605-3607, Kong Rong objects 3609-3611) and Zhao Yan's execution (3611-3612). Brief; shows Cao Cao's menace.
- Safe to cut: the full genealogy list (3594-3598; keep the opening names only), Zhang Liao's rank paperwork and Zang Ba/Sun Guan/Wu Dun/Yin Li/Chang Xi list (3584-3586), Cheng Yu's "王霸之事" remark (3612) if the hunt is motivated otherwise, the Dong Cheng/Cao Cao belt-inspecting exchange can be shortened.

### 4. COMMON MISTAKES
- LB at the hunt is already 左將軍 and 皇叔; at that moment he does not hold the Xuzhou governorship (Che Zhou holds it). Ma Teng later calls him 豫州牧 (3736): that is Ma Teng's usage. Do not give him "Governor of Xuzhou" for ch.20-21 until ch.21's end (and even then the text does not re-appoint him).
- It is **GY** who wants to kill Cao Cao at the hunt, not ZF. ZF is present but silent. (Ma Teng and Dong Cheng both later cite GY: 3738, 3744.)
- LB addresses Cao Cao as 丞相 at the hunt (3630). The hunter's guarded line: Cao Cao rides a horse named 爪黃飛電 (3619), the Emperor rides 逍遙馬.
- Dates: the edict is dated 建安四年春三月 (3690); do not claim the hunt was in a different year. Ambiguity: the text never dates the hunt or the genealogy audience; the historical chronology is different, so follow the novel.
- The conspirators named by Dong Cheng's oath at this point: six (Dong Cheng, Wang Zifu, Zhong Ji, Wu Shuo, Wu Zilan, Ma Teng) = list at 3753-3755. LB makes seven in ch.21.
- Ma Teng ("西涼太守", 3712) is not the same as Ma Chao.
- Zhang Liao is spared by Cao Cao's own pre-planned test; do not make Cao Cao genuinely about to kill him: 「故戲之耳」(3580-3581).

## Chapter 21: 曹操煮酒論英雄, 關公賺城斬車冑 (3734-3903)

**Emotional turn: terror (the dropped chopsticks) turns to the release of escape, then to the bill: killing Che Zhou makes war certain.**

### 1. BEATS

1. **Ma Teng recommends LB; Dong Cheng visits LB at night** [3736-3757]. Place: LB's lodging (小閣). Present: LB, Dong Cheng, GY and ZF standing by (3743). Dong Cheng says he saw GY go for Cao Cao at the hunt. LB tests him first (denies, praises Cao Cao), then Dong Cheng shows the edict and oath list (6 names, 3753-3755); LB signs.
   - Quote: Dong Cheng: 「前日圍場之中，雲長欲殺曹操，將軍動目搖頭而退之，何也？」(3744-3745). Dong Cheng: 「朝廷臣子，若盡如雲長，何憂不太平哉！」(3748-3749). LB: 「公既奉詔討賊，備敢不犬馬之勞。」(3755). LB signs as 「左將軍劉備」(3756).
   - Ma Teng's reasoning that LB "非不欲圖操，恨操爪牙多" (3738-3739).
2. **LB plants vegetables** [3759-3761]. LB waters vegetables to hide his ambition. GY and ZF complain.
   - Quote: GY and ZF: 「兄不留心天下大事，而學小人之事，何也？」(3760). LB: 「此非二弟所知之。」(3760-3761).
   - Note: GY and ZF do not know about the edict plot at this point in the text? (Ambiguity: GY and ZF stood by during Dong Cheng's visit (3743), so they know about the plot; yet LB hides the *reason* of the gardening from them (3761) and explains the drop of the chopsticks to them afterwards (3805-3807). Handle as: they know the conspiracy, not LB's cover scheme.)
3. **Plum-wine meeting** [3763-3796]. Place: Cao Cao's garden/pavilion in the Chancellor's residence. Present: Cao Cao, LB, Xu Chu and Zhang Liao fetch LB (3763); GY and ZF are away shooting outside the city (3763, 3799). LB thinks "有甚緊事" and is scared pale when Cao Cao says 「在家做得好大事！」(3765). Cao Cao: "learning gardening is not easy". The plum story (3767-3769). Dragon talk; LB lists Yuan Shu, Yuan Shao, Liu Biao, Sun Ce, Liu Zhang, then Zhang Xiu/Zhang Lu/Han Sui; Cao Cao dismisses each. Then Cao Cao points to LB and himself. LB drops the chopsticks under the thunder.
   - Quote: Cao Cao: 「今天下英雄，惟使君與操耳。」(3789). LB: 「一震之威，乃至於此。」(3792). Cao Cao: 「丈夫亦畏雷乎？」(3792). LB: 「聖人迅雷風烈必變，安得不畏？」(3792-3793).
   - Also useful: 「夫英雄者，胸懷大志，腹有良謀；有包藏宇宙之機，吞吐天地之志者也。」Cao Cao (3788). Cao Cao on Yuan Shao: 「色厲膽薄，好謀無斷」(3782). On Yuan Shu: 「塚中枯骨」(3780).
4. **GY and ZF burst in** [3798-3803]. They return from archery outside the city and rush in with swords drawn. GY: they came to dance swords to amuse. Cao Cao jokes about Hongmen (Xiang Zhuang/Xiang Bo) and calls them "二樊噲".
   - Quote: GY: 「聽知丞相和兄飲酒，特來舞劍，以助一笑。」(3801). Cao Cao: 「此非鴻門會，安用項莊、項伯乎？」(3801-3802).
5. **LB explains to them** [3805-3807]. GY: 「險些驚殺我兩個！」(3805). LB explains the garden was to show he has no ambition; "吾之學圃，正欲使操知我無大志" (3806).
6. **News of Gongsun Zan; LB decides to flee** [3809-3825]. Next day, second drinking session with Cao Cao; Man Chong reports Gongsun Zan dead at Yijing (易京樓); Yuan Shu is leaving Huainan for Yuan Shao's land. LB is grieved (Gongsun Zan recommended him earlier) and worried about Zhao Yun (3822-3823). He asks to intercept Yuan Shu at Xuzhou. Cao Cao agrees.
   - Quote: LB: 「我不就此時尋個脫身之計，更待何時？」(3823, thought). LB: 「術若投紹，必從徐州過。備請一軍就半路截擊，術可擒矣。」(3823-3824).
7. **Departure with 50,000 troops** [3827-3832]. Emperor weeps seeing him off. Dong Cheng follows to the 十里長亭. LB takes the general's seal and 朱靈 and 路昭 as Cao Cao's watchers.
   - Quote: LB to Dong Cheng: 「某此行必有以報命。」(3829) Dong Cheng: 「公宜留意，勿負帝心。」(3829). To GY and ZF: 「吾乃籠中鳥，網中魚。此一行如魚入大海，鳥上青霽，不受籠網之羈絆也。」(3831).
8. **Cao Cao's regret; Xu Chu sent after LB** [3832-3846]. Cheng Yu and Guo Jia: 「放龍入海，縱虎歸山」(3834). Xu Chu catches up with 500 men; LB (with GY and ZF at arms on both sides) refuses to turn back.
   - Quote: LB: 「『將在外，君命有所不受。』吾面過君，又蒙丞相鈞語，今別無他議。」(3841-3842).
9. **Ma Teng goes home; LB reaches Xuzhou; battle with Yuan Shu's army** [3850-3862]. Che Zhou welcomes LB; Sun Qian and Mi Zhu appear; LB visits his family. ZF kills Ji Ling in under 10 rounds (3855-3856). Yuan Shu: 「織席編屨小輩，安敢輕我！」(3859). LB's line: 「吾今奉明詔前來討汝。」(3858).
10. **Yuan Shu's death** [3864-3865]. Nobody in LB's party present. Honey water - only blood water; dies vomiting blood in 建安四年六月. Jade seal ends up with Cao Cao via Xu Qiu (3871-3872).
11. **Che Zhou plot; Chen Deng warns GY and ZF** [3874-3884]. LB tells Zhu Ling and Lu Zhao to go back; Cao Cao is angry and Xun Yu advises using Che Zhou. Chen Deng, who is in on Che Zhou's plan, tells his father Chen Gui, who sends him to warn GY and ZF (LB is behind them, 3884).
12. **GY takes Xuzhou by night; Che Zhou killed** [3886-3897]. Place: Xuzhou city gate, moat bridge. Present: GY, ZF, soldiers wearing Cao Cao's colours, claiming to be Zhang Liao's troops; Che Zhou, Chen Deng on the wall.
    - Quote: GY: 「匹夫安敢懷詐，欲殺吾兄！」(3893). GY on the wall: 「反賊車冑，吾已殺之；眾等無罪，投降免死。」(3895-3896). The title of the chapter 「賺城」.
13. **LB's reaction; ZF slaughters Che Zhou's family; Chen Deng offers a plan** [3898-3902]. LB aghast: 「曹操若來，如之奈何？」(3898-3899). GY: 「弟與張飛迎之。」(3899). LB regrets it. ZF has killed Che Zhou's whole family: 「殺了曹操心腹之人，如何肯休？」(3900-3901).

### 2. SET-UP
- Yuan Shu: claimed the imperial title and the Jade Seal (earlier chapters); he is LB's immediate enemy. Ji Ling (紀靈) is his general from earlier chapters (ZF fought him).
- Gongsun Zan: LB's old friend and patron (the "薦己之恩", 3822); Zhao Yun was with him (3823) - needs a quick mention if the game shows Zhao Yun in book 4.
- Cao Cao's chess game: Zhu Ling / Lu Zhao as spies on LB.
- Che Zhou must be present from ch.20 (3587) so the player recognises him; Chen Deng and Chen Gui (Xuzhou local worthies, who already had served LB).
- The Mi Zhu, Sun Qian, Jian Yong, Mi Fang line-up should be seen at Xuzhou before the Che Zhou beat.
- The dragon talk: the player needs to know all of Yuan Shu/Yuan Shao/Liu Biao/Sun Ce/Liu Zhang so Cao Cao's dismissals land.
- "Hongmen" (Xiang Zhuang, Xiang Bo, Fan Kuai) for Cao Cao's joke (3801-3803).

### 3. SIDE EPISODES / CUTS
- Side thread: Gongsun Zan's fall (3812-3818: his refusal to rescue, the besieged tower, his killing of his family). Short flashback or report; Yuan Shu's final starvation is a short side scene.
- Side thread: Xu Chu's pursuit (3836-3846): worth a short standoff.
- Cut: the Man Chong report can be reduced to one line; Yuan Yin/Xu Qiu's killing and seal delivery (3871-3872); the list of Yuan Shu's defectors (3851-3853).

### 4. COMMON MISTAKES
- At the wine meeting GY and ZF are not there; they were out shooting (3799). Xu Chu AND Zhang Liao fetch LB (3763).
- LB says 備 and "吾乃籠中鳥" to GY and ZF, not to Dong Cheng.
- Dong Cheng's oath list has six names when LB signs (3753-3755), LB is 7th.
- Cao Cao's wine pavilion is in the "後園" of his residence, not at Xutian.
- Yuan Shu: dies 建安四年六月 (3865); he dies after LB's victory, not by LB's hand. GY does not fight Yuan Shu in the text; ZF kills Ji Ling.
- Who kills Che Zhou: GY (3894-3895), not ZF. ZF kills his family (3900). LB did not order it and regrets it.
- Cao Cao addressed as 丞相 at all times. LB addresses Xu Chu as "公" (3841).
- Zhu Ling and Lu Zhao: Cao Cao's observers; LB sends them back (3874).
- Order of the Che Zhou beat: Che Zhou plans with Chen Deng; Chen Deng betrays the plan to his father who sends him to GY. Ambiguity: Chen Deng appears to be on both sides at once (3880-3884, 3894). The text treats him as LB's agent.
- LB is "刺史"? No: Che Zhou is called 刺史 (3850); LB's own title remains 左將軍 / 使君.

## Chapter 22: 袁曹各起馬步三軍, 關張共擒王劉二將 (3904-4097)

**Emotional turn: from Xuzhou's brief victory to a calm before war; GY and ZF show brothers' trust, and LB sets a diplomatic rather than martial defence.**

### 1. BEATS

1. **Chen Deng's plan: Zheng Xuan's letter** [3906-3921]. Place: Xuzhou. Present: LB, Chen Deng. Cao Cao fears Yuan Shao; LB: Yuan Shao has no relations with me and I just killed his brother. Chen Deng suggests Zheng Xuan (鄭康成), whom LB once studied under at Zhuo county and visited respectfully. LB and Chen Deng visit him; he writes the letter; LB sends Sun Qian.
   - Quote: Chen Deng: 「曹操所懼者袁紹。」(3906). LB: 「莫非鄭康成先生？」(3910). The narrator: 玄德在涿郡時，已曾師事之。(3916-3917).

Condition 3: he balks — 然則吾養雲長何用？此事卻難從 (4449). Zhang Liao cites Yu Rang's "國士" argument and Cao Cao agrees (4449-51). Xun Yu warns of a trick; Cao Cao: 雲長義士，必不失信 (4454-55) and withdraws 10 li.
- Quote: 操曰：「雲長義士，必不失信。」(4455)

**B25.4 Guan Yu and the ladies (Guan Yu, Lady Gan, Lady Mi, in the Xiapi residence).** Guan Yu enters Xiapi, finds the city untouched, kneels on the steps: 「使二嫂受驚，某之罪也。」(4458). He tells them the three conditions. Lady Gan notes the Cao army left them untouched and only fears Cao Cao will block his search for Liu Bei (4462-63); both leave the decision to him (4464-65).
- Quote: 甘夫人：「只恐日後曹操不肯容叔叔去尋皇叔。」(4463)

**B25.5 Surrender, Xuchang, the night at the post-house (Guan Yu, Cao Cao, the ladies).** Guan Yu meets Cao Cao at the camp gate: 「敗兵之將，深荷不殺之恩。」(4468). Cao Cao: 素慕雲長忠義 (4468). On the road Cao Cao puts him in one room with both ladies to confuse the lord–vassal order; Guan Yu stands outside with a candle all night (4474-76). In Xuchang he splits the mansion in two, the inner gate guarded by ten old soldiers, himself in the outer house (4476-77). The Emperor makes him **偏將軍 (Brigadier General)** (4477-78) — NOT yet 漢壽亭侯 (that comes at 4598).
- Quote: 關公乃秉燭立於戶外，自夜達旦，毫無倦色。(4475-76)

**B25.6 Cao Cao's gifts, in the novel's order (Guan Yu, Cao Cao).**
 a. Great banquet, guest's seat, brocades, gold/silver vessels — Guan Yu hands all to the ladies (4480-81). Small banquet every 3 days, big banquet every 5 (4481-82).
 b. **Ten beauties** — sent into the inner gate to serve the ladies; he bows at the inner gate every third day to ask after them (4482-83).
 c. **Green brocade war-robe**: Cao Cao notices his is worn and gives a new one; Guan Yu wears it underneath, old robe on top (4486-87). 「舊袍乃劉皇叔所賜，某穿之如見兄面，不敢以丞相之新賜而忘兄長之舊賜」 (4487-88). Cao Cao: 真義士也！ — "but in his heart displeased" (4489).
 d. **Beard bag** (紗錦囊): Guan Yu drunk, tears seen, says 「生不能報國家，而背其兄，徒為人也！」(4497-98); Cao Cao gives a silk bag for the beard; the Emperor gives him the title 美髯公 (4499-4502).
 e. **Red Hare**, Lü Bu's horse (see set-up): Cao Cao spots his thin horse (4504); gives Red Hare with saddle; Guan Yu bows twice; Cao Cao is stung: you never bowed to beauties or gold (4507-08). Guan Yu: 「吾知此馬日行千里，今幸得之，若知兄長下落，可一日而見面矣。」(4508-09). Cao Cao: 愕然悔 (4509).
- Quotes: 曹操：「何賤人貴畜耶？」(4508); 關公：「若知兄長下落，可一日而見面矣。」(4508-09)

**B25.7 The sisters' dream (Lady Gan, Lady Mi, Guan Yu).** Lady Gan dreams Liu Bei in a pit; both weep; Guan Yu kneels outside the inner gate: 「夢寐之事，不可憑信。」(4491-94). Cao Cao sees the tear-stained face at the next banquet (4496-97).

**B25.8 Zhang Liao probes; Xun Yu's remedy (Guan Yu, Zhang Liao, Cao Cao).** Guan Yu: 「吾固知曹公待吾甚厚；奈吾受劉皇叔厚恩，誓以共死，不可背之。吾終不留此。要必立以報曹公，然後去耳。」(4516-18). Zhang Liao: if Liu Bei is dead? — 「願從於地下。」(4518). Cao Cao: 事主不忘其本，乃天下之義士也 (4520). Xun Yu: he says he'll leave after earning merit — so don't give him the chance (4521).
- Quote: 關公：「吾終不留此。要必立以報曹公，然後去耳。」(4517-18)

**B25.9 Liu Bei at Yuan Shao's (Liu Bei, Yuan Shao, Tian Feng, Ju Shou; in Yuan Shao's camp).** Liu Bei grieves: brothers unknown, family in Cao's hands (4524). Yuan Shao wants to march on Xuchang; Tian Feng counsels delay; Liu Bei, asked, says Cao is a traitor and Yuan Shao would lose face with the realm (4529-30); Yuan Shao jails Tian Feng, Liu Bei pleads for him in vain (4531-32). Ju Shou scatters his wealth and bids his clan farewell (4532-34). **Liu Bei's role here is unflattering: he argues for the war.** Ambiguous whether his motive is sincere or tactical; the text does not say.
- Quote: 玄德曰：「二弟不知音耗，妻小陷於曹賊；上不能報國，下不能保家，安得不憂？」(4523-24)

**B25.10 Baima: Yan Liang kills two, Guan Yu kills him (Guan Yu, Cao Cao, Cheng Yu, Zhang Liao, Yan Liang, Song Xian, Wei Xu, Xu Huang; near Baima, Cao Cao camped by an earth hill).** Yuan Shao sends Yan Liang (Ju Shou warns he is narrow, 4536). Cao Cao leads 50,000 to Baima, camps by a hill; Yan Liang's 100,000 line up (4540-41). Cheng Yu proposes Guan Yu: Cao Cao fears he will leave after a feat; Cheng Yu: if Guan Yu breaks Yuan Shao, Yuan Shao will kill Liu Bei and Guan Yu has nowhere to go (4550-52). Guan Yu takes leave of the ladies, who say ask after Liu Bei (4553). On the hill Guan Yu scoffs: 「如土雞瓦犬耳」, 「如插標賣首耳」 (4558-60). He charges, kills Yan Liang, dismounts, cuts off his head, ties it to the horse's neck (4564-67). Presents the head to Cao Cao; mentions Zhang Fei: 「吾弟張翼德於百萬軍中取上將之首，如探囊取物耳」 (4569-70). Cao Cao orders Zhang Fei's name written inside his robe lapel (4570-71).
- Quotes: 關公曰：「吾觀顏良，如插標賣首耳！」(4560); 「吾弟張翼德於百萬軍中取上將之首，如探囊取物耳。」(4569-70)

**B25.11 Liu Bei nearly executed (Yuan Shao, Ju Shou, Liu Bei).** Yan Liang's rout reports a red-faced, long-bearded man; Ju Shou names Guan Yu; Yuan Shao orders Liu Bei beheaded (4573-76). Cliffhanger.

### 2. Set-up each beat needs (earlier in the book, ch.20-24; verify with colleague's span)
- **Peach Garden oath** (ch.1): 同生死 — the thing Zhang Liao quotes. Players need it before B25.2.
- **Liu Bei's flight / loss of Xuzhou / Xiapi**: Liu Bei, Zhang Fei lost; Guan Yu left at Xiapi guarding the ladies (placed by Liu Bei; see Zhang Liao's "付託" 4431). State this on screen before Tushan.
- **Lady Gan and Lady Mi**: Liu Bei's two wives; the novel never has them speak more than 2-3 lines each here; Lady Gan is the spokesperson (4462, 4492). Lady Mi speaks only in ch.28 (5016). Mi Zhu and Mi Fang are Lady Mi's brothers (ch.28 reunion).
- **Zhang Liao**: formerly Lü Bu's officer, now Cao Cao's; Guan Yu once saved him ("昔日蒙兄救弟" 4420 — an old favor; the text does not tell the circumstances; flag as backstory to be supplied, historically from the Xiapi/Lü Bu surrender episode). He is the go-between for the whole span.
- **Cao Cao's courting of Guan Yu**: earlier chapters show his admiration; the player should know Cao Cao as 丞相 who holds the Emperor (4446 "吾為漢相"), and Guan Yu's title 皇叔 for Liu Bei (Emperor's acknowledged uncle, ch.20 — set-up for 4442).
- **Red Hare (赤兔馬)**: Lü Bu's red horse (4506); needs a prior shot of Lü Bu riding it and its fame as a thousand-li horse; Guan Yu names it before Cao Cao says (4506).
- **Green robe** given by Liu Bei: the "舊袍乃劉皇叔所賜" (4487-88); no earlier text confirms the object, plant it.
- **Yan Liang and Yuan Shao's conflict with Cao Cao**: Yuan Shao is the northern lord (ch.22-24); Yan Liang is his top general, Wen Chou the second.
- **Tian Feng, Ju Shou**: Yuan Shao's advisers; Tian Feng jailed here (4531-32), still in jail at 4902.
- **Qing Long Dao (青龍刀)**: first named here at 4555 as Guan Yu's weapon.

### 4. Side episodes / cuttable
- Side thread (Yuan Shao's court, not Liu Bei's line): Tian Feng jailed, Ju Shou's farewell (4525-34). Short, strong, foreshadows Guandu.
- Cut safely: Cheng Yu's fake-deserter mechanics details (keep one line); the ten old soldiers detail; the full list of Cao Cao's banquets; the beard bag/美髯公 episode if pacing needs (it is characterization, not plot) — but it is also a nice tonal beat.
- Cut safely: Xun Yu/Cao Cao dialogue at 4520-21 only if the player is told "Cao Cao worries he will leave".

### 5. Common mistakes (chapter 25)
- **Who holds what at this point**: Guan Yu is 偏將軍 from the Emperor (4477-78), not yet 漢壽亭侯. Cao Cao is addressed 丞相 (Guan Yu 4440, 4468 ff.; Zhang Liao 4441), 曹公 (Zhang Liao 4437; Guan Yu 4516), and calls himself 漢相 (4446). The Emperor (獻帝) is the "漢帝" of condition one.
- "Yield to Han, not to Cao" is Guan Yu's wording; he says "不降曹操", Cao Cao answers with the pun that Han = himself.
- Zhang Liao makes **three sins** and **three advantages**; Guan Yu makes **three conditions**. Do not conflate (4427 / 4438 / 4440).
- Guan Yu's stipulation 2 asks for Liu Bei's *stipend* (俸祿) for the ladies, not for himself (4442). Cao Cao doubles it (4448).
- Order of gifts: banquets/brocades/vessels → ten beauties → green robe → beard bag → Red Hare. Only Red Hare gets a bow. Quote 4507-08.
- Cao Cao does not capture Liu Bei's family *himself* in the sense of holding them: they are left in the house, guarded (4424-25).
- Yan Liang's kills: Song Xian then Wei Xu then Xu Huang survives (4544-48). Xu Huang is not killed.
- Guan Yu says of Zhang Fei: 百萬軍中取上將之首 (4569-70); Cao Cao writes the warning on his robe (4570-71), planting Changban.
- Who is at Baima: Cao Cao leads 50,000 of 150,000 (4539-40). The "土山" at Baima (4540) is a different hill from Tushan at Xiapi (4413) — avoid conflating.

## CHAPTER 26 — 袁本初敗兵折將，關雲長挂印封金 (4579-4726)

### 1. BEATS
**B26.1 Liu Bei defends himself (Liu Bei, Yuan Shao, Ju Shou; Yuan Shao's tent).** 「天下同貌者不少，豈赤面長鬚之人，即為關某也？」(4582). Yuan Shao, 沒主張, scolds Ju Shou and seats Liu Bei (4583-84). Wen Chou volunteers (4584-87). Ju Shou advises holding at Yanjin / Guandu; dismissed; says 「悠悠黃河，吾其濟乎！」and feigns illness (4588-91). Liu Bei asks to go with Wen Chou: to repay Yuan Shao and verify Guan Yu (4593-94); given 30,000 as rearguard; Wen Chou: 劉玄德屢敗之將，於軍不利 (4594-95).
- Quote: 玄德曰：「一者報明公之德，二者就探雲長的實信。」(4593-94)

**B26.2 Guan Yu becomes 漢壽亭侯 (Cao Cao).** After Yan Liang, Cao Cao memorializes and Guan Yu is enfeoffed **漢壽亭侯**, a seal cast and sent (4598-99).

**B26.3 Yanjin: the bait (Cao Cao, Xun You/公達, Lü Qian, Wen Chou, Zhang Liao, Xu Huang, Guan Yu).** Cao Cao sends supplies in front as bait (4600-02); Wen Chou's men plunder and break order; Cao Cao's troops counter-charge (4607-11). Wen Chou shoots Zhang Liao's helmet and his horse's cheek; Zhang Liao falls; Xu Huang retreats (4613-17). **Guan Yu arrives with ten-odd riders and kills Wen Chou, striking from behind in the chase "腦後一刀" (4618-20)**. Xun You (荀攸, courtesy 公達, 4639) is the only one who read Cao Cao's bait (4607-08, 4639-40).
- Quote: 關公馬快，趕上文醜，腦後一刀，將文醜斬下馬來。(4619-20)

**B26.4 Liu Bei sees the banner (Liu Bei, in the rear, across the river).** The scouts report the red-faced one killed Wen Chou; Liu Bei rides up, sees across the river a banner 「漢壽亭侯關雲長」, thanks heaven and earth: 「原來吾弟果然在曹操處！」 He is cut off by Cao Cao's troops and must retire (4623-26).
- Quote: 玄德暗謝天地曰：「原來吾弟果然在曹操處！」(4625-26)

**B26.5 Second near-execution; Liu Bei talks his way out (Yuan Shao, Guo Tu, Shen Pei, Liu Bei; Guandu camp).** Guo Tu and Shen Pei accuse Liu Bei (4626-28). Liu Bei: Cao Cao wants Yuan Shao to kill him, using Guan Yu: 「此借公之手以殺劉備也」(4631-32). Yuan Shao relents, afraid of the name of one who harms the worthy (4633). Liu Bei offers to send a secret letter to Guan Yu; Yuan Shao: 「吾得雲長，勝顏良、文醜十倍也」(4635-37). The army pulls back to Wuyang (4637-38).

**B26.6 Guan Yu's Runan sortie (Guan Yu, Sun Qian, Gong Du, Xun Yu; Runan).** Yellow-Turban remnants Liu Pi and Gong Du are raiding (4642-43). Guan Yu volunteers: 「關某久閒，必生疾病」(4644). Xun Yu warns Cao Cao (4645-46); Cao Cao: "after this one, he won't go to the front again" (4646). Near Runan Guan Yu's patrols catch two spies, one is **Sun Qian**; Sun Qian says Liu Bei is at Yuan Shao's, and that Liu Pi and Gong Du will fake a defeat so Guan Yu can take the ladies to Liu Bei (4648-58). Guan Yu: 「吾見兄長一面，雖萬死不辭。今回許昌，便辭曹操也。」(4657-58). Next day Gong Du feints and says 「故主之恩，不可忘也。公當速進，我讓汝南。」(4662-63). Guan Yu takes Runan back to Cao Cao's account and returns to Xuchang (4664-65).

- Quote: 甘夫人：「叔宜上緊。」(4675)

**B26.8 Zhang Liao pushes again (Zhang Liao, Guan Yu).** Guan Yu: 「我與兄，朋友之交也；我與玄德，是朋友而兄弟、兄弟而又君臣也。豈可共論乎？」(4680-81). Cao Cao: 吾自有計留之 (4682).

**B26.9 Chen Zhen's letter and Guan Yu's reply (Guan Yu, Chen Zhen of Yuan Shao's staff, at Guan Yu's Xuchang house).** Liu Bei's letter: 備與足下，自桃園締盟，誓以同死；今何中道相違，割恩斷義？君必欲取功名，圖富貴，願獻備首級以成全功！(4688-89). Guan Yu weeps: 「人生天地間，無終始者，非君子也。吾來時明白，去時不可不明白。」(4692-93). His reply cites Yangjiao Ai and Zuo Botao (4697-98), explains he stayed because of the two sisters-in-law (4700-01), and swears 羽但懷異心，神人共戮 (4702).
- Ambiguity: the novel gives Chen Zhen as "南陽陳震" (4685); at 5121 he is Yuan Shao's envoy to Sun Ce.

**B26.10 Cao Cao's 迴避牌, Guan Yu's farewell letter, the departure (Guan Yu, Cao Cao, Zhang Liao).** Cao Cao hangs a "not receiving" sign (4704-05); Guan Yu calls several times, goes to Zhang Liao's house; Zhang Liao feigns illness (4706-07). He writes: 「新恩雖厚，舊義難忘」(4711). He **seals all gold in the storehouse, hangs the 漢壽亭侯 seal on the hall, leaves the ten beauties in the inner rooms, takes only his old retainers** (4714-15, 4721-23). Riding Red Hare with Green Dragon blade, he forces the north gate with a roar (4715-17). Cao Cao reads the letter; Cai Yang volunteers to chase with 3,000 (4724).
- Quote: 關公：「新恩雖厚，舊義難忘。」(4711)

### 5. Common mistakes (chapter 26)
- **Wen Chou is killed at Yanjin by Guan Yu, from behind**, after Zhang Liao has been unhorsed and Xu Huang has retreated. Do not say Zhang Liao killed him.
- **Liu Bei is in the rearguard of 30,000, Wen Chou has 70,000** (4595-96).
- The seal and the title: 漢壽亭侯 is a marquis of a *village* (亭侯); text writes it so (4598, 4625, 4714). The 7-character banner reads 漢壽亭侯關雲長 (4625).
- **Who writes what**: Liu Bei writes the first letter (via Chen Zhen); Guan Yu answers (4697-4702); Guan Yu then writes separately to Cao Cao (4710-12). Don't merge.
- Guan Yu does NOT take the gold, the maidens, the Cao-assigned servants (4705, 4714, 4722). He DOES keep Red Hare and the green robe given by Liu Bei and (by 4756, ch.27) Cao Cao's farewell robe. The Cao Cao robe from ch.25 (4486) is not mentioned again; the text does not say whether he took it.
- Guan Yu's farewell letter does not name Cao Cao's title; in his thoughts he says 「此曹丞相不容我去之意」(4707-08).
- Liu Bei's two near-execution scenes: ch.25 end (Yan Liang) and ch.26 (Wen Chou); both resolved by his argument, not by Guan Yu.
- The phrase 「千里走單騎」 is the chapter-27 title; Guan Yu is not alone (he has the ladies, "二十餘人" 4721-22).

Han Fu's arrow hits Guan Yu's **left arm**; he pulls it with his mouth and kills Han Fu, head and shoulder (4817-19). He binds the wound with silk (4820).

**B27.8 Pass 3: Yishui (沂水關) — Bian Xi, Zhenguo Temple (Guan Yu; Bian Xi, monk Pu Jing, ladies).** Bian Xi (a Yellow Turban remnant, flail user, from Bingzhou, 4821-22) greets him warmly, leads him to **Zhenguo Temple** (鎮國寺, Emperor Ming's temple, 4827), with 200 axemen hidden. The monk **Pu Jing** is Guan Yu's fellow villager from 蒲東 (4828-31). He signals with his戒刀 (4837). Guan Yu sees the ambush, kills the attackers and Bian Xi (4838-43). Pu Jing: 「貧僧此處難容，收拾衣缽，亦往他處雲游也」(4844-45).
- Quote: 關公：「卞君請關某，是好意？還是歹意？」(4838)

**B27.9 Pass 4: Xingyang — Wang Zhi, Hu Ban (Guan Yu, Wang Zhi, Hu Ban).** Wang Zhi is Han Fu's in-law (4847-48), hosts him at the post-house (4849-54), orders Hu Ban to burn it at third watch (4856-58). Hu Ban peeks, sees Guan Yu reading by lamp, left hand on beard: 「真天人也！」(4863). Guan Yu asks, hands him Hu Hua's letter (4864-65); Hu Ban warns him and opens the city gate (4866-68). Wang Zhi catches up and dies by one stroke (4871-73).

**B27.10 Pass 5: Yellow River ferry — Qin Qi (Guan Yu; Liu Yan; Qin Qi).** At Huazhou border Liu Yan (Governor of Dongjun, Baima defender) refuses boats for fear of Xiahou Dun (4875-80). Guan Yu: 「我前者誅顏良，文醜，亦曾與足下解厄。」(4879-80). At the ferry Qin Qi, Xiahou Dun's subordinate: 「你便插翅，也飛不過去！」(4885). Guan Yu: 「汝比顏良，文醜，若何？」(4887). One bout, Qin Qi dies (4889). They cross; **「關公所歷關隘五處，斬將六員」** (4891).

**B27.11 The turn (Guan Yu, Sun Qian).** Guan Yu laments: 「吾非欲沿途殺人，奈事不得已也。曹公知之，必以我為負恩之人矣。」(4897-98). Sun Qian arrives: Liu Bei has left Yuan Shao for Runan; Yuan Shao's camp is divided (4901-05). Guan Yu heads for Runan; Xiahou Dun appears behind them (4911).

### 5. Common mistakes (chapter 27)
- **Titles**: at the passes Guan Yu announces himself 漢壽亭侯 (4810, 4883). Cao Cao is **丞相** (Kong Xiu, Han Fu, Qin Qi all say 丞相文憑). The chapter title's "漢壽侯" is shorthand.
- **Does Cao Cao permit him to pass?** He never writes a pass in ch.27. His letters-of-passage are sent *after* (messengers in ch.28, 4920-22). So Guan Yu's kills are unauthorized; Cao Cao does not know of them (4922, 4928).
- **The Red Hare**: Guan Yu holds back because of the carts (4740); the horse could outpace Cao Cao's riders.
- **Who fights who**: Kong Xiu uses a spear (4798); Meng Tan double blades (4815); Bian Xi flying flails (4842); Wang Zhi spear (4872).
- Han Fu's arrow hits the **left arm** (4817); no healing episode.
- **Hu Ban opens the gate and then goes back to set the fire** to keep his cover (4870).
- **Geography caveat**: the novel sends him from Xuchang north-west through Dongling, then Luoyang, Yishui, Xingyang, to the Yellow River. This is not real geography (Yishui is between Luoyang and Xingyang by the novel only); do not map it to a modern route. Dongling's location is "取路投洛陽來" (4789) with no direction given.
- Luoyang is not called a 關 in the text; Han Fu mans a 關口 on the approach (4809).
- Liao Hua's name courtesy 元儉 (4767); he does not join in this chapter.
- Zhang Fei is not present; Liu Bei is not present.

## CHAPTER 28 — 斬蔡陽兄弟釋疑，會古城主臣聚義 (4914-5122)

### 1. BEATS
**B28.1 Xiahou Dun's chase (Guan Yu, Xiahou Dun, two Cao Cao messengers, Zhang Liao, Sun Qian).** Xiahou Dun and 200 riders overtake; Guan Yu: 「汝來趕我，有失丞相大度。」(4917). A messenger shows Cao Cao's papers; Xiahou Dun refuses: capture him alive (4920-24). A second messenger arrives; again refused: he doesn't know about the killings (4926-29). Zhang Liao arrives: 「奉丞相鈞旨... 任便放行」(4932-33). Xiahou Dun cites Cai Yang's nephew Qin Qi (4933-34); Zhang Liao will settle it with Cai Yang. Guan Yu declines to return to Cao Cao: 「安有是理！文遠回見丞相，幸為我謝罪。」(4937). Hands clasped, they part (4937-38).

**B28.2 The Guo Chang farm and Pei Yuanshao / Zhou Cang (Guan Yu, Sun Qian, Guo Chang, his son, Pei Yuanshao, Zhou Cang, Lady Gan).** In a rain Guan Yu lodges with **Guo Chang** (4941-44). His son tries to steal Red Hare and is kicked down (4952-55); Guan Yu forgives him for his father's sake (4957-59). Next day the son brings the Yellow Turban chief **Pei Yuanshao** with 100 men for the horse; Guan Yu unfastens his beard-bag and shows his beard; Pei Yuanshao kneels (4963-71). He names **Zhou Cang**, ex-Zhang Bao officer, two-arm strength of a thousand jin, bearded, on Mount Wo Niu (4975-77). Zhou Cang begs to serve on foot (4982-85). **Lady Gan** is consulted: she reminds Guan Yu he turned Liao Hua away; "一二人相從，無妨於事" (4988-93). Zhou Cang leaves his men with Pei Yuanshao and follows alone (4994-95).
- Quote: 周倉：「若以眾人相隨為不便，可令其盡跟裴元紹去。倉隻身步行，跟隨將軍，雖萬里不辭也！」(4991-92)

**B28.3 Gucheng: Zhang Fei attacks Guan Yu (Zhang Fei, Guan Yu, Lady Gan, Lady Mi, Sun Qian; outside the north gate).** Guan Yu learns a General Zhang has seized the Gucheng county (4997-5000). Zhang Fei had been at Mangdang mountain a month, then took the county seal when the magistrate refused grain (5001-02). Sun Qian announces; Zhang Fei says nothing, arms himself, rides out with 1,000 (5006). Zhang Fei attacks: 「你既無義，有何面目來與我相見！」(5009-10): "你背了兄長，降了曹操，封侯賜爵。今又來賺我！" (5010-11). The ladies intervene (5013-17); Zhang Fei: 「忠臣寧死而不辱。大丈夫豈有事二主之理！」(5017). Cao Cao's banners appear (5021-22).
- Quote: 張飛：「忠臣寧死而不辱。大丈夫豈有事二主之理！」(5017)

**B28.4 Cai Yang beheaded to the drum (Guan Yu, Zhang Fei, Cai Yang).** Zhang Fei gives him "three rolls of the drum" to kill the oncoming general (5023-24). Cai Yang: 「你殺吾外甥秦琪」(5027); Guan Yu kills him **before the first drum roll ends** (5028-29). A captured standard-bearer recounts what Guan Yu did at Xuchang; Zhang Fei is convinced (5029-32). Cai Yang had come on Cao Cao's order against Liu Pi, not against Guan Yu (5030-31).
- Quote: 只見一通鼓未盡，關公刀起處，蔡陽頭已落地。(5028-29)

**B28.5 Mi brothers (Mi Zhu, Mi Fang, Zhang Fei).** Mi Zhu and Mi Fang arrive; Jian Yong has gone to Yuan Shao (5034-39). They enter, the ladies tell all; Zhang Fei **weeps and bows to Guan Yu** (5041-43).

**B28.6 Runan, then Yuan Shao's border (Guan Yu, Sun Qian, Zhou Cang, Guan Ding).** Guan Yu goes to Runan: Liu Bei had gone back to Yuan Shao (5045-48). Guan Yu returns, insists on keeping Gucheng as a base (5051-52). He sends Zhou Cang to fetch Pei Yuanshao's men (5054-56). Guan Yu lodges at **Guan Ding's** farm at the border (5058-62).

**B28.7 Liu Bei's escape plan (Liu Bei, Jian Yong, Sun Qian, Yuan Shao, Guo Tu; Ji Province).** Jian Yong: say you will go to Jing Province to enlist Liu Biao (5064-66). Liu Bei to Yuan Shao: 「劉景升鎮守荊襄九郡，兵精糧足」(5069); Yuan Shao says he will kill Guan Yu; Liu Bei: 「顏良、文醜比之二鹿耳，雲長乃一虎也。失二鹿而得一虎，何恨之有？」(5072-74). Jian Yong goes along "to watch" Liu Bei (5077-79). **B28.9 Zhao Yun (Liu Bei, Guan Yu, Zhou Cang, Zhao Yun; Mount Wo Niu).** Zhou Cang comes back wounded: Pei Yuanshao was killed and the camp taken by a lone rider (5090-94). Roll call at 5114-15: Liu Bei, Guan, Zhang, Zhao Yun, Sun Qian, Jian Yong, Mi Zhu, Mi Fang, Guan Ping, Zhou Cang — "共四五千人". They leave Gucheng for Runan at Liu Pi and Gong Du's invitation (5115-17). ### 5. Common mistakes (chapter 28)
- **Cai Yang is killed by Guan Yu at Gucheng, in front of Zhang Fei**, before the first drum roll ends (5028-29); not by Zhang Fei, and not in the passes (4891).
- **Zhang Fei attacks Guan Yu** with the eight-foot serpent spear (5006-08, 5023) *first*; Zhang Fei cries and kneels only after the ladies' account (5042). He does not see Cai Yang's army coming before accusing Guan Yu of leading Cao Cao's soldiers (5019-22).

- **Liu Bei does not go to Gucheng with Guan Yu first**; Guan Yu fails to find him at Runan (5047-48), then meets him at Guan Ding's farm, then Zhao Yun appears on the way, then Gucheng (5083-5109). Liu Bei and Zhang Fei meet only in the Gucheng reunion (5109).
- **Liu Bei's escape**: Jian Yong stays at Yuan Shao's, Liu Bei says he will recruit Liu Biao; Guo Tu is suspicious (5078-80).
- **Who is where at the end**: Gucheng (Liu Bei, Guan, Zhang, Zhao Yun, Sun Qian, Jian Yong, Mi Zhu, Mi Fang, Guan Ping, Zhou Cang, ladies), then Runan.
- **Zhao Yun is not Cao Cao's man**; he kills Pei Yuanshao for his horse (5101-03). Pei Yuanshao's death is not at Guan Yu's hand.
- **Lady Gan rules** on Zhou Cang (4988-93); Lady Mi's only on-page speech in this span is 5016-17.
- **The Guan family**: Guan Ding (father), Guan Ning (elder, literary), Guan Ping (younger, martial, 18), 5083-87.
- **Ambiguity**: Liu Bei's letter at 4688 reached Guan Yu (answered 4697) but when Sun Qian says Liu Bei is at Runan (4903-04) the text gives no timeline; Liu Bei is also at Runan "數日" then at Yuan Shao's again (5047-48) — Liu Pi's report contradicts Sun Qian's earlier statement (4903-04). The novel is not careful here.

## Emotional turn, one line per chapter
- Ch.25: from a trapped warrior's "honor in death" to a harder honor — surviving under another man's roof without becoming his.
- Ch.26: Guan Yu proves his worth to Cao Cao (Yan Liang, Wen Chou) and, once Liu Bei's whereabouts are known, spends all that gratitude to leave, leaving gold, beauties and the seal behind.
- Ch.27: leaving costs blood — six men die because no one will honor a pass he does not have, and he ends the chapter wishing it could have been otherwise.
- Ch.28: the faith kept at such cost is nearly refused by Zhang Fei, then proven by Cai Yang's head, and the brothers and their retainers are gathered at Gucheng.
