# World 2 stills: what each one must show

Same rules as `docs/stills-must-show.md`: one line per id, who is in the frame, the single action, and what must not appear. Han dynasty, ancient China: no glass, modern clothes or European objects, no text, no speech bubbles. A still that contradicts its scene is wrong. The steps are in `tools/tk_story_w2.py` (`STILLS_W2`). Quiet scenes and the great deaths get a still only where the picture is the point; the poison scene (the boy Emperor) has none, on purpose.
