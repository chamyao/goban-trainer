# World 3 (Book 3): White Gate Tower, chapters 10-19: plan

**Spine: loyalty and its limits.** Read in full from the novel (Gutenberg #23950, ch. 10-19). This is the plan to agree before the scenes are written into `tools/tk_story_w3.py`, in the shape of Book 2: a main line that follows Liu Bei, and side threads told from the road. Grades 10K-9K.

What the Book is about: everyone in these chapters is asked how far loyalty goes. Liu Bei refuses Xuzhou twice because taking it would be disloyal to the man who asked for help, then takes it from a dying man's hand. He shelters Lü Bu, who has betrayed two fathers, and is betrayed by him. Zhang Fei swears off wine for his brother, and breaks the vow in a day. Chen Gong leaves Cao Cao over a massacre, serves Lü Bu to the end, and will not beg. At the White Gate Tower Lü Bu begs Liu Bei for one word, and Liu Bei gives him one: "Have you not seen what happened to Ding Yuan and Dong Zhuo?" That is where loyalty runs out.

Echoes: the oath ("not born on the same day…") comes back at Xuyi as "brothers are hands and feet"; Lü Bu's two fathers from Book 2; Red Hare is stolen at the end, as it was given at the start; the seal Sun Jian hid in Book 2 is pawned by his son.

## Opening scroll: Chapter 10

Picks up Book 2's last line ("What would become of the Emperor?"):
1. The Emperor stayed a prisoner of Li Jue and Guo Si in Chang'an, and they fought over him like dogs over a bone. (His road is a side thread.)
2. In the east Cao Cao had grown strong. He sent for his father, Cao Song. Tao Qian, governor of Xuzhou, feasted the old man and gave him an escort, and the escort, Zhang Kai, an old Yellow Turban, murdered him for his carts.
3. Cao Cao put on white mourning and marched to wash Xuzhou in blood. Old Tao Qian sent for help in every direction. One of his letters went to Kong Rong of Beihai, and Beihai was itself besieged by Yellow Turbans…

## Side threads (each a short road off the main one)

**A. Chen Gong's Counsel** (ch. 10-12, 16, 19). The other loyal man of the Book, so that the White Gate lands.
- `a1` The Temple in the Rain (ch10): Cao Song murdered by Zhang Kai; the narrator's line that Cao Cao, who killed Lü Boshe's household, now loses his own.
- `a2` Chen Gong Pleads (ch10): he begs Cao Cao to spare Xuzhou's people, is refused, and leaves. "Now I cannot face Tao Qian either."
- `a3` Puyang (ch11-12): Chen Gong sets the fire trap; Lü Bu taps Cao Cao's helmet in the smoke and asks where Cao Cao is. Sets up Zhang Liao's taunt at the tower.
- `a4` Three Plans (ch19, Xiapi): strike before the camp is pitched; the pincer; cut the grain. Lady Yan and Diaochan keep him in. "We will die with no ground to be buried in."
- `a5` The Wine Jars (ch19): Hou Cheng's wine, the fifty strokes, Red Hare stolen out of the east gate.

**B. The Emperor's Road** (ch13-14). Answers Book 2's last question.
- `b1` Two Dogs and a Bone (ch13): Li Jue and Guo Si at war in Chang'an; the Emperor given rotten meat.
- `b2` The River (ch13): the flight east; the Emperor let down the bank in silk; the small boat.
- `b3` Among the Thorns (ch14): court held standing in burned Luoyang; Cao Cao arrives and takes the Emperor to Xu. "From then on, all power was Cao Cao's."

**C. The Little Conqueror** (ch15). Sun Jian's son, and a promise kept.
- `c1` The Seal in Pawn (Shouchun): Sun Ce gives Yuan Shu the Imperial Seal for 3,000 men. Callback to Book 2's well.
- `c2` Shenting Ridge: Sun Ce and Taishi Ci (from scene 1) fight until each holds the other's helmet and halberd.
- `c3` By Noon Tomorrow: Taishi Ci, captured, asks leave to gather his scattered men, and comes back on time.

**D. Cao Cao's Men** (ch16-18). What Cao Cao's loyalty costs others.
- `d1` The Gate at Wancheng (ch16): Dian Wei holds the gate without his halberds, and dies on his feet.
- `d2` The Granary Officer (ch17): Wang Hou, innocent, beheaded to calm the army. "I know you are innocent."
- `d3` Father's Essence (ch18): Xiahou Dun pulls the arrow from his eye and eats it.

Lighter material left out or told in a line: Ma Teng and Ma Chao (ch10), Kong Rong's childhood, Xu Chu and Dian Wei's recruitment, Yuan Shu crowning himself (one line in `xu`), Chen Gui's "condolence" over the marriage (a line in `halberd`'s aftermath), Liu An the hunter (ch19: too grim for a scene; one line, or left out).

## The Star Lords

Twice this Book, always at their rock under a great pine (ch. 69's 盤石 under the 大松樹; no incense): at Pingyuan's gate (scene 1, counsel about keeping one's word), and before the Huainan road (scene 15, the counsel that wins it). They are at the White Gate as a board on the tower and say nothing.
