# The Diaochan arc (novel chapters 8–9): anatomy draft

Written from the Chinese original (第八回、第九回). Everything in a beat happens in the novel. Lines marked **[legwork]** fill in something the novel summarises in one sentence; each one cites the sentence it fills.

**How to read this:**
- **Contest:** a go problem *mid-scene*, at a real moment of risk in the novel.
- **Legwork:** go problems or deliveries *before* a scene, as the way in.
- **Scene:** plays with no board.
- **(b):** the attempt fails as written, even when the player solves it.

**Whom we follow.** The novel cuts between people, so the arc does too. Every handoff happens where two of them meet:

> **Wang Yun** → (Diaochan walks into the banquet) → **Diaochan** → (Lü Bu stops Wang Yun on the road) → **Wang Yun** → **Diaochan** → (Wang Yun finds Lü Bu on the ridge) → **Wang Yun** → (Li Su breaks the arrow) → **Li Su** → (the palace gate) → **Wang Yun** → (the envoy is refused) → **Jia Xu / Li Jue** *(villains)* → (Chang'an falls) → **Wang Yun**

## The beats

### A1 · The roadside banquet *(Wang Yun)* · Scene
**Place:** outside the Heng Gate. One scene in two parts, with the light changing to mark a second day.
1. Surrendered soldiers are mutilated in front of the officials while Dong Zhuo eats and laughs. **Shown only as sound and reaction**: officials' chopsticks fall and a still shows the officials' faces.
2. At the court banquet, Lü Bu whispers in Dong Zhuo's ear and drags Zhang Wen out. A red tray is carried in (offscreen).
   - Dong Zhuo: 「张温结连袁术，欲图害我。因使人寄书来，错下在吾儿奉先处，故斬之。」 ("Zhang Wen plotted with Yuan Shu against me. His letter went to my son Fengxian by mistake.")

**Why it's here:** it sets the danger. A letter in the wrong hands means death, and that's exactly what Wang Yun is about to risk.
**No board:** Wang Yun can only watch.

### A2 · The peony pavilion *(Wang Yun)* · Contest
**Place:** Wang Yun's rear garden, at **night**. Walk out into the garden; the scene starts at the pavilion.
- Diaochan sighing. 「贱人将有私情耶？」 ("Have you a lover?") She kneels and gives her vow: 「…倘有用妾之处，万死不辞。」 ("If there is any use for me, I would not refuse ten thousand deaths.")
- In the painted pavilion he kneels to her and lays out the chain plot.
- **Contest:** at 「事若泄漏，我灭门矣」 ("If this leaks, my whole family dies"). **Dilemma:** *"Stake the whole family on a sixteen-year-old girl?"* Solving it is Wang Yun deciding to trust her. She answers: 「妾若不报大义，死于万刃之下。」 ("If I do not repay this, let me die under ten thousand blades.")
- **Still:** Diaochan kneeling in the moonlit garden. *(This is the image you mentioned from the old book.)*

### A3 · The gold crown *(Wang Yun)* · Legwork
**[legwork]** fills in 「将家藏明珠数颗，令良匠嵌造金冠一顶，使人密送吕布。」 ("He set several pearls kept in the family into a gold crown by a fine craftsman, and secretly sent it to Lü Bu.")
1. Take the **family pearls** from the chest in the rear hall. *Gained: pearls.*
2. Bring them to the **jeweller** (delivery). *Gained: the gold crown.*
3. Deliver it **secretly** to **Lü Bu's quarters**. Dong Zhuo's men are on the street; that's a **go problem** at the gate, "pass the crown in without being noticed".
4. **Gate:** A4 opens once the crown is delivered.

### A4 · The first banquet *(Wang Yun → Diaochan)* · Contest
**Place:** Wang Yun's rear hall.
- Wang Yun flatters Lü Bu: 「允非敬将军之职，敬将军之才也。」 ("I honour not your rank, General, but your talent.") Then: 「唤孩儿来。」 ("Call my daughter in.")
- **Handoff:** Diaochan walks in, and **from here the player is Diaochan.**
- **Contest:** pouring Lü Bu's wine, when she has to make him want her. 「两下眉来眼去」 ("their eyes met again and again"). **Dilemma:** *"Win him."*
- Wang Yun, pretending to be drunk: 「吾欲将此女送与将军为妾，还肯纳否？」 ("I would give her to you as a concubine. Will you have her?") Lü Bu: 「布当效犬马之报。」 ("I'll serve you like a dog or a horse.")
- She sends him off with a look (秋波送情).

### A5 · The second banquet *(Diaochan)* · Contest
**Place:** Wang Yun's front hall, then the rear hall. Wang Yun has invited Dong Zhuo, catching him at court while Lü Bu was away; the narration covers this.
- Dong Zhuo arrives with a hundred guards carrying halberds. Wang Yun flatters him with omens: 「汉家气数已尽」 ("the Han's fortune is spent").
- **Diaochan dances** behind the curtain, then sings. The novel's verse about her song ends: 「丁香舌吐横钢剑，要斩奸邪乱国臣。」 ("From her clove-sweet tongue comes a steel sword, to cut down the traitor who wrecks the realm.")
- **Contest:** the dance. **Dilemma:** *"Make him take me."*
- 「真神仙中人也！」 ("Truly one of the immortals!") Dong Zhuo accepts her, and she is sent ahead to his residence in a covered carriage.
- **Still:** Diaochan dancing behind the curtain, with Dong Zhuo watching.

### A6 · Red lanterns on the road *(Wang Yun)* · Contest
**Handoff:** Lü Bu stops Wang Yun's horse on the road. **Night**, with two rows of red lanterns.
- Lü Bu grabs his collar: 「司徒既以貂蝉许我，今又送与太师，何相戏耶？」 ("You promised her to me and now you give her to the Grand Preceptor. Are you mocking me?")
- 「此非说话处。」 ("This is no place to talk.") They go to his house.
- **Contest:** the lie under pressure: "The Grand Preceptor took her to marry her to *you*." Lü Bu apologises.

### A7 · The window *(Diaochan)* · Legwork, then contest
**Place:** the Chancellor's residence, the next morning. The player walks Dong Zhuo's house as Diaochan: maids, a gatekeeper and guards to pass.
- A maid gossips: 「夜来太师与新人共寝，至今未起。」 ("The Grand Preceptor spent the night with the new girl and hasn't got up.")
- **Contest:** combing her hair at the window, she sees Lü Bu's reflection in the pond. 「故蹙双眉，做忧愁不乐之态，复以香罗频拭眼泪。」 ("She knitted her brows on purpose, put on a look of sorrow, and wiped her eyes again and again with a perfumed handkerchief.") She has to act grief convincingly.
- Middle hall: half her face shows behind the curtain while Dong Zhuo eats. Dong Zhuo: 「奉先无事且退。」 ("Fengxian, if there's nothing else, go.")

### A8 · The sickbed *(Diaochan)* · Contest
**Place:** Dong Zhuo's bedchamber, a month later.
- Diaochan nursing him: 「衣不解带」 ("she never took off her clothes").
- **Contest:** Lü Bu comes in. Behind the bed she points to her heart, then to Dong Zhuo, weeping. *Signal him without waking Dong Zhuo.*
- **(b):** Dong Zhuo wakes anyway: 「汝敢戏吾爱姬耶！」 ("You dare toy with my favourite!") Lü Bu is banished.
- Then a short scene, no board: Li Ru patches it up with ten jin of gold and twenty bolts of brocade.

### A9 · The Phoenix Pavilion *(Diaochan)* · Contest, the arc's set piece
**Place:** the rear garden of the Chancellor's residence.
- Lü Bu slips away from court and ties his horse at the gate, the clue that later gives him away. 「汝可去后园中凤仪亭边等我。」 ("Wait for me by the Phoenix Pavilion in the rear garden.")
- Diaochan walks to the pavilion through the flowers.
- Her speech, then she runs for the lotus pond. Lü Bu catches her.
- **Contest:** at 「君如此惧怕老贼，妾身无见天日之期矣！」 ("If you fear the old traitor so much, I'll never see the light of day!"). It's her most dangerous push: if she presses too hard, he leaves. 「谁想反受他人之制乎！」 ("Who'd have thought the greatest hero alive takes orders from another man!") He holds her.
- Dong Zhuo walks in, roars, seizes the halberd and throws it. Lü Bu knocks it away and runs. Dong Zhuo collides with someone at the garden gate, and the scene ends on 「未知此人是谁」 ("who this man was, we shall see").
- **Still:** Lü Bu holding Diaochan at the pavilion. *(The other image from the old book.)*

### A10 · The sword on the wall *(Diaochan)* · Contest
**Place:** the rear hall. The man was Li Ru, who has just advised Dong Zhuo to give her to Lü Bu.
- Dong Zhuo: 「我今将汝赐与吕布，何如？」 ("What if I give you to Lü Bu?")
- **Contest:** 「遂掣壁间宝剑欲自刎」 ("She snatched the sword from the wall to cut her own throat"). It's her biggest bluff, with real steel at her throat. Then: 「此必李儒之计也！」 ("This must be Li Ru's scheme!")
- Dong Zhuo will take her to Meiwu. Li Ru tries again and is threatened with death: 「吾等皆死于妇人之手矣！」 ("We will all die at a woman's hand!")
- **The carriage:** she sees Lü Bu in the crowd and covers her face as if weeping. **Still:** her face at the carriage curtain.

### A11 · The ridge *(Wang Yun)* · Contest
**Handoff:** Lü Bu on the earthen ridge, watching the carriage dust. Wang Yun comes up behind him.
- Wang Yun feigns shock (佯惊), and they go to the secret room.
- **Contest:** the persuasion. 「将军自姓吕，太师自姓董。掷戟之时，岂有父子情耶？」 ("You are a Lü; he is a Dong. When he threw that halberd, was there any father in it?") Then the choice between a loyal minister remembered for a hundred generations and a traitor who stinks for ten thousand years.
- Lü Bu cuts his arm and swears in blood.

### A12 · The conspirators *(Wang Yun)* · Legwork
**[legwork]** fills in 「允即请仆射士孙瑞、司隶校尉黄琬商议」 ("Wang Yun at once invited Shisun Rui and Huang Wan to confer") and 「以天子密诏付吕布」 ("give Lü Bu a secret imperial edict").
1. Go to **Shisun Rui** and **Huang Wan** and bring them to the secret room. Each is a **go problem**; it's dangerous to sound out a minister after Zhang Wen.
2. The plan (shown): lure Dong Zhuo from Meiwu with a false edict, and hide armed men inside the palace gate.
3. Go to the palace for the **emperor's secret edict** (*Gained: secret edict*) and deliver it to Lü Bu.
4. **Recruiting Li Su.** He's resentful because Dong Zhuo never promoted him. Lü Bu: 「昔日公说布，使杀丁建阳而投董卓…」 ("Once you talked me into killing Ding Yuan and joining Dong Zhuo..."). **Contest** on Li Su's answer; he breaks an arrow as his oath.

*The echo:* the man who bribed Lü Bu with Red Hare in chapter 3 now carries the bait to Dong Zhuo.

### A13 · Meiwu and the road of omens *(Li Su, playing the villain-turned-conspirator)* · Contest, then a route
**Handoff** at the arrow oath.
1. **Meiwu.** **Contest:** the lie. 「天子…欲会文武于未央殿，议将禅位于太师」 ("The emperor wishes to meet his officials in the Weiyang Hall and offer you the throne"), and 「王司徒已命人筑受禅台」 ("Minister Wang is already building the platform for the abdication"). Dong Zhuo: 「吾夜梦一龙罩身」 ("Last night I dreamed a dragon wrapped my body").
   - Dong Zhuo's ninety-year-old mother: 「吾近日肉颤心惊，恐非吉兆。」 ("My flesh has been trembling and my heart starting lately; it isn't a good sign.") Shown as a scene.
   - Diaochan, who knows the plan, pretends to be overjoyed when promised the rank of consort. A short cameo.
2. **The road to Chang'an is a route with omen stops, each a go problem where Li Su explains the omen away.** All three are in the novel, in this order:
   - The **wheel breaks**: 「弃旧换新」 ("discard the old for the new").
   - The **horse snaps its bridle**: the same reading.
   - **Wind and fog** (light: storm): 「红光紫雾，以壮天威」 ("red light and purple mist, to add to Heaven's majesty").
3. **Night outside the city:** children sing 「千里草，何青青！十日上，不得生！」 ("Grass of a thousand li, so green! Ten days up, it will not live!"). 千 + 里 + 草 make up 董, and 十 + 日 + 卜 make up 卓: Dong Zhuo's name. Li Su: "It means the Liu clan falls and the Dong clan rises." This is the last omen problem.
4. **Morning:** a Taoist carrying a pole hung with a strip of cloth marked 口 at each end (two 口 make 吕, Lü). 「乃心恙之人也。」 ("A madman.") Li Su has him driven off. Scene.

### A14 · The North Side Gate *(Wang Yun)* · **Boss: Dong Zhuo**
**Handoff** as the carriage rolls up.
- Dong Zhuo's guards are shut outside the gate, and only about twenty carriage men go in.
- He sees Wang Yun and the officials standing with swords. 「持剑是何意？」 ("What are the swords for?") Li Su doesn't answer and pushes the carriage on.
- Wang Yun: 「反贼至此，武士何在？」 ("The traitor is here! Where are the soldiers?") The blades don't pierce Dong Zhuo's armour.
- **Boss problem.**
- 「吾儿奉先何在？」 ("Where is my son Fengxian?") Lü Bu: **「有诏讨贼！」** ("There is an edict to kill a traitor!"). The edict is read; officials and soldiers shout "Ten thousand years!"
- **Victory.** **Still:** the gate, Lü Bu with the edict raised.

### A15 · The aftermath *(Wang Yun)* · Scenes
1. Li Ru is brought in bound by his own household slaves. Scene.
2. **The market** is a place to walk, not a scene: Dong Zhuo's body with a lamp burning in its navel, and townsfolk kicking it. **Still** for the lamp; nothing graphic on the map.
3. Meiwu is raided; it's told by a messenger and a still. Lü Bu **takes Diaochan first**. The captive women are freed, and Dong Zhuo's household is executed, his mother among them; narration only.
4. **Cai Yong.** At the victory feast, a man is reported weeping over the body. It's Cai Yong, who begs to be branded and lose his feet if he can finish the history of the Han. Ma Midi pleads for him. Wang Yun refuses: 「不可令佞臣执笔于幼主左右」 ("A sycophant must not hold the brush beside the young emperor"). Cai Yong is strangled in prison.
   - **Proposed: no board.** Wang Yun isn't attempting anything; he's hardening. The novel's verdict closes it: 「王允其无后乎！」 ("Wang Yun will have no heirs!").

### A16 · Jia Xu's rumour *(the villains: Jia Xu / Li Jue)* · Legwork
**Handoff:** Li Jue's envoy comes back from Chang'an refused. 「求赦不得，各自逃生可也。」 ("No pardon. Every man run for his life.")
- Jia Xu: 「诸君若弃军单行，则一亭长能缚君矣。」 ("Leave your army and travel alone, and any village constable can tie you up.")
- **[legwork]** fills in 「遂流言于西凉州曰：『王允将欲洗荡此方之人矣。』」 ("They spread the rumour through Liangzhou: Wang Yun means to wipe out everyone here.")
  - **Spread the rumour** through the Liangzhou villages. Each village is a delivery mark, and some are a **go problem**: a headman to convince.
  - 「徒死无益，能从我反乎？」 ("Why die for nothing? Will you rebel with me?") Gather the men.
- **Gate:** the march opens once the villages are won. Join Niu Fu on the road (scene).

### A17 · Ren Valley *(Li Jue)* · Contest
- Two quick scenes:
  - Lü Bu beheads Li Su for losing a night battle. The man who once bribed him dies by his hand.
  - Hu Chi'er murders Niu Fu for his gold.
- **Contest:** Li Jue's trick at the mouth of Ren Valley. 「鸣金进兵，擂鼓收兵」: advance on the gong, withdraw on the drum, the reverse of every army's signals. Lü Bu 「欲战不得，欲止不得」 ("could neither fight nor stop"). Meanwhile Zhang Ji and Fan Chou march on Chang'an.

### A18 · Xuanping Gate *(Wang Yun)* · Scene, the end of the act
**Handoff:** Li Meng and Wang Fang open the city gates from inside, and the four armies pour in.
- Outside the Qingsuo Gate, Lü Bu begs Wang Yun to flee with him. **(b)** He refuses: 「临难苟免，吾不为也。」 ("To save myself by running in a disaster: that I will not do.") Lü Bu leaves his family behind and flees with a hundred riders.
- The emperor on the Xuanping Gate tower. Li Jue and Guo Si: 「但见王允，臣便退兵。」 ("Give us Wang Yun and we'll withdraw.")
- Wang Yun: 「王允在此！」 ("Wang Yun is here!") He leaps from the tower and curses them to the end: 「我王允今日有死而已！」 ("I, Wang Yun, have only death today!")
- **Proposed: no board.** It's his choice, and the novel has already made it.
- The act ends on the novel's own cliffhanger: Li Jue and Guo Si draw their swords and go in after the emperor. 「未知献帝性命如何」 ("What became of the emperor, we shall see.")
