# The Cao Cao arc (novel chapters 3–4, and the start of 5): design draft

Written from the Chinese original (第三回、第四回, and the start of 第五回), following `docs/plot-playbook.md`.
Everything in a beat happens in the novel unless it is marked **(invented)**. This is the design, for the user to review
before drafting. No story code has been written yet.

**How to read this:**
- **Contest:** a go problem *mid-scene*, at a real moment of risk in the novel.
- **Legwork:** a go problem *before* a scene, as the way in.
- **Scene:** plays with no board.
- **Fails:** the attempt fails as written. The player solves the board and the story fails anyway (R7, confirmed: "yes
  failed tasks are solved but still fail").

## The arc in one paragraph

The arc stands on its own: there is no Book 1, and books need not be played in order (see "Standing on its own" below).
An opening scroll sets the scene in a few lines, and then the player is Cao Cao, the one clear-headed man at a doomed
court. He tries to stop He Jin, fails, and watches Dong Zhuo take the capital. Then the
player briefly plays Dong Zhuo's side: the boy prince who stares him down, Dong Zhuo himself at the Wenming Garden, and Li
Su buying Lü Bu with Red Hare. Back as Cao Cao, the player serves the tyrant and watches the court break one man at a time.
He takes Wang Yun's Seven-Star Sword, fails to stab Dong Zhuo, bluffs his way out of the city and down the road, and is
saved by Chen Gong. Then, at Lü Boshe's farm, he becomes the man the novel will remember: 「宁教我负天下人，休教天下人负我。」
Chen Gong stands over him with a sword in the night and walks away. The arc ends at Chenliu, where Cao Cao raises the
white banner and the call to arms goes out. That call hands the book to the coalition.

## Standing on its own

> "consider that book 1 is not a thing anymore, I did away with that, our game doesnt have to have all the books in
> chronological order completely although some continuity would be cool"

- The player may arrive knowing nothing. Every person is introduced by what they do in the scene, and their name and
  title go on the first line that names them. Nothing relies on an earlier book.
- **The opening scroll** carries only what the first scene needs, in the novel's own terms:
  - 「灵帝崩」: Emperor Ling is dead, and his young son is on the throne.
  - He Jin, the Empress's brother, once a butcher, now commands the armies.
  - The Ten Attendants, the palace eunuchs, hold the court.
  - He Jin means to destroy them, and has been advised to call the frontier armies into the capital.
  - Title: the chapter couplet 「议温明董卓叱丁原　馈金珠李肃说吕布」.
- **Continuity is a bonus, kept where it is cheap:** the payoffs into the Diaochan arc (C6 below) work whichever arc is
  played first. Played first, they plant; played second, they explain.
- The Liu Bei brothers are not assumed either. The coalition arc must introduce them from scratch (the Peach Garden oath
  can be told on its scroll).

## Core principles in this arc

| Principle | Where |
|---|---|
| **C8, tone through a first impression** | The player likes Cao Cao for three acts: the only man who argues sense to He Jin, the man who puts out the palace fire, the man who risks his life with the sword. | **C4, one villain ties the cast** | Every lead's stretch is about Dong Zhuo: Cao Cao tries to stop him, the boy prince faces him, the player is him at the Wenming Garden, Li Su works for him, Cao Cao tries to kill him. |
| **C6, pay off early moments** | Planted here, paid off in the Diaochan arc (chapters 8–9): **(1)** Lü Bu kills his first adoptive father for Red Hare and **pearls** (明珠数十颗); in A3 Wang Yun buys him with pearls again. **(2)** Lü Bu shouts 「吾堂堂丈夫，安肯为汝子乎！」 at Ding Yuan, then kneels to Dong Zhuo as his son; in A14 Dong Zhuo dies calling 「吾儿奉先何在？」. **(3)** Li Su's first errand for Dong Zhuo, and his last (A13). **(4)** Cai Yong is forced to court under threat and promoted three times in a month: that is the gratitude he weeps for in A15c. **(5)** Wang Yun weeps at his false birthday and Cao Cao laughs that weeping won't kill Dong Zhuo; in chapter 8 Wang Yun weeps in his garden and finds a way. **(6)** The Seven-Star Sword is Wang Yun's. **Within this arc:** Cao Cao stands over a man with his back turned and a sword drawn (c16). | **C1, leave gaps** | Dong Zhuo is not introduced. He is a dust cloud, then banners, then a man who dismounts for a nine-year-old. The player learns the Lü Boshe household's real intent (the pig) only after the killing, as Cao Cao does. |
| **C7, scenes around the player** | c1: the council is already arguing when Cao Cao walks in. c13: carts of heads roll in through the gate while Cao Cao walks the street. c17: the jailers are sent to Cao Cao's lodging while he is still riding for the gate. |
| **C5, handoffs at meetings** | Each handoff below happens where both characters are on screen, except two "Meanwhile" cuts to a different place (marked). |

## Whom we follow

> **Cao Cao** (c1–c3) → *Meanwhile, Beimang* → **the Prince of Chenliu** (c4–c5) → (Dong Zhuo dismounts before the
> prince) → **Dong Zhuo** (c6–c7) → (Li Su steps forward in the tent: "I can bring him to you") → **Li Su** (c8–c10) →
> (Li Su leads Lü Bu into the banquet hall, where Cao Cao sits among the officials) → **Cao Cao** (c11–c20) → (at the inn,
> Chen Gong lies awake beside him) → **Chen Gong** (c21) → *the next morning, Cao Cao wakes alone* → **Cao Cao** (c22–c23)

Seven leads' stretches, six handoffs. The Prince of Chenliu is the future Emperor Xian, nine years old here. The player
meets him again as the frightened emperor of the Diaochan arc.

**Why these leads:**
- **The prince** acts in the novel: he leads his brother out of the reeds, follows the fireflies and faces Dong Zhuo.
  Cao Cao is elsewhere (organising the search).
- **Dong Zhuo** is the doer at the Wenming Garden, and the user allowed playing the villain. Playing him for two beats
  lets the player feel what he wants ("吾若得此人，何虑天下哉？"), which is what Li Su then supplies.
- **Li Su** is the doer of the bribe, the novel's most fully staged villain task. He is also the Diaochan arc's Li Su,
  so the player plays the same man twice.
- **Chen Gong's** sword in the night is the novel's camera, and it mirrors Cao Cao's own sword over Dong Zhuo.

## The beats

### Part 1: The capital falls (chapter 3)

#### C1 · "A Jailer Would Be Enough" *(Cao Cao)* · Scene
**Place:** He Jin's residence, the council already arguing as Cao Cao walks in (C7).
- Cao Cao: 「若欲治罪，当除元恶，但付一狱吏足矣，何必纷纷召外兵乎？」 He Jin: 「孟德亦怀私意耶？」 Cao Cao, leaving:
  「乱天下者，必进也。」
- Narration: the secret edicts go out by night; Dong Zhuo marches; Zheng Tai and Lu Zhi warn and resign.
**No board:** it introduces Cao Cao, and the danger, in one scene (C2: the one fact is "He Jin is about to let a wolf in").
Cao Cao is the voice of sense, and the player starts on his side (C8). His first failure is the next beat.
- His first line names him: Cao Cao, Colonel of the Army (典军校尉).

#### C2 · The Jiade Gate *(Cao Cao)* · Contest, **fails**
**Place:** outside Changle Palace.
- Chen Lin warns He Jin the summons is a trap. Cao Cao: 「先召十常侍出，然后可入。」 He Jin laughs: 「此小儿之见也。」
- **Board 1**, posed after He Jin laughs: *"Keep He Jin out of the palace."* (Cao Cao, fails.)
- He Jin goes in alone. His head is thrown over the wall. Yuan Shao: 「阉官谋杀大臣！」 Wu Kuang sets the fire.

#### C3 · The Palace Burns *(Cao Cao)* · Legwork
**Place:** the palace at night, on fire.
- The novel's one sentence: 「曹操一面救灭宫中之火，请何太后权摄大事，遣兵追袭张让等，寻觅少帝。」
- **Legwork:** put out the fire and send out the search. **Board 1 is posed before the scene:** *"Get the fire under control
  and find the Emperor."* Road challengers on the walk through the palace (below).
- Scene: the Empress Dowager is safe (Lu Zhi saved her), but the Emperor and the prince are gone.
- Ends: *Meanwhile, on Beimang hill.*

#### C4 · The Fireflies *(the Prince of Chenliu)* · Legwork
**Place:** Beimang, the riverbank at the fourth watch.
- The brothers hide in the reeds, hungry and wet with dew, crying with their hands over their mouths. The prince:
  「此间不可久恋，须别寻活路。」 They tie their clothes together and climb the bank into the thorns.
- **Board 1, posed before the scene:** *"Find a way out of the dark."*
- Reward scene: fireflies in their hundreds light the path. The prince: 「此天助我兄弟也！」 They follow it to Cui Yi's
  haystack, where the farmer has dreamed of two red suns falling.
- Engine idea: the fireflies *are* the route light, a glowing path to Cui Yi's farm (it reuses Integration's first-visit
  route light, styled as fireflies).

#### C5 · "Have You Come to Protect Us, or to Seize Us?" *(the Prince of Chenliu)* · Contest
**Place:** the road back from Beimang.
- Min Gong arrives with Duan Gui's head on his saddle. The ministers meet the carriage, Wang Yun among them (his first
  appearance). Then: 「忽见旌旗蔽日，尘土遮天」. The Emperor trembles and can't speak.
- **Board 1**, posed after Dong Zhuo's 「天子何在？」: *"Answer for your brother."* (the prince)
- The prince: 「汝来保驾耶？汝来劫驾耶？」「既来保驾，天子在此，何不下马？」 Dong Zhuo dismounts and bows by the road.
  Narration: 「卓暗奇之，已怀废立之意。」 The Imperial Seal is found missing (planted for the coalition arc).
- **Handoff:** Dong Zhuo, on the road with the prince → **Dong Zhuo**.

### Part 2: Red Hare (chapter 3)

#### C6 · The Wenming Garden *(Dong Zhuo)* · Contest, **fails**
**Place:** the Wenming Garden, a banquet. Dong Zhuo arrives last, wearing his sword.
- Li Ru's plan: 「有不从者斩之」.
- **Board 1**, posed as Dong Zhuo stops the music: *"Put the deposition to the court."* (Dong Zhuo, fails.)
- Ding Yuan pushes back his table: 「汝欲为篡逆耶？」 Dong Zhuo: 「顺我者生，逆我者死！」 He draws his sword. Li Ru sees
  the man behind Ding Yuan, with the halberd and the furious eyes, and stops him. Lu Zhi refuses too. Wang Yun: 「废立之事，
  不可酒后相商。」
- A rider gallops outside the garden gate with a halberd. 「此丁原义儿：姓吕，名布，字奉先者也。主公且须避之。」 Dong
  Zhuo hides in the garden.

#### C7 · Lü Bu at the Front *(Dong Zhuo)* · Scene
**Place:** the field outside the city, then Dong Zhuo's camp 30 li back.
- Ding Yuan's challenge; Lü Bu in his gold hair-crown and hundred-flower robe charges straight at Dong Zhuo; the rout.
- In the tent: 「吾观吕布非常人也。吾若得此人，何虑天下哉？」 Li Su steps forward: he and Lü Bu are from the same county,
  and Lü Bu 「勇而无谋，见利忘义」. He asks for Red Hare. Li Ru: 「主公欲取天下，何惜一马？」
- **Handoff:** at that meeting → **Li Su**.
**No board:** a rout can't be won, and the scene's job is to make the player want Lü Bu as Dong Zhuo does.

#### C8 · The Gifts *(Li Su)* · Legwork, no board
**Place:** Dong Zhuo's camp.
- Collect the gifts: **Red Hare** from the stables (led on a halter behind Li Su), and **a thousand taels of gold, some
  dozens of bright pearls and a jade belt** from the paymaster.
- A delivery errand with items, no board. The weight is in the next beat.

#### C9 · An Old Friend *(Li Su)* · Contest, three boards
**Place:** the road to Lü Bu's camp, then Lü Bu's tent.
- On the road: 「伏路军人围住」. The ambush pickets are a **blocking challenger** (the novel's own).
- Red Hare is led in. Still of the horse. The verse 「奔腾千里荡尘埃……火龙飞下九天来」.
- Over wine, three turns, each a board, rising in difficulty:
  1. **Board 1**, posed before the provocation: *"Make him ashamed of his master."* 「令尊却常会来。」 「先父弃世多年。」
     「非也，某说今日丁刺史耳。」
  2. **Board 2**, posed before the proverb: *"Show him a better master."* 「良禽择木而栖，贤臣择主而事。」
  3. **Board 3**, posed before the gold: *"Lay out the gold."* The gold, pearls and jade belt: 「此是董公久慕大名，特令某将此
     奉献。赤兔马亦董公所赠也。」 Lü Bu, after a long silence: 「吾欲杀丁原，引军归董卓，何如？」
- **Echo:** this is the same three-turn persuasion Wang Yun uses on the ridge in A11.

#### C10 · The Second Watch *(Li Su)* · Scene
**Place:** Ding Yuan's tent, then Dong Zhuo's camp.
- Night. Ding Yuan reading by a candle: 「吾儿来有何事故？」 Lü Bu: 「吾堂堂丈夫，安肯为汝子乎！」 (the killing offscreen;
  the candle goes out).
- Morning: Lü Bu brings the head to Li Su. Li Su leads him to Dong Zhuo, who bows first: 「卓今得将军，如旱苗之得甘雨也。」
  Lü Bu kneels: 「公若不弃，布请拜为义父。」
- Li Su walks Lü Bu into the Secretariat banquet, where the officials sit. **Handoff:** → **Cao Cao**, seated among them.
**No board:** the killing is Lü Bu's, and settled.

### Part 3: The Seven-Star Sword (chapter 4)

#### C11 · "Your Sword Is Sharp, and So Is Mine" *(Cao Cao)* · Scene
**Place:** the Secretariat banquet, Lü Bu's thousand armoured men around the walls.
- Dong Zhuo draws his sword and announces the deposition. Yuan Shao: 「汝剑利，吾剑未尝不利！」 Li Ru stops the killing.
  Yuan Shao walks out, hangs his seal of office on the East Gate and rides for Jizhou.
- Cao Cao watches a man draw a sword on Dong Zhuo and walk out alive (planted for c17). Yuan Shao is planted for the
  coalition arc.

#### C12 · The Jiade Hall *(Cao Cao)* · Scene
**Place:** Jiade Hall, the first day of the ninth month.
- Li Ru reads the deposition. The boy Emperor is helped down, his seal ribbons untied; he kneels facing north. Ding Guan
  strikes at Dong Zhuo with his ivory tablet, 「吾当以颈血溅之！」, and is taken out cursing.
- The Prince of Chenliu, now Emperor Xian, takes the throne at nine.
- After: Li Ru recommends Cai Yong. Cai Yong refuses, and Dong Zhuo sends: 「如不来，当灭汝族。」 He comes, and is promoted
  three times in a month. (Plants A12y and Cai Yong's death.)

#### C13 · Luoyang under Dong Zhuo *(Cao Cao)* · Scene, walked
**Place:** Luoyang's streets and the city gate.
- Cao Cao walks the city in Dong Zhuo's service (the novel: 「操屈身以事卓者，实欲乘间图之」). Townsfolk carry the colour:
  - A palace servant has heard the deposed Emperor's poem about the swallows from the tower of Yong'an Palace. Later
    the same servant says the Prince of Hongnong has died.
  - At the gate, the carts come back from Yangcheng with heads hung beneath them, "a great victory over bandits",
    and the heads are burned under the gate (C7, it happens around the player).
- **The poisoning of the young Emperor is narrated over a still**, not played (his poem, Li Ru's cup, the Empress thrown
  from the tower, the Tang consort). See open question 3.

#### C14 · Wu Fu *(Cao Cao)* · Scene
**Place:** the palace gallery (阁下).
- Wu Fu, with armour under his robes and a short knife, stabs at Dong Zhuo, who seizes his arms; Lü Bu throws him down.
  「汝非吾君，吾非汝臣，何反之有？」 He dies cursing. From then on Dong Zhuo goes everywhere with armed guards.
- A rehearsal: the player sees a hidden-knife attempt fail before Cao Cao makes his own.

#### C15 · The False Birthday *(Cao Cao)* · Contest
**Place:** Wang Yun's Luoyang house, the rear hall.
- Yuan Shao's secret letter reaches Wang Yun. Wang Yun calls the ministers to "his birthday". After a few rounds he weeps,
  and they all weep. Cao Cao claps and laughs: 「满朝公卿，夜哭到明，明哭到夜，焉能哭死董卓耶？」
- **Board 1**, posed after Wang Yun's 「汝祖宗亦食禄汉朝，今不思报国而反笑耶？」: *"Ask for the Seven-Star Sword."*
- Cao Cao: 「闻司徒有七星宝刀一口，愿借与操入相府刺杀之，虽死不恨！」 Wang Yun pours for him; he pours it out as an
  oath. **Gained: the Seven-Star Sword.**

#### C16 · The Mirror *(Cao Cao)* · Contest, two boards (boss)
**Place:** the Chancellor's residence, the small pavilion.
- 「孟德来何迟？」「马羸行迟耳。」 Dong Zhuo sends Lü Bu to choose him a horse. Cao Cao: 「此贼合死！」 but fears Dong Zhuo's
  strength. Dong Zhuo, too fat to sit long, lies down facing the wall.
- **Board 1**, posed as Dong Zhuo turns to the wall: *"Strike while his back is turned."* (**fails**)
- Cao Cao draws. Dong Zhuo looks up into the dressing mirror and sees him. 「孟德何为？」 Lü Bu's horse is at the door.
- **Board 2**, posed on 「孟德何为？」: *"Turn the blade into a gift."* (succeeds)
- Cao Cao kneels: 「操有宝刀一口，献上恩相。」 The sword goes to Lü Bu. 「借愿试一骑。」 He rides out of the gate toward the
  south-east. **Given: the Seven-Star Sword (to Dong Zhuo, then Lü Bu).**
- Boss staging (`["boss", "dongzhuo"]`).

#### C17 · The East Gate *(Cao Cao)* · Legwork
**Place:** Luoyang's streets to the East Gate.
- While Cao Cao rides (C7, cut in as narration): Lü Bu says he looked like an assassin. Li Ru's test: send for him, and
  see if he comes. Four jailers go to his lodging.
- **Board 1, posed before the scene:** *"Get through the East Gate."*
- At the gate: 「丞相差我有紧急公事。」 He is through.
- Narration: the warrants go out with his portrait. 「擒献者，赏千金，封万户侯；窝藏者同罪。」

### Part 4: The road east (chapters 4–5)

#### C18 · Zhongmou *(Cao Cao)* · Contest, **fails**
**Place:** the pass at Zhongmou.
- Road challengers on the way: post stations with his portrait (below).
- The pass guards take him to the magistrate.
- **Board 1**, posed as the magistrate studies his face: *"Pass for a merchant named Huangfu."* (**fails**)
- 「吾前在洛阳求官时，曾认得汝是曹操，如何隐讳？」 Jailed, to be sent to the capital for the reward.

#### C19 · The Back Courtyard *(Cao Cao)* · Contest
**Place:** the county jail's back courtyard, midnight.
- The magistrate questions him alone: 「我闻丞相待汝不薄，何故自取其祸？」 Cao Cao: 「燕雀安知鸿鹄志哉！」
- **Board 1**, posed after 「汝休小觑我。我非俗吏，奈未遇其主耳。」: *"Tell him why."*
- 「吾祖宗世食汉禄，若不思报国，与禽兽何异？……吾将归乡里，发矫诏，召天下诸侯兴兵共诛董卓。」 The magistrate unties him
  with his own hands: 「公真天下忠义之士也！」 He is Chen Gong, and he leaves his post to follow. **Party: Cao Cao, Chen
  Gong.**

#### C20 · Lü Boshe *(Cao Cao, with Chen Gong)* · Scene, walked
**Place:** Chenggao, dusk; Lü Boshe's farm.
- Lü Boshe, Cao Cao's father's sworn brother, welcomes them, bows to Chen Gong for saving the family, and rides off on
  his donkey to buy wine in the west village.
- **Walked:** the sound of knives being sharpened behind the house. The player, as Cao Cao, creeps to the back of the
  thatched hall and hears: 「缚而杀之，何如？」 (Stealth: walk; no board.)
- 「今若不先下手，必遭擒获。」 The killing happens offscreen: the screen goes dark, with sound. Eight people are dead.
  In the kitchen: a pig, tied up for the slaughter. Still. Chen Gong: 「孟德心多，误杀好人矣！」
- On the road, two li on: Lü Boshe comes back with two jars of wine on his saddle and fruit in his hand. 「吾已分付家人宰
  一猪相款。」 Cao Cao rides on, then turns back: 「此来者何人？」 Lü Boshe looks round, and Cao Cao cuts him down.
- Chen Gong: 「知而故杀，大不义也！」 Cao Cao: **「宁教我负天下人，休教天下人负我。」** Chen Gong says nothing.
**No board:** a problem here would make the player "win" a massacre (R4).

#### C21 · The Inn *(Chen Gong)* · Scene
**Place:** a roadside inn, moonlight.
- Cao Cao sleeps first. **Handoff:** → **Chen Gong**, lying awake. The player walks him to Cao Cao's bed. He draws his
  sword: 「我将谓曹操是好人，弃官跟他；原来是个狠心之徒！今日留之，必为后患。」
- He stops: 「我为国家跟他到此，杀之不义。不若弃而他往。」 He sheathes it, mounts and leaves for Dongjun before dawn.
- The closing couplet of chapter 4: 「设心狠毒非良士，操卓原来一路人。」
- **Handoff:** *the next morning* → **Cao Cao** wakes alone: 「此人见我说了这两句，疑我不仁，弃我而去；吾当急行，不可久留。」
**No board:** Chen Gong's choice is his own conscience, and the novel settles it.

### Part 5: Chenliu (chapter 5)

#### C22 · Wei Hong *(Cao Cao)* · Legwork
**Place:** Chenliu: the Cao family house, then Wei Hong's mansion.
- His father: 「资少恐不成事。此间有孝廉卫弘，疏财仗义，其家巨富；若得相助，事可图矣。」
- **Board 1, posed before the scene:** *"Win over Wei Hong."*
- At the banquet, Wei Hong: 「吾有是心久矣，恨未遇英雄耳。」 **Gained: Wei Hong's fortune.**

#### C23 · The White Banner *(Cao Cao)* · Scene, walked
**Place:** the village drilling ground.
- Deliver Wei Hong's fortune to the drilling ground. The white banner goes up: 「忠义」.
- **Walked:** volunteers gather (`crowd`), then the named officers arrive on their own, as in the novel: Yue Jin and Li
  Dian; Xiahou Dun (who killed a man for insulting his teacher) and Xiahou Yuan, with a thousand men each; Cao Ren and Cao
  Hong. No boards: the novel gives them no obstacle.
- Yuan Shao comes from Bohai with 30,000 men. Cao Cao writes the call to arms; it is read in full:
