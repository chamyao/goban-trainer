# 미생 Season 1, 착수0 to 33수: episode by episode

These notes go with `misaeng-research.md` (sections 2.0 and 2.1). They cover the first part of the series ("1부"), from 착수0 to 33수, which ends with the hiring results and the first-morning memorial.

## How to read this

**Confidence tags**
- **[V]** I read it in a source; the URL is given.
- **[V-wiki]** It is namu.wiki text, read through the mirror (namu.moe/w/미생(웹툰)/시즌1) or a page saved earlier in `dl/` and `dl2/`.
- **[V-cmt]** I inferred it from the Daum best comments for that episode. The event itself is solid; the wording is the reader's.
- **[?]** It is a guess, or the readers disagree, or I could not place it.

**Comment source.** For episode NNN, the comments are at `C-NNN` = https://niceaji.github.io/misaeng/comment/NNN.txt. Local copies are in `scratchpad/dl3/c/NNN.txt`.

**Dates.** Each date is the episode's upload date (`dateCreated`) in https://niceaji.github.io/misaeng/javascripts/episodes.js. The trailer (예고편) went up on 2012-01-17. New episodes came out every Tuesday and Friday.

**Move commentary.** Each episode opens with one move of game 5 of the 1st Ing Cup final (Nie Weiping, White, against Cho Hunhyun, Black). The move number equals the episode number. Several readers explained the moves in the comments:
- **허허허** from 18수 onward (namu, `dl/moe_webtoon.txt`: "18화부터 허허허라는 독자가…").
- **항해** for 1–17.
- Others in single episodes.

Where a reader tied a move to the plot, the entry says so.

**What I could not find.** Every Korean blog search failed. Naver returns only namu snippets (its blog tab loads through JavaScript), Bing returns irrelevant results, DuckDuckGo blocks the request, and the namu.moe pages for 한석율, 오상식 and 김동식 return 404. Everything below therefore comes from:
- the best comments for 000–033 (all read),
- namu's 시즌1 and character pages (saved earlier),
- the Yes24 blurbs for volumes 1 and 2, with reader reviews, in `dl4/g7437073.txt` and `dl4/g7437078.txt`,
- the 2013 Media Today column (`dl4/mt.html`, https://www.mediatoday.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=108068).

**The drama is a different story.** Many details people "know" come from the 2014 tvN drama, for example 한석율 selling a notebook and fabric while Jang uses 오상식's slippers, or the 딱풀/기안서 version of the glue incident. I use a drama detail only when webtoon comments confirm it.

## 착수0 — 2012-01-20
**Setting.** Jang's past: the baduk world, then the moment he gives up.

**People.**
- 장그래, as a child prodigy and then as a failed trainee
- his parents

**Events.**
1. A recap of Jang's baduk life. His father's company went bankrupt and the father poured everything into him. His mother clipped Lee Chang-ho's and Lee Sedol's rankings and prize money. [V, Media Today]
2. He fails to turn pro. The others in his trainee cohort pass. [V, Media Today]
3. The core monologue rejects every excuse. It was not lack of talent, not losing by half a point every time, not juggling baduk with part-time jobs, not parents who could not give pocket money. It was not his father's death and his mother being bedridden. "That would hurt too much," so he settles on "I just didn't try hard enough." [V, Media Today, which quotes it verbatim]
4. "Only the world in my eyes has changed": the blue sky and green leaves have turned grey. [V, Media Today]
5. He throws his baduk stones away a few at a time, not all at once. The last panel shows him crying, and the pupil of his eye is drawn as a baduk stone. [V-cmt, C-000, reader "wawssw"]
6. A reader writes that only after failing does he see his parents' wrinkled faces. [V-cmt, C-000]
7. A reader saw a hint in the nose in the last panel that the whole monologue is a lie to himself. [?, C-000]

**Key lines.**
- "열심히 안 한 것은 아니지만 열심히 안 해서인 걸로 생각하겠다." — "It's not that I didn't work hard, but I'll tell myself it's because I didn't." [V, Media Today]
- "난 열심히 하지 않아서 버려진 것 뿐이다." — "I was just thrown away because I didn't try hard enough." [V, Media Today]

**Move.**
- Readers note the series is numbered from 0.
- Reader "낭만소쿠리" (C-003) identifies the game as 145 moves, Black winning by resignation, and so predicts 145 episodes.

**Key line.** "그래도 바둑이니까. 내 바둑이니까." — "Still, it's baduk. My baduk." Cho Chikun's saying, recalled by Jang and later turned into "내 일이니까, 나에게 허락된 세상이니까." [V, Media Today; the episode it appears in is unknown ?]

## 13수 — 2012-03-06: the waybill
**Setting.** Sales Team 3's desks, then the lobby.

**People.**
- Jang
- **김석호**, an intern in 고 과장's team. Married early as the eldest grandson (장손), with a baby. Skilled at translation and interpreting.
- a **국장** (director)
- 오 과장
- 김 대리
- all the interns

**Events.** [V-wiki, `dl/moe_s1.txt` §김석호 intern; V-cmt C-013]
1. Jang is away from his desk. 김석호 comes over and borrows Sales Team 3's glue stick (딱풀). His papers stick to Team 3's **운송장** (waybill), which carries Team 3's 결재 (approval) details. Jang was supposed to dispose of it.
2. Hurrying, 김석호 notices the stuck sheet, tosses it on a desk with "이것 좀 버려 달라" ("throw this away for me, please") and walks off. It ends up on the **lobby floor**.
3. The 국장 finds it, storms into Team 3, and erupts about document security. A reader quotes his "heartfelt encouragement": "야. 잘하자." ("Hey. Do better.")
4. All the interns get a harsh group punishment (기합). Everyone assumes it was Jang's mistake.
5. 오 과장 does not take his anger out on Jang. He only says "치우자" ("let's clean it up").
6. Readers contrast this with 김석호, whose senior teaches him nothing and leaves him to cadge supplies.

**Author's note in the comments.** 윤태호 himself commented: "김석호는 전에 나온 한석율(개벽이)와 다른 사람입니다. 닮게 그려 죄송해요." ("김석호 is a different person from 한석율. Sorry for drawing them alike.") [V, C-013]
