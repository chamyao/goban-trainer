# Lü Bu's fall (novel chapters 11–19): design, building

Written from the Chinese original (第十一回 to 第十九回), following `docs/plot-playbook.md`. Every beat happens in the
novel unless it is marked **(invented)**. Being built as its own book (n=14 suggested, book 3, after the Diaochan arc).

## The narrative (Part 0)

**The house of Lü Bu, and the two voices that pull at it.** Lü Bu has a halberd, Red Hare, and no judgment. **Chen Gong**
gives him counsel throughout. Some of it is taken (seizing Xuzhou in ch. 14; sending the bride off at once in ch. 16,
which Chen Gui then undoes), but his most important plans are refused, above all at the end in Xiapi. **The women of his
house** decide two turning points in the novel's own words. Lady Yan settles their daughter's marriage (「布意遂決」), though
that match is broken off days later. At Xiapi, after Lady Yan's first plea leaves him undecided for three days
(「布躊躇未決，三日不出」), her second plea and Diaochan's 「將軍與妾作主，勿輕騎自出」 end Chen Gong's last plan, together with
Lü Bu's own boast 「吾有畫戟、赤兔馬，誰敢近我？」. The novel's verdict is in its closing poem, 「戀妻不納陳宮諫」, and in Song Xian's
charge, 「聽妻妾言，不聽將計」. The arc ends at White Gate Tower, where Chen Gong chooses death over Cao Cao, and Liu Bei asks
Cao Cao: 「公不見丁建陽、董卓之事乎？」

(Corrected after the user asked whether this was all true to the novel. An earlier draft overstated it: "none is taken",
"the women win every time". The user's direction: "I just want you to emphasize if theres opportunity to highlight the
female characters, not force".)

**Why it's a good arc:**
- **Stakes in a centre:** a family under siege, a daughter Lü Bu loves more than anything (「布最鍾愛」), a wife he
  abandoned once already.
- **Women at the turning points**, in the novel's own words. Lady Yan settles the marriage and pleads twice at Xiapi; Diaochan speaks last before he gives up the plan.
- **It pays off both arcs already built:**
  - Chen Gong spared Cao Cao at the inn (Cao Cao arc). Here he refuses Cao Cao and walks down the stairs to die.
  - Lü Bu killed Ding Yuan and Dong Zhuo (both arcs). Here Liu Bei names them both.
  - Diaochan returns.
  - Lü Bu looks in a mirror during the flood (「一旦取鏡自照」), as Dong Zhuo did in the Cao Cao arc's mirror scene.

## The new mechanic (Part 0): the flood, and the horse that crosses water

(Mechanics needn't involve go: "the mechanics dont have to fit w go btw". The flood is a map mechanic in its own right.)

> "did you introduce any new game mechanics? … something like that"

**The flood of Xiapi.** Cao Cao breaks the dykes of the Yi and Si rivers: 「只剩得東門無水；其餘各門，都被水淹」.
- **The map floods in stages** across the Xiapi beats. Streets and gates go under, routes close, and the east gate is
  the only way out.
- **Red Hare crosses water.** Lü Bu boasts: 「吾有赤免馬，渡水如平地」. While Lü Bu rides Red Hare, he can cross flooded
  tiles that stop everyone else. When Hou Cheng steals Red Hare, that power is gone, and the water closes in.
- **Stealing Red Hare** is the turn. The player plays Hou Cheng for one beat: into the stables at night, out by the east
  gate, where Wei Xu "chases" him for show (「卻佯作追趕之狀」).

**Two smaller mechanics** from the same chapters, if wanted:
- **Carrying the daughter** (the breakout, 19): 「以綿纏身，用甲包裏，負於背上…只恐有傷，不敢衝突重圍」. With her on his
  back, Lü Bu can't fight, so a catch is not a board but a turn-back. It fails, as in the novel (R7).
- **The chase, reversed** (reusing the Cao Cao arc's chase): Zhang Liao rides 30 li to bring the bride back
  (「三十里之外將女搶歸」), and Chen Gong rides down Liu Bei's courier on a hunting day. This time the player is the
  chaser.

**A touch** (like the fireflies): the arrow letters. Chen Deng shoots three letters down from Xiao Pass, and Cao Cao
shoots placards into Xiapi. A letter could fly over the wall and land where the player picks it up.

## The beats (outline)

**Opening scroll:** what lies between. Li Jue took Chang'an; Lü Bu fled with a hundred riders and left his family behind (「呂布只得棄卻家小，引百餘騎飛奔出關」); he and Chen Gong took Yanzhou while
Cao Cao was away, and lost it at Puyang, where Chen Gong's fire trap almost killed Cao Cao (chapters 11–12 in a few lines).

### Prologue · Chang'an (ch. 9–11)
- **L0 · Left Behind** *(Lady Yan)*: Chang'an under Li Jue and Guo Si. Lady Yan and her daughter, left behind when Lü
  Bu fled, are hidden in Pang Shu's house. Walk them out of the city past Li Jue's patrols: stealth, which reuses the
  Diaochan arc's watchers. **(invented staging of 「龐舒在長安城中，私藏呂布妻小，送還呂布」; the novel later tells us
  Li Jue executed Pang Shu for it.)** Board: legwork. Ends with the reunion and the scroll's jump to Xuzhou.

### Part 1 · Xuzhou (ch. 13–14)
- **L1 · A Guest at Xuzhou** *(Lü Bu)*: Liu Bei takes him in. At the feast Lü Bu has his wife and daughter come out and
  bow (「布令妻女出拜玄德」); he calls Liu Bei 賢弟, Zhang Fei explodes, and he is sent to Xiaopei. Contest: win Liu Bei
  over (**fails**, as written).
- **L2 · Two Tigers** *(Lü Bu)*: Liu Bei shows him Cao Cao's secret letter ordering his death. Scene.
- **L3 · The Night Gate** *(Lü Bu)*: Cao Bao's letter; a clear moonlit night; 「劉使君有機密使人至」 at the gate. Legwork
  board before: get the gate opened. Zhang Fei escapes drunk; Lü Bu sets a guard on Liu Bei's house so no one troubles
  his wives.

### Part 2 · The halberd and the bride (ch. 15–16)
- **L4 · The Halberd at the Camp Gate** *(Lü Bu)*: Ji Ling and Liu Bei both at his banquet. 「吾若一箭射中戟上小枝，你兩家
  罷兵」. Contest, one board: the shot.
- **L5 · A Match for a Princess** *(Lady Yan)*: Han Yin proposes the marriage. 「布入謀於妻嚴氏」. Contest: Lady Yan decides
  (「若成大事，則吾女有后妃之望」). Succeeds.
- **L6 · The Condolence Call** *(Lü Bu)*: the bride leaves to drums and music; Chen Gui arrives 「聞將軍死，故特來弔喪」, and
  Lü Bu sees the trap. Scene.
- **L7 · Thirty Li** *(Zhang Liao)*: the chase, reversed. Ride down the bridal party and bring her home.

### Part 3 · Chen Gong (ch. 16–18)
- **L8 · The Hawk** *(Lü Bu)*: Chen Deng's 「吾待溫侯，如養鷹耳」 talks Lü Bu out of killing him. Scene.
- **L9 · The Hunt** *(Chen Gong)*: his warning ignored (「忠言不入，吾輩必受殃矣」), he rides out hunting, spots a
  courier and rides him down by a side path. The chase, reversed; the search finds Liu Bei's letter to Cao Cao.

### Part 4 · Xiapi (ch. 19)
- **L10 · The Family Moves** *(Lü Bu)*: Chen Deng persuades him to move his wife, children and grain to Xiapi
  (「吾當並妻小移去」). Escort the household on the road. Scene with a walk.
- **L11 · Three Letters** *(Chen Deng)*: at Xiao Pass he shoots three letters down to Cao's camp (the arrow-letter
  touch), then lights the fire, and Chen Gong's and Lü Bu's men kill each other in the dark. Legwork board: get the
  letters out unseen.
- **L12 · Locked Out** *(Lü Bu)*: Mi Zhu on Xuzhou's wall, Cao Ren's flags on Xiaopei's. Scene.
- **L13 · The Counsel** *(Chen Gong → Lady Yan → Diaochan)*: the centrepiece. Three boards, three speakers:
  1. **Chen Gong**: 「此乃犄角之勢也」. The two-position plan. Solved, and **fails** (Lady Yan: 「君委全城，捐妻子…」).
  2. **Lady Yan**: three days later Chen Gong tries again (cut the grain road), and she weeps: 「妾昔在長安，已為將軍所棄，
     幸賴龐舒私藏妾身…」. Succeeds. (Pays off L0.)
  3. **Diaochan**: 「將軍與妾作主，勿輕騎自出」. Succeeds. Lü Bu: 「吾有畫戟、赤兔馬，誰敢近我？」 Chen Gong: 「吾等死無葬身之地矣！」
- **L14 · Through the Lines** *(Lü Bu)*: the daughter on his back, at the hour the diviner chose. The carry mechanic; he
  turns back (**fails**, as written).
- **L15 · The Flood** *(Lü Bu)*: the dykes broken; the map floods in stages; Red Hare crosses. The mirror: 「吾被酒色傷矣！」,
  then the ban on wine.
- **L16 · Fifty Lashes** *(Lü Bu)*: Hou Cheng's wine; the flogging; the officers' plot. Scene.
- **L17 · Red Hare** *(Hou Cheng)*: steal Red Hare at night and ride out of the east gate. Stealth; board as legwork.
- **L18 · Asleep on the Gate Tower** *(Lü Bu)*: the white flags; he dozes in his chair; his halberd is stolen and he wakes
  bound. Scene.
- **L19 · White Gate Tower** *(Cao Cao)*: the boss scene, played as Cao Cao. Boards at Gao Shun's silence, at Chen Gong's
  refusal (he walks down and doesn't look back), and at Lü Bu's plea. 「公不見丁建陽、董卓之事乎？」「大耳兒！不記轅門射戟時耶？」
  Zhang Liao's 「可惜當日火不大」; the arc ends on the cliffhanger 「畢竟救張遼的是誰」.

About 20 beats. Exact boards, road challengers and stills come with the full design.

### The flood (engine and map spec)
- **Xiapi's states:** `siege` (x15–x16), `flood1` (x17: the low streets and the south and west gates under water),
  `flood2` (x18–x19: everything but a causeway to the east gate). In `flood2`, 「只剩得東門無水」.
- **Water** is impassable on foot or on an ordinary horse. A mount with `"crosses_water": true` (Red Hare) rides across it.
- **x17:** Lü Bu rides the flooded city on Red Hare to inspect the gates. The player feels the power: only he can cross.
- **x19:** Hou Cheng steals Red Hare and rides it out of the dry east gate. Riding Red Hare, he can cut across the water
  past the watchers, so the player gets to use the power one last time as the thief. Wei Xu opens the gate and "chases"
  him for show.
- **After x19:** a step removes Red Hare from Lü Bu's items (`["lose", "redhare"]`). In x20 everyone is on foot.

### Carrying the daughter (x16)
- Lü Bu's daughter rides on his back (a carry pose or follower drawn on his back). With her there he can't fight. The
  scene's walk ends at Liu Bei's camp line, where Guan Yu and Zhang Fei block the way, then Xu Huang and Xu Chu come up
  behind. He turns back (scripted, faithful).
