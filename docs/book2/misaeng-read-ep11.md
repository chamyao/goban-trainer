## 11수 (id 15572)

**1. Scenes in order**
- *Title board.* The 1st Ing Cup final, game 5: Nie Weiping (White) against Cho Hunhyun (Black). **Black 11** is marked on the lower-left side.
- *Ulsan, a fabric warehouse.* An older man in a green sweater, probably the site 부장 from ep 10, is showing Han bolts of specialty fibre.
  - He explains: it's our own technology, so we give no ground on royalties. Strength, carbonisation and density matter. But don't try to explain all that to the buyer; leave it to the experts.
  - Han: to do business abroad, shouldn't I know this cloth inside out? The man: your job is to get the original samples and documents to the buyer on the agreed date and time, with no mistakes. The buyer has his own experts; if he likes it, he buys.
  - Han: he can't open his mouth unless he's sure of himself. Sorry to bother you, but please help. The man softens ("that guy…") and says Han can buy him a soju when it's done. Han, beaming: as much as you like.
- *Phone call 1 (Han outside the plant, smoking; Jang in the Sales 3 office).* Jang has re-planned the three items and sent them by mail. Han sneers: is he writing an after-school hobby plan? At the company where Han interned, he spent **four days on one item** and wrote **20 tightly packed pages per item**. Does this look easy? Jang, flat: please check it, and if something's lacking he'll redo it.
- *Night, a lounge with leather sofas.* Jang types, alone. The page counters read 1/42, 1/40, 1/53. He talks back to himself: more pages don't make it better, and an item plan isn't a book report. He keeps going anyway, set-faced.
- *Sales 3, next day (it is Jang's team work, not the PT).*
  - Oh, red-eyed and exhausted, holds up a document Jang made. Jang should have followed the seniors' finished materials. "Will fixing it solve anything? I have to take this in and report!" The colours don't match, the text boxes are scattered everywhere, and the big and small groupings and levels are a mess. What did he do yesterday? Jang: sorry.
  - Kim covers for him: the interns probably had no time because of the PT exam. Oh: "Kim 대리, you want to play house? Really?" Kim apologises and asks Jang if he can fix it now. Jang stammers yes, yes, yes.
  - Kim's aside: answer too fast and he gets angrier, because he didn't get enough time to be angry.
  - Oh, as a parting order: shrink the boxes. Then he yawns, eyes watering.
- *Phone call 2 (Jang slips into an empty meeting room; Han in the street at Ulsan).*
  - Han: interns are staff too. Wasn't this personal work instead of company work? Look at the volume…
  - Jang: how is it? Han: well… Jang beats him to it: **"Again?"** Han, after a pause: the **second item is good**. Jang: are you sure? Han: yes, decided.
  - Jang: then that's settled. They chose the item together. Han is more experienced, so working with Jang costs him nothing, and he agreed to this item. From here, **Jang writes the PT details, his way. They share the process, but he takes no instructions.** That's what they agreed, isn't it? Han: what?
  - Jang: "And…", then, in **반말** (dropping honorifics): **how old are you, anyway?** Han, in the street: huh…? (a startled sound effect).
- *Narration over a Seoul street montage.* Baduk is at bottom a fight, a war. You step back when pressed and sometimes look for coexistence, but winners and losers are clear. He lived in that world for over ten years. A beaten soldier, but **someone raised as a competitor (승부사)**. Panels: a hall full of players, then a grim pro's face.
  - The pun: a **선수 (player) who doesn't hand over 선수 (sente)**. It is drawn over Jang on the phone in the meeting room.
- *Ending.* Han mutters an insult, roughly "this little runt…". Jang: **"Not going to say?"** To be continued.

**2. Dialogue.** About 8 captions and about 50 balloons.
- Jang: 「너 몇 살이나?」: "How old are you, anyway?" It is in 반말; the switch of speech level is the point.
- Narration: 「선수(先手)를 넘기지 않는 선수다.」: "A player who doesn't hand over sente."
- Jang: 「다시?」: "Again?" He says it himself, before Han can.

**3. Against our game (m19; end of m18/m18b)**
- **m18/m18b (ep 10's close):** consistent with ep 11. Ep 11 opens at Ulsan with Han now humble ("help me") and the site man warming to him, which pays off m18b's "I can do it!".
- **In ep 11 and right:**
  - "And. How old are you?" is correct. It should be marked as Jang **dropping honorifics** ("anyway", 반말); in English, a register shift.
  - "Not going to say?" is correct, and it is the episode's last line.
- **Wrong or misplaced:**
  - **Oh: "You're all over the place. Pick one thing and do it."** Not said. Oh is complaining about **a document Jang made for the team**: inconsistent colours, text boxes everywhere, the hierarchy a mess, "what did you do yesterday?", "shrink the boxes". "All over the place" is a fair gloss of the *document*, not of Jang. "Pick one thing" is invented. The scene also has Kim covering for him and Kim's "answer too fast" tip.
  - **Han: "Again. No. Again."** Wrong. In ep 11 Han **does not** say again. Jang pre-empts "Again?" and Han **approves the second item**. Han's real complaint at the start is the opposite: the first redo was too thin (his 20 pages, four days). The second complaint is that the 40–50-page redo is personal work, not company work. "Again!" belongs to ep 10's text (m18), so m19 repeats it.
  - **Staging.** Han and Oh are not in the room together. Han is **at Ulsan on the phone** in both calls, and the age question is asked **over the phone**, from an empty meeting room. Oh belongs to a separate office scene with Kim.
  - **Han's "..."** is a startled "huh?" and then a muttered "this little runt…" before "Not going to say?".
  - **"My heroes are disappearing"** and the newspaper clippings with his mother are **not in ep 11**. The closing narration is the war/승부사/"a player who doesn't hand over sente" passage. The heroes memory must be later (ep 12+); keep it out of m19 until it's found.
- **Missing, worth adding:**
  - Jang's take-back speech, which is the actual move. The item is chosen jointly and Han agreed; Jang writes the PT, shares progress, takes no instructions; "we agreed, didn't we?". It is the payoff of ep 9's "make it however you like" and of m17/m18's "Take sente" / "Hold your ground" dilemmas. The m19 dilemma "Take the PT back" should resolve with this speech, not with "He stops talking."
  - The all-nighter in the lounge: 42/40/53 pages, and "an item plan isn't a book report."
  - The Ulsan fibre-warehouse scene: Han out of his depth, and the soju promise.
  - The 선수/선수 narration. It is ready-made text for the Black 11 record board.
- **Black 11 (the board).** The title diagram matches our SGF (`docs/book2/misaeng-ing-cup-g5.sgf`): Black 11 is **cp (C4)**, directly on top of Black 9 (cq, C3) and solidly connected to it, beside White's D4/D3 (dp/dq). Black 7 is at **cn (C6)**, one space up the side, with White 8 at cl (C8) above it. So m19's "Black links up with the stone it played at move 7" is **imprecise**. Black 11 connects to **Black 9**, which makes the corner group solid and leaves a one-space link toward 7. A truer narration: "Black makes his corner stones solid, then turns to fight." Note that the node's `choices` for move 11 (dl, dr, dc, bp, cc, ed, ip) don't list cp. Presumably cp is the scored answer from `record`; worth a check.
