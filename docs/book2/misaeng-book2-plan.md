# Misaeng Book 2: 도전 Challenge (episodes 17–33, world 22): the plan

Status: **plan for the user's approval.** Nothing is drafted yet. Built from readers first
(`misaeng-research-ep0-33.md`: the Daum best comments for every episode, namu.wiki, the Yes24 reader reviews), the
drama where it helps (R19), and the comic (chamyao/misaeng) last, read while drafting. W = webtoon, D = drama,
(staging) / (invented) as in Book 1.

**The shape.** Two weeks before the PT, Jang watches other people's games. A gentle salesman who never pushes anyone
learns to push, and then takes the blame himself; a section chief who never asks favours has to ask one, and sees her
daughter's drawing of her. Then the PT week. Jang and Han, who want opposite things from work, are told to sell
something to the person they least want to sell to: each other. Han chokes, Jang stammers, Ahn is flawless. Jang buys
Han's field notebooks, "to sell textiles with you"; Han refuses Oh's slippers again and again, then gives in. The
results: Ahn first, Han and Baek-gi in, and Jang on a two-year contract. On the first morning Oh takes the new hires
to stand in front of a memorial for workers who lost their jobs. Quiet ending, as Book 1's.

**The theme Book 1 handed on.** "There will be dead stones in his own work too." Book 2's line: *everyone has their
own baduk*: Park's, Sun's, Han's. Book 1 said it once (m22); here Jang learns what it costs.

**Count.** 17 episodes, 18 beats (episode 33 takes two). The frame game runs Black/White 17 → 33.

## The beats

Each beat: what happens · who leads · the board · the setups it needs (R22) · where.

### Park Jong-gi, IT sales (17–20)

**b17 · 17수 · Bread on the Street** (W)
- An assistant manager from IT sales eats bread standing up on Jongno and throws it up. His resignation letter is
  in his jacket. At home: a happiness he says he can't carry.
- **The player is Park** for the cold open: walk him from the bakery to the tower (the setup for who he is: gentle,
  a "farmer" who never pushes a client). On the roof he meets Jang; Jang's offhand praise melts him; he puffs up and
  lectures the intern on sales, then invites him along to watch him handle a client (*motive* for b18).
- Board: the record, Black 17 ("to thrust hard, your feet must be planted").
- Setup planted here for later: **Sun Ji-young**, Sales Team 1's deputy head, on the roof or the floor: she never asks
  a junior for a personal favour (her rule, for b21). And **Oh's slippers**, a detail: he pads about the floor in them
  (for b31–32; Places can give his seated NPC the line).
- Places: Jongno (a bakery), the roof.

**b18 · 18수 · Through the Door** (W)
- Park takes Jang to the supplier he has always gone easy on. Outside a half-open door they overhear the staff: push
  One International's orders to the back. Jang's eyes on him: *show me*. For once Park says it: "Shall we proceed by
  the procedure, then?" (the contract, the claims).
- Board: **Park's decision** (R21): caption "They're laughing at you. Keep the peace?"; the choice comes after.
- Places: the supplier's office (new).

**b19 · 19수 · The Proper Move** (W)
- The supplier's president scolds his own man in front of them, loudly; it's staged, so Park will back down. Park
  starts to fold; Jang pins the president to his own words. Park, rattled, garbles his sentence.
- Board: Jang's tesuji. Proverb after the solve: "You answer a trick move with the proper move." (묘수는 정수로 받는다)
- Places: the supplier's office.

**b20 · 20수 · Be Irresponsible** (W)
- The supplier's president comes to One International: unheard of. The bosses gather (Director Choi). Jang slips Park
  a note dressed up as a document: "Be irresponsible!" (blame the supplier). Park reads it, then: "The one who
  deceived… was me." He asks to be disciplined. Every boss's face becomes his own (a still). The company punishes no
  one; the partnership is too old.
- Jang: "I'm sorry." Park: "Thank you." The callback: *everyone has their own baduk* (Book 1, m22), and Jang now knows
  his advice was his own game, not Park's.
- Board: **Park's decision**, revealed after: caption "Whose mistake was it?" The player holds the note while
  deciding.
- Places: an executive meeting room (One International).

### Sun Ji-young (21–22)

**b21 · 21수 · The Doorbell** (W)
- Sun can't make the daycare pickup: the usual fight over who goes. Against her own rule (set up in b17) she asks
  Jang and Ahn, mortified. They fetch Somi. At the daycare the full-day children run to the door at every bell,
  "Mum's here!", and sag.
- **The player walks Jang** from the tower to the daycare (a walk with a child's backpack as the item?). Ahn comes
  because Sun asked both of them (*motive*); she stays cool (Book 1's note on her).
- Board: none, or a light one. The still: the children at the door.
- Places: a daycare (new), the streets between.

**b22 · 22수 · Her Back** (W)
- Sun at home. Somi's drawing of her mother shows only her back. Every morning Somi bows goodbye in baby talk, and her
  mother is already walking away on the phone. Sun cries: "I won't put you off for the sake of making a living."
- **The player is Sun** for this beat (a cutaway, like Oh's in Book 1).
- Board: the record, White 22 (two weak stones joined into one wall; a reader's reading: a family).
- Places: Sun's flat (an earlier plan has one).

### The PT, preparation (23–26)

Jumps are marked: a countdown line opens each beat, as in Book 1 ("Ten days to the PT").

**b23 · 23수 · Copy and Paste** (W)
- PT prep. Half the interns' decks come off the internet and look the same. Han talks and persuades; Jang builds the
  slides and goes along.
- (invented, a setup) **The numbers**: Jang checks Han's figures twice; Han waves it off. A senior's scolding: "The
  calculator does the sums, so how do you get them wrong?" This plants b30's calculation error.
- Board: the record, Black 23.
- Places: Sales 3, the meeting room.

**b24 · 24수 · Questions, Not Answers** (W)
- Han's method: the evaluators know more than you, so arm yourself with good questions, not answers. Han in before
  dawn, off to the airport and the port (a still). Jang sees Han's **field notebooks** (the setup for b31).
- Ahn's partner, **Lee Sang-hyun**, introduced (for b30): a senior leans on him to buy insurance.
- Board: Han's? a joseki-like "questions" board, or the record.
- Places: Sales 3; the port as a still.

**b25 · 25수 · The Person You Least Want to Sell To** (W)
- The individual task: sell something to the person you least want to sell to (your partner), who decides whether to
  buy. Jang and Han clash head-on: Han won't take orders from anyone without field experience.
- Board: the record, Black 25, a butting move (치받는 수), as the episode is.
- Places: the training centre's briefing room (new) or the HR floor.

**b26 · 26수 · Through Others** (W)
- "It's through others that I'm revealed." Each intern picks an item. Jang asks Oh for something (we don't see what:
  the slippers stay a surprise, though they've been on screen since b17). Ahn's deadpan "안녕히" gag. The PT week
  card, then the training centre: "like a battlefield".
- Board: Jang's decision: caption "What do you sell to a man who only trusts the field?"; revealed only in b32.
- Places: Sales 3 → the training centre (a cut, with a card).

### The PT (27–32)

**b27 · 27수 · Next** (W)
- PT day. A team stretches the company logo and is finished on the spot. A team in costume ("We're the Janggu
  Team!") gets "Next." Ahn doesn't blink.
- Board: the record, Black 27. Light, a breath before the storm.
- Places: the PT room (new).

**b28 · 28수 · Nerves** (W)
- Han, so sure of himself while others presented, is shaking. His phone won't stop: his mother, then the shop floor.
  Someone produces a herbal calmative.
- **The player is Han** for the wait (the setup for b29's choke and his father; the player feels the nerves).
- Board: none, or a short one at the water cooler.
- Places: the waiting area.

**b29 · 29수 · Black Nails** (W)
- The pair PT. Han starts, chokes on his water, can't go on. Jang takes over: he knows the material but has never
  presented, and stammers. Han recovers and speaks for the dignity of the factory floor. Flashback: a boy tells his
  father his nails are black; the father: grease, it won't wash off; are you ashamed? Han now: "I'm not ashamed of you,
  or of the floor." Oh: "So someone *else* is keeping an eye on Jang."
- Board: **Jang's decision** (R21): Han has stopped mid-sentence; caption "Your partner has stopped. The room is
  waiting."; revealed after: he stands.
- Stills: the choke; the father's hands.
- Places: the PT room.

**b30 · 30수 · The President's Daughter?** (W)
- Han's team is marked down for a sum (the payoff of b23). Then Ahn's PT: flawless. An evaluator: "You're not the
  president's daughter, are you? Or a plant?" Her partner Lee barely gets a word in.
- **The player is Ahn** for her PT: her board, cold and exact.
- Board: Ahn's, a precise endgame.
- Places: the PT room.

**b31 · 31수 · Field Notebooks** (W, D)
- The individual task, Han first. Han lays out his field notebooks and unrolls a bolt of fabric with a flourish. Jang
  buys **only the notebooks**: he's buying Han's experience, because he wants to sell textiles with him. Ahn, watching,
  smiles; so does Oh, who is sitting there **barefoot**.
- Board: **Jang's decision**: caption "Two things on the table. Buy, or not?"; revealed after.
- Places: the PT room.

**b32 · 32수 · Combat Boots** (W, D) · the book's boss beat
- Jang's turn. His item: Oh's office slippers, "the office worker's combat boots", set against Han's field boots. Han:
  "I won't buy them." Again. Again. Jang's closing: there are no meaningless stones on the board; Cho Chikun playing
  from a wheelchair. Han: no product a company makes exists without a reason. He admits he was narrow. Later Han
  wears slippers, carefully, and hands pairs round the office.
- Board: the boss board (redmond), the hardest of the book.
- Places: the PT room; Sales 3 for the slippers.

### After (33)

**b33 · 33수 · Contract** (W)
- The results. Ahn first overall. Han in. Jang Baek-gi in (steel team), with the joke of his tiny hand mirror in an
  enormous box. Jang: **a two-year contract.** He puts on a proper employee ID anyway.
- Board: the record, Black 33 ("Jang's real company life is about to begin").
- Places: the HR floor or the training centre.

**b33b · 33수 · The First Morning** (W)
- The first working day. Before the office, Oh takes the new hires to a memorial tent by Daehanmun, in front of
  Deoksugung: portraits of laid-off car workers. They stand. Ahn seems to know what this is. The book ends on the
  portraits, quietly.
- No board.
- Places: Daehanmun (an earlier plan has the tent).

## Leads (the player switches)

Jang leads most of the book. Short switches, each with a reason the player can see (R22):
Park (b17's cold open), Sun (b22), Han (b28), Ahn (b30). Each ends by handing back to Jang with a fade or a walk.

## The R22 audit, in short

- **Setups**: Sun's rule (b17 → b21); Oh's slippers (b17 → b31–32); Han's field notebooks (b24 → b31); the sums
  (b23 → b30); Lee Sang-hyun (b24 → b30); Han's mother and father (b28 → b29); "own baduk" (Book 1 m22 → b20).
- **Motives**: Park invites Jang (b17 → b18); Sun asks both (b21); Han's nerves (b28) before the choke; Jang buys only
  the notebooks *because* he wants Han as a colleague (said on screen).
- **Jumps**: countdown lines (two weeks → PT week); a card for the training centre; "The first working day" for b33b.

## Two calls for the user

1. **The memorial at Daehanmun (b33b).** In the webtoon it's real: the Ssangyong Motor workers' memorial altar, 2012,
   with the Jaeneung Education building behind. Readers took it as Oh's rite for new hires, solidarity "as people who
   live by work". The drama didn't use it. *Plot's recommendation:* keep it, quietly, as the book's ending, naming
   only "a memorial for laid-off car workers". The alternative is to end on the ID card at b33.
2. **Jang Baek-gi and the glasses intern.** Book 1's unnamed "glasses intern" (the study group, the gossip) has no
   name. Baek-gi, the elite steel-team intern who looks down on Jang, first matters in b33 and in Book 3. *Plot's
   recommendation:* make the glasses intern Baek-gi from b23 on, so the player already knows him when he's hired. To
   be checked against the comic while drafting.
