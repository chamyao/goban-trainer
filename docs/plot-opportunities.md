# Story opportunities in later chapters: women who could lead

Marked at the user's request: "are there any diaochen-type super opportunites later on for other books? lets mark them
now so we dont miss them". The rule (playbook, Part 0): "if theres an opportunity to tell the narrative from a female
characters perspective in an interesting and natrual way then we should opt to do so".

**Status:** listed from Plot's knowledge of the novel. **Not yet checked against the text.** The playbook's rule applies:
read the chapters in full and confirm every detail before designing around any of them. Chapter numbers are the Mao
edition's. Where Plot is unsure of the exact chapter, it says so.

## Strongest (Diaochan-type: she acts, decides, and carries the stretch)

| Who | Chapters | What she does | Why it plays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lady Sun** (孫夫人, Sun Quan's sister) | 54–55, 61 | Married to Liu Bei as part of Zhou Yu's trap. She sides with her husband, and when Wu's generals block their escape at the river she faces them down herself (玄德智激孫夫人). Later she is recalled to Wu and tries to take the infant heir with her; Zhao Yun intercepts the boat (趙雲截江奪阿斗). | A warrior-princess with armed maids, caught between brother and husband. She has initiative at every turn. The escape could be its own mechanic. |
| **Lady Xu** (徐氏, Sun Yi's widow) | 38 | Her husband is murdered by his own officers. She pretends to accept the murderer, sets the night, and has loyal men kill him: a widow's revenge by ruse (戰長江孫氏報仇). | A short, complete story, entirely hers, with tension and deception. It is closest to Diaochan in shape. |
| **Lady Cai** (蔡夫人, Liu Biao's wife) | 34, 40 | She listens behind a screen to Liu Biao and Liu Bei (蔡夫人隔屏聽密語), plots to have Liu Bei killed (he escapes with the leap over the Tan creek), and later secures the succession for her son. | A villain with initiative. Eavesdropping and palace intrigue make it natural for stealth. Playing the villain is allowed. |
| **Lady Mi** (糜夫人) at Changban | 41 | Wounded, she hands the infant A Dou to Zhao Yun and throws herself into a well so he can escape with the child (趙子龍單騎救主). | A short, devastating decision that is hers. Probably a decisive beat inside Zhao Yun's stretch, not a whole arc. |
| **Lady Zhu Rong** (祝融夫人, Meng Huo's wife) | about 89–90 (verify) | During the southern campaign she rides out to battle herself, wielding flying knives, and captures Shu generals before she is taken by a trick. | A playable warrior woman in the field. Her fighting and capture could be a contest stretch. |

## Good moments (a beat or a scene, not a whole stretch)

| Who | Chapters | What | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lady Wu** (吳國太, Sun Quan's mother) | 54 | Inspects Liu Bei at the temple (吳國太佛寺看新郎), likes him, and overrules her son and Zhou Yu so the marriage becomes real. | A decider: she turns the trap into a wedding. She could be the decider on a board, or a short lead. |
| **Lady Zou** (鄒氏) | 16 | Taken by Cao Cao at Wancheng; she herself suggests he move outside the city, which isolates his tent for Zhang Xiu's night attack (checked in the Lü Bu survey). | Her suggestion turns the plot, but her fate is never told. Handle with care. |
| **Empress Fu** (伏皇后) | 66 | Writes a secret letter to her father against Cao Cao; it is discovered and she is dragged from the palace (伏皇后為國捐生). | Tragic and brave. A secret-letter stretch could be hers. |
| **Wang Yi** (王異, Zhao Ang's wife) | about 64 (verify) | Urges the defenders on against Ma Chao and gives up her jewels for the soldiers. | A minor but real moment of resolve. |
| **Xin Xianying** (辛憲英) | about 107 (verify) | Advises her brother on what to do during Sima Yi's coup. | Small: a counsel beat. |
