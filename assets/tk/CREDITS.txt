Three Kingdoms campaign art

- Jade Tileset Pack by Willibab — https://willibab.itch.io/free-jade-tileset-pack — CC-BY
  (j_*.png: trees, bamboo, rocks, flowers, banners)
- Retro Pixelated: Eastern Fantasy Mountains by GameTorch — https://gametorch.app/collections/21 — CC0
  (g_*.png, p_immortal.png, fan.png)
- Terrain, characters, portraits and effects are drawn in code (tk.js).
- Town (assets/tk/jade): Jade Tileset Pack by Willibab, https://willibab.itch.io/free-jade-tileset-pack, CC-BY.
  folk1/folk2.png are the pack's character sheets scaled back to 1x; tiles.png is cut from it by tools/build_tk_town.py.
- Town engine: Phaser 3 (MIT), loaded from cdnjs.
- Generated world maps (assets/tk/ninja): Ninja Adventure Asset Pack by Pixel-boy and AAA,
  https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack, CC0. Fetched by tools/fetch_ninja_pack.py.
- Map kits (assets/tk/kits) say how each pack draws the abstract maps; see docs/map-format.md.
