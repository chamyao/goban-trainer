Wide shot from behind Lü Bu inside an open army tent. Lü Bu, in a crested helmet, draws a great bow to full stretch. Far off across the open ground, at the camp gate, a tall halberd stands planted upright, its small side blade catching the light. At the table on either side, Liu Bei holds his breath with eyes half closed, and Ji Ling watches with a sceptical frown. Lü Bu is a tall, strikingly handsome young warrior, broad-shouldered, in ornate red and gold armour with a gold crown bearing two long pheasant-feather plumes, carrying a tall crescent-bladed halberd. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
