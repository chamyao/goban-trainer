A crowded dusty market square in a county town at midday under a pale overcast sky. Townsfolk in plain hemp clothes press toward a whitewashed wall where a large paper notice has been pasted, columns of black brushed characters and red seals clear to see; one man reads aloud while others strain on tiptoe over shoulders. Wide shot. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
