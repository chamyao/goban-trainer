Character portrait of Liu Bei, a gentle, kind-hearted man of twenty-eight with a plain, ordinary face, not handsome: a round, open face, a broad nose, soft tired eyes with smile lines, a slightly weathered complexion, a short untidy black beard, very long earlobes, hair in a simple topknot tied with cloth; a modest, slightly worn white robe with a little gold trim, a straw-woven mat maker's calloused hands held together politely; a warm, humble, sincere half-smile, someone people trust at once. Half-body from the waist up, turned three-quarters toward the viewer, a characteristic expression. In the style of Genshin Impact character art: polished anime cel shading, clean lineart, vibrant colours, soft rim light. Plain flat pure white background, nothing else behind the figure. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD: no text, no modern items.
