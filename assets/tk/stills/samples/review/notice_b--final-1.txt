Medium shot at the edge of the crowd before the notice on the wall. Liu Bei stands a little apart with his arms hanging at his sides, head lowered, letting out a long sigh. A step behind him Zhang Fei has noticed it and leans in over his shoulder, brow furrowed, about to speak. Afternoon light, dust in the air. Only these two. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
