Morning outside a county hostel: fifty or sixty old villagers in patched hemp clothes crowd at the gate, weeping and gripping the bars, begging to be let in. Zhang Fei rides up from the side on a horse, reins in hard and frowns down at them, a wine gourd at his saddle. Overcast, dusty ground. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
