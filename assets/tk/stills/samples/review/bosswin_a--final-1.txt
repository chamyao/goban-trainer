A hillside ridge in daylight: Han soldiers crouching behind rocks heave clay jars of filth down the slope. The jars burst on the ground below; where the black whirlwind meets them it shreds apart into flying scraps of paper, and Zhang Bao's rebels stumble back from the stink and spatter. Mid-action, dust and flying clay. Zhang Bao is a sorcerer general with long loose black hair and a yellow headscarf, in a yellow robe. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
