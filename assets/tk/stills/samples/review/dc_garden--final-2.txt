A moonlit rear garden at night. Beside a small peony pavilion, Diaochan sits alone on a stone bench, her hands resting in her lap, looking up at the full moon and sighing. Peonies in bloom around the pavilion, a willow trailing over a still pond, soft blue moonlight. Diaochan is a graceful young woman of sixteen in a flowing, sheer pale silk robe with long translucent gauze sleeves and a light silk shawl drifting around her, hair in an elegant bun with a jade pin. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
