A dialogue screenshot from an ink-wash painting game in the manner of Tale of Immortal and Eastern Exorcist: the whole world painted like a Chinese ink scroll, rice-paper texture, soft washes of colour, characters with brush outlines. The game world (an ancient Chinese village inn at evening) behind, dimmed; in front, a large character portrait of Guan Yu (a towering man with a deep red face, narrow eyes, a very long black beard, green robe and green headscarf) on the left, and a dialogue box along the bottom with his name and the line "Guan Yu: Wine, quickly! I'm off to the city to join the army." with the Chinese "关羽：快斟酒来吃，我待赶入城去投军！" above it. Han dynasty China; nothing modern.
