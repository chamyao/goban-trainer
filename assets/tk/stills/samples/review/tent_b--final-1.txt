Inside a large army tent lit by a bronze oil lamp, Liu Bei steps in and bows to his old teacher Lu Zhi, who has risen from his camp desk and reaches out both hands to take his, smiling warmly. Scrolls and a map lie on the desk behind them. Medium shot, both men in profile. Only these two. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Lu Zhi is a dignified old scholar-general with a long grey beard, in plain grey clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
