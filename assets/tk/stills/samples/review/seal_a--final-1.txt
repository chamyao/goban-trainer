Night in the ruins of a palace: a round stone well glows with pale five-coloured light rising from its mouth. Sun Jian and a few soldiers with torches lean over the rim, faces lit from below, one soldier pointing down into the well. Broken columns around them. Wide shot, ash on the ground. Sun Jian is a broad, powerful general in his late thirties with a short beard, in red lacquered armour and a red cap. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
