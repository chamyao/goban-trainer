A private rear garden at night under a full moon. Beside a small open pavilion surrounded by peonies, a sixteen-year-old girl in pale silk with her hair up has dropped to her knees on the stone path, startled, looking up. Before her stands an elderly minister with a grey beard in a dark robe, leaning on a walking staff, his face wet with tears. Moonlight, a trellis of white roses behind him. Only these two. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
