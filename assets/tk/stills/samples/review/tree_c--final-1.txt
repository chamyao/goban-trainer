Inside a poor country house in the afternoon: Liu Bei's mother sits at a loom by the door, weaving straw mats; on the right Liu Bei as a young man sits at a low table reading a bamboo scroll, and Gongsun Zan, a young man of his own age, sits across from him sharing the book. Through the open door on the left, the mulberry tree stands in the yard. Calm, plain overcast light. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Gongsun Zan is a lean, proud man dressed in white. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
