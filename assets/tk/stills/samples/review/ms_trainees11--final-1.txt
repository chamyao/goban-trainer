A long bright room at the Korea Baduk Association: rows of go boards on low tables, dozens of children and young teenagers playing in silence, game clocks ticking. Among them, at a middle table, a boy of eleven with a fringe sits very straight, hands in his lap, facing an older opponent. Newspaper clippings of famous players are pinned on a corkboard by the door. Style: 2D Korean webtoon, hard cel shading. Modern-day Seoul, South Korea.
