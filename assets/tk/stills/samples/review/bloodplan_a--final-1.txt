A hillside at the roadside shrine in grey afternoon light: on the left Liu Bei points down the hill toward a village of pens and huts; in the middle Guan Yu and Zhang Fei stand listening, Guan Yu pointing at one ridge and Zhang Fei at the other; on the right, the two ridges rise above the road. Only these three men. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
