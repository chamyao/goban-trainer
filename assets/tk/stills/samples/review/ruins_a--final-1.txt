The burned palace city of Luoyang under a flat grey sky: charred black columns, collapsed roofs, thin smoke rising from the rubble, an empty gate hall. In the foreground, small against the ruin, Liu Bei, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei stand side by side looking at it, their horses behind them. Wide shot, ash on the ground. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
