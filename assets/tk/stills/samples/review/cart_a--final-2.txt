A dusty country road on a grey afternoon, seen close from beside the wheels. A wooden cage cart fills the middle of the frame, an old man in plain grey clothes inside it, gripping the bars; imperial soldiers with spears walk at its wheels. Three riders, Liu Bei, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei, have just reined in on the other side of the cart, close enough to see their faces, staring at the prisoner. Medium shot, dust hanging low. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
