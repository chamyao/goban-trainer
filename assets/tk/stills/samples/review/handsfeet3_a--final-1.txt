Close shot inside an army tent by lamplight. Zhang Fei, face twisted with shame, has drawn his sword to his own throat. Liu Bei has thrown his arms around him from the front and grips his wrist, pulling the blade away. Guan Yu, a step behind, reaches toward them. Strong emotion, tears on Zhang Fei's face. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
