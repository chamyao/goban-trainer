A narrow valley mouth between steep hills. Stones and arrows rain down from a hillside held by an enemy army; behind Lü Bu's army, another enemy column is pulling back as drums roll. In the middle, the great general on his red horse wheels around in fury, halberd raised, not knowing which way to charge. Gongs and drums on stands on both hillsides. Lü Bu is a tall, strikingly handsome young warrior, broad-shouldered, in ornate red and gold armour with a gold crown bearing two long pheasant-feather plumes, carrying a tall crescent-bladed halberd. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
