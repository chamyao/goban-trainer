Only these three men in the picture; Liu Bei is not tied or beaten. the inspector is a plump, sneering court official with a thin moustache, in a black gauze official's hat and a blue silk robe. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold.
