A scholar's study in the afternoon, lattice light. Bundles of bamboo-slip books are stacked everywhere, and on a stand rests a qin zither whose tail end is scorched black. At a low go board, mid-game, sits an elderly scholar with a long grey beard and a gentle, tired face, holding a stone between two fingers. Across from him, the grey-bearded minister Wang Yun studies him rather than the board. Only these two. Wang Yun is an old minister with a long grey beard, in dark court robes and a tall official's cap. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
