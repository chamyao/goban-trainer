A torchlit camp at night: Sun Jian in armour stands with one hand raised, swearing, jaw set. Before him Yuan Shao sits at a low table with his hand held out as if waiting to receive something. Behind them other lords look on. Neither man holds anything. Sun Jian is a broad, powerful general in his late thirties with a short beard, in red lacquered armour and a red cap. Yuan Shao is a proud, richly dressed nobleman in his forties with a neat beard, in a purple brocade robe and a tall official's cap. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
