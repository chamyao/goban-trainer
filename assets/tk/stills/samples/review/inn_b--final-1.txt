Close shot of three men at a small inn table at night, heads bent together over clay wine bowls, one small oil lamp between them. Guan Yu, in the middle, is speaking quietly, hands flat on the table, while Liu Bei and Zhang Fei listen, leaning in. Everything past the lamp falls into soft shadow. A calm, intimate conversation. Exactly three men. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
