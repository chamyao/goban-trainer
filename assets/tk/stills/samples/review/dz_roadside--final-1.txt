A long banquet tent pitched beside a dusty road outside a city gate of Chang'an, afternoon. Rows of Han ministers in dark court robes and black caps kneel at low tables; their faces are pale, eyes lowered, and several have dropped their chopsticks onto the tables. At the head table a huge, fat warlord in rich brocade and a jewelled cap holds up a wine cup, laughing with his mouth open. Whatever they are watching is off the edge of the picture, to the right; nothing of it is shown. Medium-wide shot from behind the ministers' shoulders toward the laughing man. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
