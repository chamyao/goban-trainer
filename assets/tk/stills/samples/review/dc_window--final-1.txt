Morning in an inner bedroom of a great mansion. A young woman in a loose silk robe sits at an open lattice window combing her long black hair. Below the window is a small garden pond, and on its still surface is the reflection of a very tall man in a hair-binding cap, peering from behind a rock out of sight. She has seen the reflection; her eyes slide toward it while her brows knit in feigned sorrow, a perfumed handkerchief at her eye. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
