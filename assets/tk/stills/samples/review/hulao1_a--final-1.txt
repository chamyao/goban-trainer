Wide panoramic composition under a heavy sky. On the right, the towering walls of Hulao Pass with Dong Zhuo's black banners. Before the gate, alone, Lü Bu sits on a huge red horse, a crested helmet on his head and a long halberd in his hand. On the left, the lords' armies are drawn up in long lines under many coloured banners, their champions in front, holding back. Dong Zhuo is a huge, heavy, bearded bully of a warlord with a sneer, in a general's armour. Lü Bu is a tall, strikingly handsome young warrior, broad-shouldered, in ornate red and gold armour with a gold crown bearing two long pheasant-feather plumes, carrying a tall crescent-bladed halberd. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
