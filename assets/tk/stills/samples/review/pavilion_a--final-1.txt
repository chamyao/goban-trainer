Evening beside a lotus pond at a garden pavilion: Diaochan rests against Lü Bu's chest, her face turned up to his, wet with tears; he holds her with both arms. His tall halberd leans against the pavilion railing beside them. Grey light on the water, lanterns not yet lit. Only these two. Diaochan is a graceful young woman of sixteen in a flowing, sheer pale silk robe with long translucent gauze sleeves and a light silk shawl drifting around her, hair in an elegant bun with a jade pin. Lü Bu is a tall, strikingly handsome young warrior, broad-shouldered, in ornate red and gold armour with a gold crown bearing two long pheasant-feather plumes, carrying a tall crescent-bladed halberd. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
