Wide panoramic composition at dawn outside a walled city ringed with Yellow Turban camps. In the middle, a single rider, Taishi Ci, gallops away from the city gate at full stretch, turned in the saddle and drawing his bow back at the pursuers. Behind him, several Yellow Turban horsemen tumble from their saddles with arrows in them. On the walls behind, Han soldiers watch. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
