The courtyard of a high-walled fortress, soldiers pouring in through the gate behind. In the foreground the towering general Lü Bu, still in armour, has taken the hand of a young woman in pale silk and is leading her away from a doorway. Her face is calm and unreadable, her eyes lowered. Around them, soldiers carry out chests of gold and bolts of silk, and a line of young women is being let out through another gate. Lü Bu is a tall, strikingly handsome young warrior, broad-shouldered, in ornate red and gold armour with a gold crown bearing two long pheasant-feather plumes, carrying a tall crescent-bladed halberd. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
