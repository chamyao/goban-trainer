Wide panoramic composition among low hills under a dusty afternoon sky. On the right, a Yellow Turban host pours down a valley under great yellow banners, Zhang Jiao in a yellow robe riding among them. In the middle, Han soldiers in red run before them, dropping spears, their commander Dong Zhuo, heavy and bearded, fleeing on horseback. On the left, on the crest of a hill, Liu Bei, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei rein in and look down at the rout. Zhang Jiao is a gaunt old sorcerer with long white hair and a long white beard, a yellow headscarf and a flowing yellow robe, burning eyes. Dong Zhuo is a huge, heavy, bearded bully of a warlord with a sneer, in a general's armour. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
