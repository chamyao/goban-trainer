Inside the top floor of a city gate tower, grey daylight. Cao Cao and Liu Bei sit side by side behind a low table. Guan Yu and Zhang Fei stand beside them. On the floor before them, Lü Bu kneels, tied with heavy ropes, still in his armour, his crested helmet gone, looking up at Liu Bei. Cao Cao is a lean man of about thirty with a thin moustache and a quick, sharp look, black official's cap, dark red robe with gold trim. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Lü Bu is a tall, strikingly handsome young warrior, broad-shouldered, in ornate red and gold armour with a gold crown bearing two long pheasant-feather plumes, carrying a tall crescent-bladed halberd. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
