Grey early morning over a smoking plain: Cao Cao gallops toward us on a dark horse with his sword drawn and raised, mouth open mid-shout, a column of cavalry behind him under bright red banners. Fleeing rebels in yellow headscarves scatter ahead of them. Dust and trampled grass. Cao Cao is a lean man of about thirty with a thin moustache and a quick, sharp look, black official's cap, dark red robe with gold trim. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
