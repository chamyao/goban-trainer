Under a huge mulberry tree a small boy of six in a plain hemp tunic and straw sandals points up at the round crown, chin raised, announcing that he will ride in a carriage like that one day. Beside him his uncle, a middle-aged farmer in a hemp robe, has caught the boy's sleeve and half turns to hush him, startled. Overcast afternoon, a dirt yard. Only these two. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
