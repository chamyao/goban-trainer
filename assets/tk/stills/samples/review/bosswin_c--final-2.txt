Close shot, looking up into a grey sky after a storm: torn scraps of paper, pieces of paper soldiers and paper horses, a head, an arm, a half-horse, tumble and flutter down through the air like fallen leaves, caught mid-fall at different heights with the dark clouds thinning behind them. A strip of green hillside at the bottom of the frame. Nothing stands upright; only falling paper, and no living people. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
