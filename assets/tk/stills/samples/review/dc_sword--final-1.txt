A rear hall in a great mansion, a rack on the wall with one sword missing. A young woman in silk has drawn that sword and holds the blade to her own neck, eyes shut, face set. A huge fat man in brocade lunges toward her with both hands out to grab the sword, his face full of alarm. No blood. Only these two. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
