Inside a dim bedchamber lit by one oil lamp. An old man, Tao Qian, lies propped on pillows in a plain bed, thin and grey, holding out a square seal on a cord with a shaking hand. Liu Bei kneels at the bedside, hands raised, half refusing. Two officers stand in the shadows behind. Quiet and heavy. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
