At a palace gate in the early morning, Dong Zhuo, huge and heavy in armour, has fallen from a carriage with a broken wheel and lies on the ground looking up in terror; Lü Bu stands over him and drives his halberd down at his throat. Li Su rushes in beside him with a sword; guards freeze in the background. Dong Zhuo is a huge, heavy, bearded bully of a warlord with a sneer, in a general's armour. Lü Bu is a tall, strikingly handsome young warrior, broad-shouldered, in ornate red and gold armour with a gold crown bearing two long pheasant-feather plumes, carrying a tall crescent-bladed halberd. Li Su is a lean officer in plain armour with a drawn sword. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
