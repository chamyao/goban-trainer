Wading a river at dusk: Cao Cao rides a strong horse with an arrow in his shoulder, swaying, while Cao Hong, on foot in the water beside it, strips off his armour and leads the horse by the bridle, glancing back at the far bank. Splashing water, reeds, a few pursuers' torches behind them. Cao Cao is a lean man of about thirty with a thin moustache and a quick, sharp look, black official's cap, dark red robe with gold trim. Cao Hong is a sturdy young officer with a short beard, stripped to a plain tunic. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
