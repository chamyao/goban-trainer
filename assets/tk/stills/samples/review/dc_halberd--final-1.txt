A mansion garden with winding paths. In the foreground a huge, fat man in brocade, red-faced with rage, has just thrown a long painted halberd with all his strength. In the background a tall young general in armour, running away, twists round and strikes the flying halberd aside with his arm. The Phoenix Pavilion and lotus pond behind. A young woman watches from the pavilion, hand to her mouth. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
