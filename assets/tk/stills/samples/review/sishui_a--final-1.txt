Wide panoramic composition on a grey day before a mountain pass. On the right, the stone walls and gate of Sishui Pass. In the middle, a band of Dong Zhuo's horsemen rides forward, one holding up a long pole with a red head-cloth hung at its tip, the others shouting. On the left, the palisade and tents of the lords' camp, where soldiers and officers watch from the gate in silence. Dong Zhuo is a huge, heavy, bearded bully of a warlord with a sneer, in a general's armour. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
