Inside a richly furnished hall of a fortress, a tiny, very old woman of over ninety, white hair thin, sits on a couch and clutches the brocade sleeve of her enormous fat son, who stands over her laughing, ready to leave. Her face is creased with dread; his is full of greed and joy. Through a doorway behind him, a waiting procession and banners. Only these two in the room. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
