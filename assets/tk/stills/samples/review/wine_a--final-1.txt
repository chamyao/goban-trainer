Outside the lords' camp, beside a low table, Cao Cao holds out a bronze cup of steaming hot wine. Guan Yu, already in the saddle with his long crescent-bladed halberd, does not take it; he looks toward the gate and the sound of drums beyond, turning his horse. Steam rises from the cup. Overcast grey afternoon. Cao Cao is a lean man of about thirty with a thin moustache and a quick, sharp look, black official's cap, dark red robe with gold trim. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
