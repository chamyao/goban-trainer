A dusk road: Cao Cao sits his horse with his sword lowered, jaw tight, staring straight ahead. Beside him Chen Gong looks at him in appalled silence, hand loose on the reins. Far behind them on the road lie a fallen donkey and a figure that does not move, two wine jars rolled in the dust. Cao Cao is a lean man of about thirty with a thin moustache and a quick, sharp look, black official's cap, dark red robe with gold trim. Chen Gong is a thin, upright county magistrate in his thirties with a sparse beard, in a plain dark robe and scholar's cap. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
