A long banquet table in a great lords' tent, lamps burning. Yuan Shao sits at the head in rich robes among the other lords; at the far end, at the lowest place, sits Liu Bei, hands on his knees, looking at no one. Behind him Guan Yu and Zhang Fei stand with arms folded, faces cool. The lords talk among themselves and ignore him. Yuan Shao is a proud, richly dressed nobleman in his forties with a neat beard, in a purple brocade robe and a tall official's cap. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
