Inside a lamp-lit government hall, two rows of officials in court robes sit at low tables facing each other. Down the aisle between them a servant walks carrying a lacquered red tray covered with a cloth. Every official has turned rigid, staring at the tray, faces grey with fear; one has half risen. At the far end of the hall the fat warlord Dong Zhuo sits smiling. The tray stays covered. Low angle down the aisle. Dong Zhuo is a huge, heavy, bearded bully of a warlord with a sneer, in a general's armour. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
