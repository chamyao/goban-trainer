A small second-floor baduk class above a row of shops in a 1990s Seoul neighbourhood: low tables, worn wooden boards, plastic bowls of stones, a hand-written ranking chart on the wall. A boy of about seven, in a striped T-shirt, leans over a board, chin almost on the stones, while two grown men behind him (an uncle in a sweater, a father in shirtsleeves) watch with cash folded in their hands. Afternoon light through a dusty window. Style: 2D Korean webtoon, hard cel shading. Modern-day Seoul, South Korea.
