The top of a high city gate tower at dusk, smoke and flames rising from the city behind. A frail grey-bearded minister in court robes stands at the very edge of the parapet, looking down. Behind him under a yellow canopy, a pale boy Emperor of about twelve reaches out a hand as if to stop him. Far below, a vast rebel army fills the square, and two generals on horseback look up with swords drawn. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
