A city market street at night, deserted stalls. In the middle of the street lies a great dark mound under a rough cloth, only its outline seen; on top of it a small flame burns like a lamp, and dark grease has spread across the paving stones around it. Two soldiers stand guard with spears, their faces lit by the flame. In the shadows at the edge of the street, townspeople look on. Nothing of the body is shown but the covered shape. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
