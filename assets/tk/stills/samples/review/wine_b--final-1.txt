Before the lords' table, Guan Yu stands calm and tall with his crescent-bladed halberd grounded beside him; on the ground at his feet lies the helmeted head of an enemy general, and on the table the same bronze cup is still steaming. Cao Cao, seated, looks at the cup. The other lords stare in stunned silence. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Cao Cao is a lean man of about thirty with a thin moustache and a quick, sharp look, black official's cap, dark red robe with gold trim. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
