Wide panoramic composition under a pale sky at the foot of a rocky mountain. On the right, a vast Yellow Turban host fills the slope, yellow scarves and yellow banners as far as the eye can see, with Cheng Yuanzhi, a heavy rebel general, riding out in front. On the left, a small band of village volunteers holds the road, and at their head Liu Bei, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei sit on their horses, weapons ready. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
