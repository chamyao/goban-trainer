Inside a tent at night, Ding Yuan, a grey-haired governor, sits at a desk reading by a candle and looks up in mild surprise. Lü Bu, in armour, stands over him with a drawn sword held low at his side. Close two-shot, candle flame between them, shadows large on the tent wall. Only these two. Ding Yuan is an old provincial governor with a grey beard, in a dark official robe. Lü Bu is a tall, strikingly handsome young warrior, broad-shouldered, in ornate red and gold armour with a gold crown bearing two long pheasant-feather plumes, carrying a tall crescent-bladed halberd. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
