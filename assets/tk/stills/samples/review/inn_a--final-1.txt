Evening in a small village inn: plank walls, paper lattice windows, clay wine jars on a shelf, plain paper lanterns glowing. Guan Yu has just shoved the door open, stooping under the lintel and pulling a wooden handcart in behind him; every head in the room has turned. At a table by the wall Liu Bei and Zhang Fei look up at him with cups halfway to their mouths. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
