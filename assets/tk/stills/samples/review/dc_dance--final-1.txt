A hall lit by painted candles at night. A bead curtain hangs across the room. On the far side of it a slender young woman in flowing silk sleeves dances, turning, her sleeves in the air, seen half-hidden through the strands of beads. On the near side, a huge fat warlord in brocade sits at a table with a wine cup, leaning forward, watching her hungrily. An old minister sits a little behind him. Musicians with reed pipes at the edge of the light. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
