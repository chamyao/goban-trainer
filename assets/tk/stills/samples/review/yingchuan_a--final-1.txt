Wide panoramic composition on an overcast morning. On the left, trampled burnt grass with smoke still rising and Yellow Turban banners lying flat. In the middle, Huangfu Song and Zhu Jun on horseback, looking over the field, with Han spearmen around them. On the right, Liu Bei, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei arriving at the head of their column on the road. Huangfu Song is an imperial general with a long black beard, iron helmet, blue robe over armour. Zhu Jun is an imperial general with a short beard, iron helmet, dark red robe over armour. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
