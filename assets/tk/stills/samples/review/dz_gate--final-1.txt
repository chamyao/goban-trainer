Inside a palace gate, morning. An overturned carriage, soldiers with halberds in a ring. In the centre the towering general Lü Bu holds his halberd in his left hand and with his right raises an unrolled yellow imperial edict high above his head, shouting. At his feet, a huge fat body in brocade lies face down beside the carriage, the face not shown. Officials in court robes holding swords stand at the hall gate behind, and officers are raising their arms in a cheer. No blood shown. Lü Bu is a tall, strikingly handsome young warrior, broad-shouldered, in ornate red and gold armour with a gold crown bearing two long pheasant-feather plumes, carrying a tall crescent-bladed halberd. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
