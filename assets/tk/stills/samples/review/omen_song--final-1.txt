Night outside the walls of Chang'an, a full moon over the fields. A ring of small children in plain clothes stand in the grass, singing, their faces solemn. In the near distance, the glowing tent of a great lord, its flap stirred by the wind, a guard at its entrance turning his head toward the song. The city wall dark behind. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
