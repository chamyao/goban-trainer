Close shot from just behind an old man sitting inside a cage cart: the back of his grey head and his thin shoulders in the near corner of the frame, his arms in grey sleeves reaching forward so his hands rest on the rough wooden bars. Beyond the bars an empty, rutted dirt road runs away between bare fields toward distant hills under a flat grey sky, a few wisps of straw dropped in front. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
