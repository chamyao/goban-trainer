Early morning on a broad street leading to a palace gate. By the roadside stands a thin Taoist in a blue robe and white headcloth, holding up a long pole from which hangs a long strip of white cloth; at each end of the cloth is written a single large black character 口. A grand procession is passing; from his horse a fat lord in brocade has turned to stare at the cloth. Soldiers are moving to drive the Taoist off. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
