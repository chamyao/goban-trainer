Wide panoramic composition outside the gate of a small walled town on a clear autumn morning. On the right, a long column of cavalry under Gongsun Zan's banners rides along the road, with Gongsun Zan in silver armour on a white horse at its head, reining in. On the left, at the open town gate, Liu Bei in a magistrate's plain robe steps out to greet him, with Guan Yu and Zhang Fei standing behind him. Gongsun Zan is a lean, proud man dressed in white. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
