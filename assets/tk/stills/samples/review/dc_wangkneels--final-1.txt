Inside a small painted pavilion lit by one lamp, at night. The elderly minister Wang Yun, grey-bearded, in a dark robe, kneels and touches his forehead to the floor. Before him, seated on the only chair where he has placed her, the young singer Diaochan in pale silk shrinks back with her hands raised, shocked that her master is bowing to her. Painted beams and screens, no one else present. Wang Yun is an old minister with a long grey beard, in dark court robes and a tall official's cap. Diaochan is a graceful young woman of sixteen in a flowing, sheer pale silk robe with long translucent gauze sleeves and a light silk shawl drifting around her, hair in an elegant bun with a jade pin. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
