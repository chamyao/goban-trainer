In an open army camp among tents and banners on a hazy afternoon, Dong Zhuo sits at ease on a camp stool, sneering. Facing him, Zhang Fei lunges forward with his hand on his sword hilt, roaring; Liu Bei and Guan Yu each grab one of his arms and haul him back. Dong Zhuo's guards shift in the background. Dong Zhuo is a huge, heavy, bearded bully of a warlord with a sneer, in a general's armour. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
