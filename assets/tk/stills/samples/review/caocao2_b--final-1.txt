Afternoon in a scholar's quiet study: shelves of bamboo scrolls, a low table with a teapot and cups, light through a paper window. Cao Cao, a young man, sits cross-legged leaning back, laughing out loud with delight; opposite him Xu Shao sits upright and grave, watching him closely. Only these two. Cao Cao is a lean man of about thirty with a thin moustache and a quick, sharp look, black official's cap, dark red robe with gold trim. Xu Shao is a calm scholar with a thin beard and knowing eyes, scholar's hat, pale robe. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
