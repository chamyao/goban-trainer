A night street in Chang'an lit by two long rows of red lanterns carried by soldiers on either side. A huge young general in armour on a great red horse leans down from the saddle and grips an old minister in court robes by the collar; the minister, on a smaller horse, holds up a hand to calm him. A halberd rests across the general's saddle. Deep shadows, red light on their faces. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
