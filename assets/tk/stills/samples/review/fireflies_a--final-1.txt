At night in tall river reeds, two boys in torn silk robes, one about fourteen and one about nine, hold hands, scratched and tired. In front of them a line of fireflies hangs over the water, leading off between the reeds, and the younger boy points after it. Only the two boys, seen from behind and slightly to the side. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
