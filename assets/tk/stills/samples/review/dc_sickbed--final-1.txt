A dim bedchamber with a large curtained bed. On it a huge fat man lies asleep under brocade covers. Behind the bed, half leaning out from the bed-curtains, a young woman points with one hand at her own heart and with the other at the sleeping man, tears on her cheeks, looking across the room. At the room's entrance stands a tall young general in armour, frozen, staring back at her. Only these three. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
