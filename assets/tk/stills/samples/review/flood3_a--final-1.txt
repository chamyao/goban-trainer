Wide panoramic composition under a dull sky. A walled city, Xiapi, stands in a broad sheet of brown floodwater that reaches halfway up its walls, roofs and treetops showing above the water around it. Only the road to the east gate is still dry. On the hills in the foreground, Cao Cao's banners and soldiers watch. Cao Cao is a lean man of about thirty with a thin moustache and a quick, sharp look, black official's cap, dark red robe with gold trim. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
