Wide panoramic composition on a grey afternoon. In the middle, the walls and gate tower of Qingzhou, with Han soldiers and red banners along the battlements. Yellow Turban rebels surround the walls on every side with ladders and yellow banners. In the left foreground, on a low hill, Liu Bei, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei look down on the siege from horseback with a few hundred men behind them. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
