A wide dirt road across yellow loess hills. A great ornate carriage with a canopy has lurched to one side, one big wheel splintered and broken off its axle. Guards in armour have stopped around it, staring. A huge fat man in brocade is being helped down from it, scowling, while a groom leads up a horse. A wiry officer walks beside him, smoothing his face into a smile. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
