A garden pavilion with a curved red rail above a pond full of lotus flowers, afternoon. A young woman in pale silk has one hand on the rail and is leaning out over the water as if to jump; a towering young general in armour has caught her round the waist with both arms and pulled her back against him. His painted halberd leans against the pavilion pillar. Willows and flowering branches all round. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
