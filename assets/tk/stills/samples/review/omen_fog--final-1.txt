A long procession of guards, banners and a mounted lord on a road, swallowed by a sudden storm of wind: banners whip sideways, dust and dark fog blot out the sky, riders bend their heads. In the middle, the fat lord on horseback looks up at the darkened sky, uneasy; an officer rides close beside him, talking. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
