A dark wood at night. A burnt tree stump stands in a clearing with a red cap hung on top of it; enemy cavalry with torches ring it, edging forward warily. Half hidden among the trees behind them, Zu Mao crouches low with a sword in each hand, ready to charge. Smoke and torchlight. Zu Mao is a wiry officer in red armour with two swords. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
