Inside a plain county office in a small town, evening lamplight. Liu Bei in a simple official robe and cap sits at a low table with bowls of rice and greens, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei sharing the table with him, the three eating together and talking. Through the open door, townsfolk pass in a quiet street. Warm, calm light. Exactly three men at the table. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Guan Yu is a towering giant of a man, a head taller than anyone, with massive shoulders and a thick neck, a deep red face (only his face is red; his neck and hands are normal skin), a very long black beard down to his chest and narrow eyes under heavy brows, hair in a topknot, in a green robe. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
