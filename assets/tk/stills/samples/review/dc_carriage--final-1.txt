A great curtained carriage drawn by horses moves out through a city gate, surrounded by guards and a crowd of officials bowing by the roadside. From the carriage window, the curtain lifted, a young woman looks back and covers her face with her sleeve as if weeping. Far back in the crowd, a head taller than everyone, a young general in armour stares at the carriage. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
