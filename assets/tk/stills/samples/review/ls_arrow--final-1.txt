The same lamp-lit secret room. A wiry officer in a cavalry commander's robe, a sly, hungry face, snaps an arrow in two between his fists. Watching him, the huge general Lü Bu stands with a hand on his sword, and the old minister Wang Yun sits with an imperial edict scroll tucked in his sleeve. Two other officials sit at the table's edge. Lü Bu is a tall, strikingly handsome young warrior, broad-shouldered, in ornate red and gold armour with a gold crown bearing two long pheasant-feather plumes, carrying a tall crescent-bladed halberd. Wang Yun is an old minister with a long grey beard, in dark court robes and a tall official's cap. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
