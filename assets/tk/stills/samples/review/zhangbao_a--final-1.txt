Wide panoramic composition at dusk. On the left, Zhu Jun's Han camp with cooking fires and a line of spearmen below a ridge. In the middle, a long grassy valley. On the right, on a rocky hilltop, Zhang Bao stands before a small altar with a sword and paper charms, his long hair loose, yellow banners behind him, a thin dark cloud gathering over his head. Zhu Jun is an imperial general with a short beard, iron helmet, dark red robe over armour. Zhang Bao is a sorcerer general with long loose black hair and a yellow headscarf, in a yellow robe. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
