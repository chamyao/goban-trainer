A warm rear hall at evening, low tables laden with dishes. Diaochan, dressed in her finest silks and hair ornaments, kneels to offer a cup of wine with both hands to a towering young general, Lü Bu, in a crimson robe with a gold crown on his head; they are looking straight into each other's eyes. Beside them the grey-bearded host Wang Yun slumps with a flushed face, pretending to be drunk, watching from the corner of his eye. Two maids in blue stand by the door. Diaochan is a graceful young woman of sixteen in a flowing, sheer pale silk robe with long translucent gauze sleeves and a light silk shawl drifting around her, hair in an elegant bun with a jade pin. Lü Bu is a tall, strikingly handsome young warrior, broad-shouldered, in ornate red and gold armour with a gold crown bearing two long pheasant-feather plumes, carrying a tall crescent-bladed halberd. Wang Yun is an old minister with a long grey beard, in dark court robes and a tall official's cap. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
