A great hall during a victory feast, officials in court robes at low tables. Two guards have brought in and forced to kneel an elderly scholar with a long grey beard and a scholar's cap, his face wet with tears, hands raised in supplication. At the head of the hall the old minister Wang Yun sits rigid and cold. The officials stare, shocked; one has half risen as if to plead. Wang Yun is an old minister with a long grey beard, in dark court robes and a tall official's cap. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
