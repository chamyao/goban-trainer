Evening in a plague-stricken town: white-haired Zhang Jiao stands on a wooden platform in a yellow robe, one arm out pointing across the crowd, mid-sermon, mouth open. Hundreds of followers in yellow headscarves fill the square below, faces turned up, many holding bowls of charm water. Overcast grey light, torches being lit at the edges. Zhang Jiao is a gaunt old sorcerer with long white hair and a long white beard, a yellow headscarf and a flowing yellow robe, burning eyes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
