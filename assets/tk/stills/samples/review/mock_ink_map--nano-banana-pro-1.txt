A gameplay screenshot from an ink-wash painting game in the manner of Tale of Immortal and Eastern Exorcist: the whole world painted like a Chinese ink scroll, rice-paper texture, soft washes of colour, characters with brush outlines. Top-down three-quarter view of a small ancient Chinese village with earth-walled houses, a huge mulberry tree and peach trees in blossom; the three heroes Liu Bei (a gentle man with a short black beard, white robe with gold trim), Guan Yu (a towering man with a deep red face and a very long black beard, green robe, holding a crescent-bladed halberd) and Zhang Fei (stocky, wild black beard, black clothes, a long spear) walk together along a dirt path, a few villagers nearby. A small quest marker in the top-left corner and a minimap in the top-right. Han dynasty China; nothing modern.
