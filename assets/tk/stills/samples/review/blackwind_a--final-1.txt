A rocky hilltop under a darkening sky. Zhang Bao stands on the crest with his long hair streaming loose, sword raised in one hand, shouting a spell; a black whirlwind of storm cloud, sand and stones pours down the slope onto a line of Han spearmen, who throw down their shields and run. Pale paper riders gallop inside the cloud. Zhang Bao is a sorcerer general with long loose black hair and a yellow headscarf, in a yellow robe. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
