A dirt road at dusk: old Lü Boshe rides a donkey toward us with two clay wine jars hanging from the saddle, raising one hand in a friendly greeting. Ahead of him two riders, Cao Cao and Chen Gong, have stopped; Cao Cao has turned his horse back and his sword is already out. Wide shot, long shadows. Lü Boshe is a kindly old farmer with a white beard, in a patched hemp robe and a straw hat. Cao Cao is a lean man of about thirty with a thin moustache and a quick, sharp look, black official's cap, dark red robe with gold trim. Chen Gong is a thin, upright county magistrate in his thirties with a sparse beard, in a plain dark robe and scholar's cap. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
