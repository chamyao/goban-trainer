A steep wooden stair inside a gate tower. A thin scholar in a plain blue robe, Chen Gong, walks down it alone, back straight, hands bound before him. At the top of the stairs Cao Cao has half risen, one hand reaching after him, his face wet with tears. Guards stand aside and do not hold him. Chen Gong is a thin, upright county magistrate in his thirties with a sparse beard, in a plain dark robe and scholar's cap. Cao Cao is a lean man of about thirty with a thin moustache and a quick, sharp look, black official's cap, dark red robe with gold trim. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
