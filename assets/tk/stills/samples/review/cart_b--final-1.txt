Close shot through the bars of a wooden cage cart: old Lu Zhi, in the cage, speaks quietly to Liu Bei, who stands outside holding two of the bars. Behind Liu Bei's shoulder Zhang Fei glares at the guards, both hands white-knuckled on his spear. Overcast afternoon on a dirt road. Lu Zhi is a dignified old scholar-general with a long grey beard, in plain grey clothes. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
