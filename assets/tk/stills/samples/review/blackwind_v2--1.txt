On a hilltop Zhang Bao, hair loose, raises his sword and chants; a black whirlwind of storm cloud, sand and stones pours down the slope onto a line of soldiers with spears, who break and run; ghostly paper-like riders gallop inside the cloud. Zhang Bao is a sorcerer general with long loose black hair and a yellow headscarf, in a yellow robe.
