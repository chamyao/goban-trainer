In front of a county office gate, a plump official, the inspector, is tied against a thick wooden hitching post with ropes wound across his chest, howling. Zhang Fei, red-faced and furious, lashes a bundle of thin green willow switches across the inspector's legs; broken switches litter the ground. Liu Bei runs in from the left with one arm out to stop him. Afternoon. the inspector is a plump, sneering court official with a thin moustache, in a Han dynasty black gauze official's cap (a small stiff cap with two flaps, not a top hat) and a blue silk robe. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Liu Bei is a gentle man of twenty-eight with a short neat black beard, long earlobes and kind eyes, hair in a topknot, in a white robe trimmed with gold. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
