A wide dusty field before a fortress pass on an overcast afternoon. Lü Bu, on a tall red horse, thunders after Gongsun Zan on a white horse, halberd drawn back to thrust at his back. From the side Zhang Fei charges in on horseback, serpent spear levelled, roaring. Dust and flying clods. Only these three riders. Lü Bu is a tall, strikingly handsome young warrior, broad-shouldered, in ornate red and gold armour with a gold crown bearing two long pheasant-feather plumes, carrying a tall crescent-bladed halberd. Gongsun Zan is a lean general in white armour on a white horse. Zhang Fei is a stocky, barrel-chested, powerfully muscled man, broad rather than tall, with a fierce square jaw, big glaring eyes and a wild bristling black beard, hair in a topknot, in black and dark brown clothes. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
