A steep wooded ridge at night: Sun Jian on horseback among the trees looks up as stones and arrows rain down on him from the slope above, his horse rearing, branches snapping. His thirty riders scatter behind him, shouting. Dark, torch-lit from the side, dust and falling rock. Sun Jian is a broad, powerful general in his late thirties with a short beard, in red lacquered armour and a red cap. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
