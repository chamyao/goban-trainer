A bare earthen ridge outside the walls of Chang'an, late afternoon. A lone huge warrior in armour and a pheasant-plumed helmet sits on a great red horse, reins slack, gazing after a column whose dust hangs on the road far away. Behind him, small, an old minister in court robes is walking up the slope toward him. Wide shot, long shadows. Style: 2D donghua, hard cel shading. Han dynasty China, about 184 AD.
