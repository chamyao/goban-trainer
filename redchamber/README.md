# Dream of the Red Chamber (红楼梦)

A separate area for books drawn from *Dream of the Red Chamber*. It is kept apart from the Three Kingdoms game (`tk*`):
nothing here is wired into that engine, and nothing here follows its formats by default.

| Book | Chapters | Leads | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · 侯门深似海 *Deep as the Sea* | 1–6 (played: 3 and 6) | Lin Daiyu, then Granny Liu | `book1/design.md`, `book1/script.md` |

Source text: zh.wikisource.org, 紅樓夢/第NNN回 (the 120-chapter text). Chinese lines in the scripts are the novel's own
vernacular in simplified characters, lightly modernised only where a word would stop a reader.
