# Book 1 · 侯门深似海 *Deep as the Sea*: script

*Dream of the Red Chamber*, chapters 1–6. Design: `design.md`.

**How to read this.** Narration in italics. **Name:** speech. *(Name, thinking)* is a private aside the player hears and
no one in the room does. **◆ MOMENT** marks a read-the-room choice: what the room shows to someone who watches, the
choice, what the novel's character does, and what happens on a slip. [still: `id`] marks a still. Chinese follows each
line; it is the novel's own wording wherever the novel has the line. In a moment's **Watch** list, a cue in 「」 is the
novel's own; the others are staging that shows what the text says she noticed or knew. Every slip reaction is staging.

## Opening scroll · 第一回至第二回

> 甄士隐梦幻识通灵　贾雨村风尘怀闺秀 / 贾夫人仙逝扬州城　冷子兴演说荣国府

> When the goddess Nüwa mended the sky, she smelted thirty-six thousand five hundred and one stones, and used all but
> one. The last was left at the foot of Greensickness Peak. It had learned to feel, and it grieved that it alone was not
> good enough to mend the sky.  
> 女娲氏炼石补天之时，炼成顽石三万六千五百零一块，只用了三万六千五百块，单单剩了一块未用，弃在青埂峰下。谁知此石自经煅炼之后，灵性已通，因见众石俱得补天，独自己无材不堪入选，遂自怨自叹，日夜悲号惭愧。

> In heaven, by the Three Lives Rock, a stone page watered a crimson pearl flower with sweet dew every day. When he went

> give him all the tears of a lifetime."  
> 三生石畔，有绛珠草一株，神瑛侍者日以甘露灌溉。神瑛下世为人，绛珠道：「他是甘露之惠，我并无此水可还。他既下世为人，我也去下世为人，但把我一生所有的眼泪还他，也偿还得过他了。」

> In Yangzhou, Lady Jia, wife of the salt commissioner Lin Ruhai, has died. Their only child is a daughter, Daiyu, sickly

> 扬州巡盐御史林如海之妻贾氏病故，只有一女，名唤黛玉，聪明清秀，自幼多病。京中外祖母荣国府贾母，遣了船只来接。

> Of the Rong mansion, an antique dealer once said: "A centipede dies, but does not fall. The frame still stands outside,

> 古董商冷子兴曾说那荣国府：「百足之虫，死而不僵……如今外面的架子虽未甚倒，内囊却也尽上来了。」

# Part 1 · 步步留心 *Step by Step*

**Lead: Lin Daiyu.** Goal shown on entry: *"Meet your mother's family. Give no one a reason to laugh."* /
「拜见外祖母一家。不叫人耻笑了去。」

## D1 · The Side Gate (角门)
*The Rong mansion · the west side gate → the festooned gate* · no moment

*She has said goodbye to her father. He told her: you are often ill, you are very young, you have no mother to raise
you and no sisters to stand by you. Go to your grandmother.*  
林如海说：「汝父年将半百，再无续室之意，且汝多病，年又极小，上无亲母教养，下无姊妹兄弟扶持，今依傍外祖母及舅氏姊妹去，正好减我顾盼之忧，何反云不往？」黛玉听了，方洒泪拜别。

*Her mother always said that her grandmother's house was not like other houses. Even the third-rank serving women sent
to fetch her eat and dress like no one she has seen.*  
这林黛玉常听得母亲说过，他外祖母家与别家不同。他近日所见的这几个三等仆妇，吃穿用度，已是不凡了，何况今至其家。

*So she watches every step and minds every moment. She will not say one word more than she must, or take one step more,
for fear of being laughed at.*  
因此步步留心，时时在意，不肯轻易多说一句话，多行一步路，惟恐被人耻笑了他去。

[still: `hl_sedan`]

*(The player rides in the sedan chair and can look out through the gauze window. Busy streets; then, on the north side
of the street, two great stone lions and three great doors with beast-head knockers. The main door is shut. A dozen
richly dressed men sit before it. The plaque reads: Ning Guo Mansion, Built by Imperial Command.)*  
街北蹲着两个大石狮子，三间兽头大门，门前列坐着十来个华冠丽服之人。正门却不开，只有东西两角门有人出入。正门之上有一匾，匾上大书「敕造宁国府」五个大字。

*(Daiyu, thinking)* This must be my grandfather's elder brother's house.  
这必是外祖之长房了。

*A little further west: three more great doors. The Rong mansion. The chair does not go in by the main door. It goes in
by the side gate on the west.*  
又往西行，不多远，照样也是三间大门，方是荣国府了。却不进正门，只进了西边角门。

*A bowshot inside, at a turning, the bearers set the chair down and leave. Four pages of seventeen or eighteen, neatly
dressed, take it up. The serving women walk alongside. At a festooned gate they set it down, the pages withdraw, and
the women lift the curtain and help her out.*  
那轿夫抬进去，走了一射之地，将转弯时，便歇下退出去了。另换了三四个衣帽周全十七八岁的小厮上来，复抬起轿子。众婆子步下围随至一垂花门前落下。众小厮退出，众婆子上来打起轿帘，扶黛玉下轿。

*(Staging: the player walks Daiyu on the old woman's arm through the festooned gate, past the great marble screen, to
the courtyard where parrots and thrushes hang in cages along the covered walks. On the steps, girls in red and green
jump up laughing.)*

**Maid:** The old lady was just asking for you, and here you are!  
刚才老太太还念呢，可巧就来了。

*Three or four of them race to lift the door curtain. Someone inside calls: "Miss Lin is here."*  
于是三四人争着打起帘笼，一面听得人回话：「林姑娘到了。」

## D2 · Grandmother (外祖母)
*Grandmother Jia's rooms* · ◆ whom to bow to first

*Two women come forward supporting an old lady with silver hair.*  
只见两个人搀着一位鬓发如银的老母迎上来。

**◆ MOMENT · Whom to bow to first.** *The room is full of women. Daiyu means to kneel and bow.*
- **Watch:** the silver-haired lady is held up on both sides, and everyone else stands back from her. / the two women
  supporting her stand a half-step behind.
- **Choice:** bow first to the silver-haired lady · to the women at her side · wait.
- **The novel:** she knows her grandmother at once and moves to bow (「黛玉便知是他外祖母。方欲拜见时」). Before she can,
  her grandmother pulls her into her arms.
- **Slip:** a maid by the door hides a smile.

[still: `hl_embrace`]

*Her grandmother pulls her into her arms, "my heart, my flesh", and weeps aloud. Everyone standing in the room covers
their face and weeps. Daiyu weeps and cannot stop.*  
早被他外祖母一把搂入怀中，心肝儿肉叫着大哭起来。当下地下侍立之人，无不掩面涕泣，黛玉也哭个不住。

*When they have calmed down, she bows to her grandmother at last.*  
一时众人慢慢解劝住了，黛玉方拜见了外祖母。

**Grandmother Jia:** This is your elder uncle's wife. This is your second uncle's wife. This is your cousin Zhu's widow.  
这是你大舅母，这是你二舅母，这是你先珠大哥的媳婦珠大嫂子。

*Daiyu bows to each.*  
黛玉一一拜见过。

**Grandmother Jia:** Call the girls. A guest has come from far away: they needn't go to their lessons today.  
请姑娘们来。今日远客才来，可以不必上学去了。

*Three girls come, with nurses and maids. The first is a little plump, gentle and quiet. The second is slim, with
quick eyes and fine brows, and a look that makes you forget yourself. The third is still small. All three are dressed
exactly alike.*  
第一个肌肤微丰，合中身材，腮凝新荔，鼻腻鹅脂，温柔沉默，观之可亲。第二个削肩细腰，长挑身材，鸭蛋脸面，俊眼修眉，顾盼神飞，文彩精华，见之忘俗。第三个身量未足，形容尚小。其钗环裙袄，三人皆是一样的妆饰。

*They talk of how her mother fell ill, the doctors, the funeral. Her grandmother breaks down again.*  
不过说些黛玉之母如何得病，如何请医服药，如何送死发丧。不免贾母又伤感起来。

**Grandmother Jia:** Of all my children, she was the one I loved. And she's gone before me, and I never saw her face
again. Now I see you, how can I not grieve?  
我这些儿女，所疼者独有你母，今日一旦先舍我而去，连面也不能一见，今见了你，我怎不伤心！

*They see that she is frail, and ask what medicine she takes.*  
众人见黛玉……身体面庞虽怯弱不胜，却有一段自然的风流态度，便知他有不足之症。因问：「常服何药，如何不急为疗治？」

**Daiyu:** I've always been like this. I've taken medicine since I could eat, and no doctor has helped. When I was three,
a scabby-headed monk came and wanted to take me away to be a nun. My parents refused, of course. Then he said: if you
won't part with her, she'll never be well, unless from now on she never hears anyone weep, and never sees any family
but her parents. It was mad talk, and nobody paid him any mind. I still take ginseng pills.  
我自来是如此，从会吃饮食时便吃药，到今日未断，请了多少名医修方配药，皆不见效。那一年我三岁时，听得说来了一个癞头和尚，说要化我去出家，我父母固是不从。他又说：「既舍不得他，只怕他的病一生也不能好的了。若要好时，除非从此以后总不许见哭声，除父母之外，凡有外姓亲友之人，一概不见，方可平安了此一世。」疯疯癫癫，说了这些不经之谈，也没人理他。如今还是吃人参养荣丸。

**Grandmother Jia:** Good, I'm having pills made up now. They'll make a batch more.  
正好，我这里正配丸药呢。叫他们多配一料就是了。

## D3 · "I'm Late" (我来迟了)
*Grandmother Jia's rooms* · ◆ what to call her

*Before she has finished, laughter from the back courtyard.*  
一语未了，只听后院中有人笑声，说：

**Voice:** I'm late! I wasn't here to welcome our guest!  
我来迟了，不曾迎接远客！

*(Daiyu, thinking)* Everyone here holds their breath, so solemn and correct. Who is this, so loud and careless?  
这些人个个皆敛声屏气，恭肃严整如此，这来者系谁，这样放诞无礼？

[still: `hl_xifeng`]

*A crowd of women and maids sweeps in from the back door around one young woman, dressed like no one else: gold and
pearls, phoenix pins, red brocade with a hundred butterflies. Phoenix eyes, willow-leaf brows. Her powdered face holds
spring, and hides its power. Her red lips laugh before they part.*  
只见一群媳妇丫鬟围拥着一个人从后房门进来。这个人打扮与众姑娘不同，彩绣辉煌，恍若神妃仙子……一双丹凤三角眼，两弯柳叶吊梢眉，身量苗条，体格风骚，粉面含春威不露，丹唇未起笑先开。

*Daiyu rises at once.*  
黛玉连忙起身接见。

**Grandmother Jia:** You don't know her. She's the famous hellion of this house. In the south they'd call her a hot
pepper. Just call her Pepper Feng.  
你不认得他，他是我们这里有名的一个泼皮破落户儿，南省俗谓作「辣子」，你只叫他「凤辣子」就是了。

**◆ MOMENT · What to call her.** *Daiyu doesn't know what to call her (「黛玉正不知以何称呼」). "Pepper Feng" is the
grandmother's joke.*
- **Watch:** the three sisters lean in and whisper: 「这是琏嫂子。」 ("This is Cousin Lian's wife.") / she remembers her
  mother saying: her elder uncle's son, Jia Lian, married her second aunt's niece, Wang Xifeng, raised as a boy.
- **Choice:** "Pepper Feng" · "Madam" · "Cousin" (嫂子, elder brother's wife).
- **The novel:** she smiles and greets her as cousin (「黛玉忙陪笑见礼，以『嫂』呼之」).
- **Slip:** calling her "Pepper Feng": the maids laugh outright, and Xifeng laughs loudest. Calling her "Madam": a
  sister covers her mouth.

*Xifeng takes her hands and looks her up and down, then sets her back by her grandmother.*  
这熙凤携着黛玉的手，上下细细打量了一回，仍送至贾母身边坐下。

**Xifeng:** Is there really anyone this lovely under heaven? Today I've seen one at last! And the air about her! She's
not like the old ancestress's granddaughter by a daughter at all, she's like her own son's girl. No wonder you think of
her every hour. Only, my poor sister, what a hard fate. Why did my aunt have to die!  
天下真有这样标致的人物，我今儿才算见了！况且这通身的气派，竟不象老祖宗的外孙女儿，竟是个嫡亲的孙女，怨不得老祖宗天天口头心头一时不忘。只可怜我这妹妹这样命苦，怎么姑妈偏就去世了！

*She dabs her eyes with her handkerchief.*  
说着，便用帕拭泪。

**Grandmother Jia:** I've only just stopped crying, and you start me off again. Your sister's come a long way and she's
not strong. We've just got her calm. Don't bring it up.  
我才好了，你倒来招我。你妹妹远路才来，身子又弱，也才劝住了，快再休提前话。

*(The player sees it, if watching Xifeng: at the grandmother's word, her face turns from grief to joy in one breath.)*  
这熙凤听了，忙转悲为喜道：

**Xifeng:** So I did! The moment I saw her, my heart went out to her. I was so glad and so sad at once, I forgot the old
ancestress. I deserve a slap! I deserve a slap!  
正是呢！我一见了妹妹，一心都在他身上了，又是喜欢，又是伤心，竟忘记了老祖宗。该打，该打！

**Xifeng:** How old are you, sister? Have you started your lessons? What medicine are you taking? You mustn't be
homesick here. Anything you want to eat or play with, just tell me. If a maid or a nurse isn't good to you, tell me.  
妹妹几岁了？可也上过学？现吃什么药？在这里不要想家，想要什么吃的，什么玩的，只管告诉我，丫头老婆们不好了，也只管告诉我。

*Tea and fruit come. Xifeng serves them with her own hands. Lady Wang asks her a question.*  
说话时，已摆了茶果上来。熙凤亲为捧茶捧果。又见二舅母问他：

**Lady Wang:** Have the monthly allowances been paid?  
月钱放过了不曾？

**Xifeng:** All paid. And I took people up to the back storehouse to look for the satin, and searched half the day, and
never found the kind you described yesterday. Perhaps you misremembered, madam?  
月钱已放完了。才刚带着人到后楼上找缎子，找了这半日，也并没有见昨日太太说的那样的，想是太太记错了？

**Lady Wang:** It doesn't matter. But you ought to take out a couple of lengths for your sister's clothes. Remember to
send someone tonight. Don't forget.  
有没有，什么要紧。……该随手拿出两个来给你这妹妹去裁衣裳的，等晚上想着叫人再去拿罷，可别忘了。

**Xifeng:** I'd thought of that already. I knew she'd come any day, so I've had them ready. I'll send them over once
you've looked at them, madam.  
这倒是我先料着了，知道妹妹不过这两日到的，我已预备下了，等太太回去过了目好送来。

*Lady Wang smiles and nods, and says nothing.*  
王夫人一笑，点头不语。

## D4 · The Elder Uncle's House (大舅)
*By carriage to Lady Xing's compound* · ◆ stay to dinner?

*Her grandmother sends her to pay her respects to both uncles. Lady Xing, the elder uncle's wife, offers to take her.*  
贾母命两个老嬷嬷带了黛玉去见两个母舅。时贾赦之妻邢氏忙亦起身，笑回道：「我带了外甥女过去，倒也便宜。」

*A green-curtained carriage, pulled by pages to the open ground, then by a mule, out of the west side gate, past the
Rong mansion's main gate, in at a black-lacquered gate. The courtyards here are smaller, prettier, cut off from a
garden.*  
众小厮拉过一辆翠幄青绸车，邢夫人携了黛玉，坐在上面……出了西角门，往东过荣府正门，便入一黑油大门中……黛玉度其房屋院宇，必是荣府中花园隔断过来的。

*Lady Xing sends to the study for her husband. The servant comes back alone.*  
一面命人到外面书房去请贾赦。一时人来回话说：

**Servant (for Jia She):** The master says: "I haven't been well these days. Seeing the young lady would only grieve us
both, and I can't bear it yet. Tell her not to be sad or homesick. With the old lady and her aunts it will be the same
as home. Her cousins are plain girls, but they'll keep her company. If anything's wrong, she must say so, and not treat
us as strangers."  
老爷说了：「连日身上不好，见了姑娘彼此倒伤心，暂且不忍相见。劝姑娘不要伤心想家，跟着老太太和舅母，即同家里一样。姊妹们虽拙，大家一处伴着，亦可以解些烦闷。或有委屈之处，只管说得，不要外道才是。」

*Daiyu stands to hear it all.*  
黛玉忙站起来，一一听了。

**◆ MOMENT · Stay to dinner?** *She sits a little longer and rises to go. Lady Xing presses her to stay for dinner
(「苦留吃过晚饭去」).*
- **Watch:** the old nurses her grandmother sent stand ready at the door. / Through the window, the light is going. /
  Her grandmother's order was to see **both** uncles.
- **Choice:** accept · refuse · thank her, and give the reason.
- **The novel:** a refusal wrapped in thanks, with the second uncle as the reason:
  **Daiyu:** You're kind to offer me dinner, aunt, and I oughtn't to refuse. But I still have to call on my second
  uncle, and it would be disrespectful to keep him waiting. Another day, if you'll allow me.  
  舅母爱惜赐饭，原不应辞，只是还要过去拜见二舅舅，恐领了赐去不恭，异日再领，未为不可。望舅母容谅。
- **Slip:** a plain "no": the concubines along the wall exchange a look. Accepting: an old nurse coughs at the door.

**Lady Xing:** That's right.  
这倒是了。

*She sees her to the inner gate, and watches the carriage go.*  
邢夫人送至仪门前，又嘱咐了众人几句，眼看着车去了方回来。

## D5 · The Kang (度其位次)
*Rongxi Hall · Lady Wang's rooms* · ◆ where to sit (twice) · ◆ answer the warning

*Back in the Rong mansion. A great courtyard, a straight paved way to the main gate. This is the true main house. Over
the hall, a gold plaque on blue: Hall of Glorious Felicity, written by the Emperor for her great-grandfather.*  
黛玉便知这方是正经正内室，一条大甬路，直接出大门的。进入堂屋中，抬头迎面先看见一个赤金九龙青地大匾，匾上写着斗大的三个大字，是「荣禧堂」。

*Lady Wang doesn't sit in the great hall. She lives in three side rooms to its east. The old nurses lead Daiyu in. On
the kang under the window: red rugs, a back-rest, bolsters. Along the west wall, four chairs in a row.*  
原来王夫人时常居坐宴息，亦不在这正室，只在这正室东边的三间耳房内。于是老嬷嬷引黛玉进东房门来。

**Nurse:** Sit on the kang, miss.  
（老嬷嬷们让黛玉炕上坐。）

**◆ MOMENT · Where to sit (1).** *The nurses invite her up onto the kang.*
- **Watch:** on the edge of the kang, two brocade cushions are laid **facing each other** (「炕沿上却有两个锦褥对设」):
  two places, for two people who are not here. / the four chairs below have footstools set before them.
- **Choice:** the kang · a chair.
- **The novel:** she reads the order of the seats, and doesn't go up. She sits on a chair on the east side
  (「黛玉度其位次，便不上炕，只向东边椅子上坐了」).
- **Slip:** the kang: a maid bringing the tea stops, and smiles.

[still: `hl_kang`]

*The maids bring tea. As she drinks, Daiyu studies them: their dress, how they move. Not like other houses.*  
黛玉一面吃茶，一面打谅这些丫鬟们，妆饰衣裙，举止行动，果亦与别家不同。

**Maid:** The mistress asks Miss Lin to come and sit with her there.  
太太说，请林姑娘到那边坐罷。

*Three small rooms on the east corridor. On the kang a low table with books and a tea set. A worn blue back-rest
against the east wall, facing west. Lady Wang sits on the west side, lower, on a worn blue cushion too.*  
正房炕上横设一张炕桌，桌上磊着书籍茶具，靠东壁面西设着半旧的青缎靠背引枕。王夫人却坐在西边下首，亦是半旧的青缎靠背坐褥。

**◆ MOMENT · Where to sit (2).** *Lady Wang sees her and moves over, making room on the east.*
- **Watch:** (wait) Lady Wang shifts east to make room (「便往东让」). / The east place has the back-rest; Lady Wang
  herself sits on the lower, west side. / Below the kang, three chairs in a row, covered in worn black-patterned cloth.
- **Choice:** the east place on the kang · a chair below.
- **The novel:** she is sure the east place is her uncle Jia Zheng's (「黛玉心中料定这是贾政之位」), and sits on a chair.
  Lady Wang has to draw her up four times before she sits on the kang **beside her** (「王夫人再四携他上炕，他方挨王夫人坐了」).
- **Slip:** sitting in Jia Zheng's place: Lady Wang says nothing, and a maid looks at the floor.

**Lady Wang:** Your uncle is fasting today. You'll see him another time. Only there's one thing I want to tell you. Your
three cousins are good girls. You'll study together, and learn your characters and needlework, and if you play, they'll
always give way to you. But there's one thing I can't rest easy about. I have a root of all evil, a curse on the

这话没的叫人恶心。不过借赖着祖父虚名，作了穷官儿，谁家有什么，不过是个旧日的空架子。俗语说，「朝廷还有三门子穷亲戚」呢，何况你我。

*(The payoff of the opening scroll: 「外面的架子虽未甚倒，内囊却也尽上来了」.)*

*She sends Zhou Rui's wife to see whether Lady Wang is free. Stewards' wives come to report; Ping'er sends them away

说着，又问周瑞家的回了太太了没有……就有家下许多媳妇管事的来回话。平儿回了……我就叫他们散了。

太太说了，今日不得闲，二奶奶陪着便是一样。多谢费心想着。白来逛逛呢便罢，若有甚说的，只管告诉二奶奶，都是一样。

**Granny Liu:** There's nothing in particular. We only came to see the lady and the mistress, as relations do.  
也没甚说的，不过是来瞧瞧姑太太，姑奶奶，也是亲戚们的情分。

没甚说的便罢，若有话，只管回二奶奶，是和太太一样的。

**Xifeng:** Never mind for now. *(Then:)* Where's your Master Rong?

**Jia Rong:** My father sent me to beg you, aunt. That glass screen for the kang that your uncle's lady gave you last

我父亲打发我来求婶子，说上回老舅太太给婶子的那架玻璃炕屏，明日请一个要紧的客，借了略摆一摆就送过来。

**Xifeng:** You're a day too late. I gave it to someone yesterday.

贾蓉听着，嘻嘻的笑着，在炕沿上半跪道：

也没见你们，王家的东西都是好的不成？你们那里放着那些好东西，只是看不见，偏我的就是好的。

**Jia Rong:** Where would we find one as fine as that? Only be merciful!  
那里有这个好呢！只求开恩罢。

**Xifeng:** If it gets one knock, mind your skin!

因命平儿拿了楼房的钥匙，传几个妥当人抬去。贾蓉喜的眉开眼笑……说着便起身出去了。这里凤姐忽又想起一事来，便向窗外叫：

**Xifeng:** Rong-ge, come back.

外面几个人接声说：「蓉大爷快回来。」贾蓉忙复身转来，垂手侍立，听何指示。那凤姐只管慢慢的吃茶，出了半日的神，又笑道：

**Xifeng:** Never mind. Go on. Come after dinner and we'll talk. There are people here now, and I'm tired.

贾蓉应了一声，方慢慢的退去。

*Granny Liu's heart settles at last.*  
这里刘姥姥心神方定，才又说道：

今日我带了你侄儿来，也不为别的，只因他老子娘在家里，连吃的都没有。如今天又冷了，越想没个派头儿，只得带了你侄儿奔了你老来。

你那爹在家怎么教你来？打发咱们作煞事来？只顾吃果子咧。

凤姐早已明白了，听他不会说话，因笑止道：

**Xifeng:** Say no more, I understand. *(To Zhou Rui's wife:)* Has the granny had her breakfast?  
不必说了，我知道了。……这姥姥不知可用了早饭没有？

**Granny Liu:** We set out first thing to get here. When would we have had time to eat?  
一早就往这里赶咧，那里还有吃饭的工夫咧。

*Xifeng has a meal brought for them in the east room, and while they eat she asks Zhou Rui's wife what Lady Wang said.*  
凤姐听说，忙命快传饭来……又叫过周瑞家的去，问他才回了太太，说了些什么？

太太说，他们家原不是一家子，不过因出一姓，当年又与太老爷在一处作官，偶然连了宗的。这几年来也不大走动。当时他们来一遭，却也没空了他们。今儿既来了瞧瞧我们，是他的好意思，也不可简慢了他。便是有什么说的，叫奶奶裁度着就是了。

**Xifeng:** I thought as much. If they were family, how could I not have heard of them?  
我说呢，既是一家子，我如何连影儿也不知道。

*Granny Liu finishes eating, and comes back with Ban'er, licking her lips and thanking her.*  
说话时，刘姥姥已吃毕了饭，拉了板儿过来，舔舌咂嘴的道谢。

且请坐下，听我告诉你老人家。方才的意思，我已知道了。若论亲戚之间，原该不等上门来就该有照应才是。但如今家内杂事太烦，太太渐上了年纪，一时想不到也是有的。况是我近来接着管些事，都不知道这些亲戚们。二则外头看着虽是烈烈轰轰的，殊不知大有大的艰难去处，说与人也未必信罢。今儿你既老远的来了，又是头一次见我张口，怎好叫你空回去呢。可巧昨儿太太给我的丫头们做衣裳的二十两银子，我还没动呢，你若不嫌少，就暂且先拿了去罢。

那刘姥姥先听见告艰难，只当是没有，心里便突突的，后来听见给他二十两，喜的又浑身发痒起来，说道：

**Granny Liu:** Ah, I know about troubles. But as the saying goes, "a starved camel is still bigger than a horse". Say

嗳，我也是知道艰难的。但俗语说的：「瘦死的骆驼比马大」，凭他怎样，你老拔根寒毛比我们的腰还粗呢！

周瑞家的见他说的粗鄙，只管使眼色止他。凤姐看见，笑而不睬。

## G7 · The Back Gate (后门)

我的娘啊！你见了他怎么倒不会说了？开口就是「你侄儿」。我说句不怕你恼的话，便是亲侄儿，也要说和软些。蓉大爷才是他的正经侄儿呢，他怎么又跑出这么一个侄儿来了。
